# UI: the shown bubble resizes from any edge (U2236, U2237)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U2236, U2237. Work Issue: #2211. Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the owner: "The terminal bubbles should be resizable", then "we only need a resizer - terminals themselves could manage splitting and tiling not us - we just have a single bubble open at a time in the center". Narrows U164 (a bubble resizes "from its bottom-right corner") and keeps the rest of U164 and U421: one bubble shows at a time, picked from the bottom tab bar (U469); roundup adds no splitting, tiling or bubble manager, and a user who wants splits runs tmux inside the Terminal. Observer: Vitest against the fake App and the isolated Chromium harness (U26) at 1280 by 800, dark. Never a native Mac capture.

Engineering calls, each made here so the implementation run asks nothing:

1. **Centred.** A resize keeps the bubble's centre where it is: dragging an edge by *d* moves it by *d* and the opposite edge by *-d* (a corner does both axes), so the size on an axis is twice the distance from the centre to the pointer. A size the pane cannot hold on that side is the largest `clamped` (`bubbles/places.ts`) allows, which shifts the place inside the pane and never grows past it. A bubble the user moved (U164, U421) resizes about its moved centre.
2. **Hit areas.** Eight transparent strips on the shown bubble: four edges `--space-2` (8 px) wide, centred on the edge line so none reaches the 12 px padding where the header controls sit, and four corners `--space-4` (16 px) square over the corner. Each takes pointer events only; none is drawn, shows a border, grip or hover change, or takes layout. They are `aria-hidden` and outside the Tab order: the keyboard route stays U164's `⇧` arrows on the focused header.
3. **Cursors.** The native resize cursors by direction: `ew-resize` on the left and right edges, `ns-resize` on the top and bottom, `nesw-resize` on the top-right and bottom-left corners, `nwse-resize` on the top-left and bottom-right.
4. **Bounds.** The minimum is U164's (`MIN_WIDTH` by `MIN_HEIGHT`: 24 columns by 6 rows, chrome included); the maximum is the centre pane. Nothing new.
5. **Per Project.** The size is the bubble's `Place` (`w`, `h`), stored with its place exactly as U164 stores it: per Project path and Terminal id, in the window's session storage, surviving a reload (U103) and forgotten at `daemon-exited` (engineering call 2 of `scenarios/quick-terminal.md`). The last size before a fill (U2237) joins the stored `Place` as an optional `restore` rectangle, kept and forgotten the same way. A stored `Place` without it is valid.

**U2236 any edge or corner resizes the bubble.** Narrows U164. Given a shown bubble in an open Project, then:

- **Drag.** `pointerdown` on an edge or corner captures the pointer; while it is down the bubble's size follows the pointer by call 1, at most one style write per animation frame (U164's rule for a drag); `pointerup` ends it; `Escape` while it is down restores the place it began with and ends it. From each of the eight zones a drag of +40 px outward grows the bubble by 80 px on that axis (both axes for a corner), centre unchanged within 1 px. Test: `u2236_each_edge_and_corner_resizes_about_the_centre`, `u2236_escape_restores_the_place_it_began_with`, `u2236_a_drag_writes_style_once_per_frame`.
- **Bounds.** Dragging inward past the minimum stops at `MIN_WIDTH` by `MIN_HEIGHT`; dragging outward past the pane stops at the pane and the bubble stays inside it. A bubble near the pane's edge that grows keeps inside it. Test: `u2236_a_drag_stops_at_the_minimum_and_the_pane`.
- **Invisible.** The eight zones are `aria-hidden="true"` and `tabindex` absent. D2 and D8 hold (Tokens only: the widths are `--space-2` and `--space-4`). Test: `u2236_the_zones_are_hidden_from_the_keyboard_and_assistive_tech`.
- **Fit.** The emulator refits as U13 says: at most one `terminal.resize` per animation frame while the drag is down, and none when the fitted `cols` and `rows` did not change; the drag's end leaves the Terminal fitted to its final body. The pane under the bubble does not resize (U164). Test: `u2236_a_resize_calls_terminal_resize_once_per_size_change`.
- **Persist.** After a drag the new size is the bubble's stored `Place`; closing and reopening the webview on the same path shows it at that size (U103, U164); another Project keeps its own; `daemon-exited` clears it. A minimised bubble (U469) shows no zones; restoring it returns the size (U164). Test: `u2236_the_size_survives_a_reload_per_project`, `u2236_daemon_exited_forgets_the_size`.
- **Unmoved.** A resize sends no `terminal.write`, moves no selection or focus, leaves the keyboard route (`⇧` arrows on the header) and the right-button Pull (U421) as they are, and does not start a header drag (U164): a press on a zone is never a move. Test: `u2236_a_resize_is_not_a_move_a_write_or_a_selection_change`.

Tests (`u2236_`, Vitest in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`, and Chromium on `just harness` for the cursor and the invisible clauses). Moves: D2, D8. Owns `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`.

**U2237 double-clicking the header fills the pane and restores.** Narrows U164. Given a shown bubble in an open Project, then:

- **Fill.** A double-click on the header's empty space (not on `Minimise`, `Close` or the name that U511 edits in place) sets the bubble's place to the whole centre pane (x 0, y 0, the pane's width and height) and stores the previous rectangle as `restore`. Test: `u2237_a_double_click_on_the_header_fills_the_pane`.
- **Restore.** A second double-click returns the bubble to `restore` (clamped to the pane as it is now) and clears `restore`, as a macOS title bar does. A drag or a resize (U2236, or U164's `⇧` arrows) of a filled bubble keeps `restore`, so the next double-click restores while the bubble is the pane's size and fills otherwise. Test: `u2237_a_second_double_click_restores_the_last_size`, `u2237_a_resize_of_a_filled_bubble_keeps_the_restore_size`.
- **Fit and persist.** Each change refits the emulator once (U13) and sends no other call; a filled bubble and its `restore` survive a reload (call 5) and are forgotten at `daemon-exited`. Test: `u2237_fill_and_restore_refit_once` (Chromium, `u2236_browser.test.ts`), `u2237_a_filled_bubble_survives_a_reload`.
- **Unmoved.** A single click, a header drag (U164) and a Pull (U421) still behave as they do; a double-click on the name still starts the rename (U511). Test: `u2237_a_single_click_a_drag_and_the_rename_are_unchanged`.

Tests (`u2237_`, Vitest in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`). Moves: D2. Owns `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`.
