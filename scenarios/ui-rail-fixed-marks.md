# UI: every Rail mark keeps one fixed place (U7590a to U7590c)

Module: `apps/desktop/src/rail/` (the webview). Ids: U7590a, U7590b, U7590c. Work Issue: #7590. Journey stage: Understand (`.agents/data/journey.md`). Source: the user's report #7590, with a screenshot of the Delivery row whose fold chevron touches its name. Amends U7488a's "Names first" clause (the ring and count give way on hover, focus and selection), U7488c's ring and count placement, and U4707's chevron swap. Every other clause of those holds. Webview only: no RPC method, App command, contract, code term or Token. Terms: Rail, Todos, Glyph (`GLOSSARY.md`).

**U7590a The Todos ring and its count sit at one x on every row.** Given Rail rows of every kind at depths 0 to 3 whose Todos counts differ in width, for example `2/3` and `34/34`, at the narrowest and widest Rail and the largest text scale, then:
- **Fixed slot.** The Todos slot has one fixed inline size wide enough for the widest count the Rail shows, and the ring's left edge and the count's left edge are the same x on every row, within 1 CSS px, whatever the count's width. A wider count never moves the ring. Asserted in `u7590a_the_ring_x_is_equal_across_counts` (real headless Chromium on the `_browser.test` lane: rows with `2/3` and `34/34` give equal ring left edges).
- **Hover, focus and selection.** Hover, keyboard focus and selection move neither the ring nor the count. Asserted in `u7590a_the_ring_does_not_move_on_hover_focus_or_selection` (the same lane: the ring's and the count's bounding boxes at rest equal those in hover, focus and selection, within 1 CSS px).

**U7590b The revealed actions displace nothing.** Amends U7488a's "Names first". Given any Rail row that shows actions, then hover, focus or selection shows the actions in their fixed slots without moving the ring, the count, the Glyph or the name: the name's start and its truncation point hold, and the actions take room the row already holds at rest. Asserted in `u7590b_revealed_actions_move_no_mark` (real headless Chromium: the Glyph, name, ring and count bounding boxes at rest equal those in hover, focus and selection, within 1 CSS px, on `agents-10` and `tree-40`). Where the name and the actions cannot both fit, the name truncates with `…` before any mark moves (U7205c).

**U7590c The fold chevron keeps a gap to the name.** Amends U4707's swap. Given a Workstream row on hover or keyboard focus, then the chevron takes the Glyph's place and keeps at least `--space-1` between its right edge and the name's left edge, at depths 0 to 3; the name does not move. Asserted in `u7590c_the_chevron_keeps_a_gap_to_the_name` (real headless Chromium: the gap between the chevron's and the name's bounding boxes is at least `--space-1`, and the name's left edge equals its rest value).
