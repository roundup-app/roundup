# UI: a Todo row the user can read, tick and drag (U361 to U363)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U361 to U363.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes; `todo.complete` and `todo.move` are called as U35 and U157 say. Found by the user's report #812 on the Shelf: "Todos and it's button is pur trash unusable", "Spacing and hover bg with no padding is pure shit". Seen: on hover `Move` and `Complete` (U35, U157) draw over the title and hide its first words; `Move` does not say what it does; `#<id>` and `In <home>` are noise in every row; on a done Todo the hover wash touches the text with no inset. The shared row geometry is #673's (U359, U360) and the Shelf tabs are #687's; this scenario changes what a Todo row holds and does, not those rules. Narrows U15, U35, U112, U151, U157 and U359, and changes #398's Rule (`scenarios/ui-shelf.md`) as U361 says.

**U361 a Todo row aligns its name and circle and keeps Open reachable.** _Amended by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`) and the #4535 amendment below; U3789a–d govern the earlier replacement clauses._ Given the Shelf's `todos` list (U15, U60) on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run` (U26), light and dark for non-capture behavioral observers, at 1280 by 820 and 1280 by 800, with U359's added fixture (300-character title, ten open blockers) and a Todo with a 40-character title blocked by a Todo with a 40-character title, when a test reads the rendered tree, computed style and boxes with the pointer outside the list, then:

- **Tick box.** An open Todo's row starts with a tick box in the Glyph's place (U359's 16 px column): a native `input type="checkbox"` unchecked, accessible name `complete <title>`, with U86's 24 px hit area, visible focus and Space to toggle, in the tab order after the row button (deliberate: Tab reaches the row, then its tick box, while the tick box is drawn first). Checking it calls `todo.complete {id}` once and nothing else (U35: no Touch, the Drawer stays shut); the row changes only when `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15). A failed call unchecks it and shows `✕ <message>` in place of the `Waits on` line (U35). A blocked Todo's `⏸` (U15) stays visible: its tick box sits beside it at the same left edge as an unblocked Todo's, so U359's one Glyph edge holds. A done Todo's row (U15's `✓ n done` unfolded) shows a checked tick box that can be unticked to reopen it under U461a. Test: `u361_a_tick_box_completes_a_todo`.
- **No button over the title.** A row at rest, on hover and on focus holds no `Complete` word and no `Move` word, and no element is drawn over the title: no element of the row other than its own text box overlaps the title's box, measured at rest, on hover and on focus. U35's out-of-flow `Complete` and U157's out-of-flow row `Move` are removed; U112 holds, so hovering or focusing moves no row's top edge, height or wrapping. Test: `u361_nothing_draws_over_a_title_on_hover_or_focus`.
- **No id, no Home line.** A Todo row's text is its title, then for a blocked Todo only the `Waits on` line. The row shows no `#<id>` and no `data-todo-home` element, so U151's items 1 and 3 and U359's meta-line entries `#<id>` and `In <home>` no longer hold for a Todo (Pads have no person metadata, U3736). The Home is read in the Drawer (U17, U157) and from the Rail (U362). Test: `u361_a_row_shows_no_id_and_no_home`.
- **Blockers by title.** The `Waits on` line names each open blocker by its title in id order, `Waits on <title>, <title>`, in its own element `data-todo-waits`; a title shows at most one line and then `…` and the whole line is the element's `title` attribute (U359's Long text). Each blocker title is a click target that opens that blocker's Drawer (U17) without opening the blocked Todo's own, as U35 says of `#<id>`, with the accessible name `open blocker <title>`. #398's Rule (`scenarios/ui-shelf.md:5`) changes: a Todo row shows at rest its status, title and each open blocker, not `#<id>` or its Home; two Todos with the same title are told apart by their order and the Drawer, which still shows the `#<id>` (U17). The Drawer's `Waits on` list, `Remove blocker #<id>` (U152) and `rup` output keep ids. Test: `u361_waits_on_names_blockers_by_title`.
- **Kept.** Every action of U152 keeps a keyboard path (U31): `Complete` is the tick box (Tab, Space); `Move` is U362's keyboard path; reorder, `⌥↑` and `⌥↓` (U155) and Enter to open the Drawer are unchanged. `window.__checks()` passes D1 to D14 on every seed above in dark (U136) with no Check passing on `main` regressing. Tests rewritten in the implementing PR, named in its body: `u15_`, `u35_`, `u151_`, `u152_`, `u154_`, `u157_`'s row cases and `u359_`, to read the tick box and the title, with no change to what they assert about calls.

## Todo row amendment (#4535)

Implementation work order: #4555, blocked by this specification.

Policy — this amendment takes precedence over U361's historical tick/blocker presentation only where U3789d and U4275a/c already replaced it. It adds alignment, quiet interaction and a permanent Open arrow; it does not restore blocker/body previews. U3789a still separates selection from opening. No RPC, App command, data model or Token contract changes.

- Policy — alignment: at 1512×950, the visible circle and the name's first-line optical centre differ vertically by at most 1 CSS px. Compare root, child and grandchild rows, open/blocked/done states, one-letter, emoji, non-Latin and 300-character names. Use U4275c's one fixed row height and U4335a's shared indent. The design critic measures the rendered circle and text, rather than comparing only their containing boxes.
- Policy — quiet state: focus, selection and pointer press use one existing Token text-colour cue, shared when states combine. They add no background wash, outline, border, shadow, font-weight or geometry change. Keyboard focus remains distinguishable and D5 contrast holds. U3800a's active drag source decoration remains scoped to an actual drag. No focus/selection animation is added.
- Policy — trailing room: the name uses remaining row width; only the existing Token row padding, gaps and the required Open/grip/completion/disclosure hit areas may reserve trailing space. Keep the latter controls’ width reserved while hidden so reveal moves nothing; remove every other unused action column or minimum width. The Open arrow precedes the rightmost grip of U3800a. Neither overlaps the title, and reveal of completion/disclosure changes no name width or row geometry.
- Every Todo in the Shelf has a separately named Open button drawing its open arrow at rest, on hover, focus and selection, including a done row. A primary click on that arrow opens that exact Todo once, with the existing read Touch and destination focus; Return on the focused row does the same. Neither path requires a context menu or a preceding selection click. Selection, completion, disclosure and a recognized grip drag never open it. Extend main’s `u3789a_return_activates_once_%s` and `u490a_cancel_and_noop_escape_cancels`, and implement the commissioned `u3789a_return_and_open_activate_once` arrow cases at the existing connected App/browser seams to cover these paths and call counts; arrow visibility is policy for the rendered observer.

Policy — control budget: for Todo rows only, Open remains visible at rest; a draggable row's grip shows as U5147e (`ui-drag.md`) says. Completion and disclosure keep their existing applicability and focus/selection reveal rules; when both apply they remain independently reachable. Permit those four controls together, with no extra quick-action group. The grip also adds one permitted control to other draggable row kinds in U3800a's inventory; unrelated actions keep U3789d/U4275b's limits. This expressly supersedes U3789d/e, U4275b's Todo/Pad budget statement and `.agents/data/journey.md` F3 only for these required controls. State is still one non-colour glyph; a grip is an action, never a second state glyph.

Policy — rendered delivery: implementation compares main/head on the same U26 `tree-40` and `agents-10` fixtures at 1512×950, dark only, using a dedicated headless agent-browser session and fresh profile. Include the name/state/depth cases above, a Todo with body/blockers/comments, a folded dependency group and a refused completion or move with readable cause/recovery on the densest seed. Capture rest, hover, focus, selected, combined states and every U3800a inventory list during drag. Run `just capture U361 U3710 U3789a U3800a U3800c U4275c`; leave before/after PNG/video and Check readings under `artifacts/ux/<id>/`, never commit media. The critic owns alignment, paint, space, arrow/grip visibility and landing geometry; behavioral tests assert actions, roles, focus and contracts only. This specification needs no rendered or native observation and claims no implementation.

**Tick box** (`TickBox`): the shared native Todo completion control on its row and before its Drawer title; a done Todo is checked; unticking it reopens it, and ticking an open Todo completes it. Both keep the accepted value until the Daemon accepts the change.

**U362 the user moves a Todo by dragging its row onto the Rail.** Narrows U157 and U155; replaces U157's row `Move` word and its popover; the Drawer's `Move` (U157) stays. Given the Shelf's open Todos and a Rail with Workstreams (T12, T13), when the user drags a Todo row (U155's drag, started the same way) out of the Shelf and over the Rail, then:

- **Targets.** A Workstream row and the pinned Overview band for the Project root (U2716a) are drop targets; the Todo's current Home is not. While a Todo is dragged over a target the target shows U22's drop highlight (`--hover` wash, no drop line, no row makes room) and the Todo's row stays in the Shelf with its place held (U155); over any other part of the Rail, or outside both lists, nothing is highlighted. Test: `u362_only_other_homes_are_targets`.
- **Drop.** Releasing over a target calls `todo.move {id, home}` once, `home` the Workstream's Rail id or `null` for the Project root (T12), and nothing else (U157: no `todo.get` Touch). The row leaves or stays in the list only when `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15, U158); it keeps its place in the Todo order (T10). A drop over a non-target, a release outside the Rail and the Shelf, and `Esc` call nothing and settle the row back (motion.md, "Drag to reorder"). A failure shows `✕ <message>` in place of the row's `Waits on` line (U157) and the row settles back. Tests: `u362_a_drop_moves_to_the_workstream_then_project_root`, `u362_a_drop_elsewhere_or_escape_calls_nothing`, `u362_a_failed_move_shows_the_message`.
- **No conflict with reorder.** A drag that ends inside the Shelf's list is U155's reorder and calls `todo.reorder`, never `todo.move`; a drag that ends on the Rail never reorders. Test: `u362_a_drag_inside_the_shelf_still_reorders`.
- **Keyboard.** The keyboard path to `todo.move` is the Drawer's `Move` (U157, Enter on the row opens the Drawer), unchanged: Tab to `Move`, Enter, choose a Home. U152's `Move` row reads `drag the row onto a Workstream or the Project root` for the pointer and the Drawer for the keyboard. Test: `u362_the_drawer_move_still_works_by_keyboard` (the U157 Drawer cases, run unchanged).
- **Kept.** U35's "no word over a title", U112 and U138's focus rule hold: after a drop, focus is on the Todo's row if it is still in the list, else on the Rail row dropped on, never `body`. Test: `u362_focus_after_a_drop_is_never_body`. The Todo's Home is visible after the move in its Drawer (U157) and in the Shelf when included by U2744a’s Rail-derived scope.
