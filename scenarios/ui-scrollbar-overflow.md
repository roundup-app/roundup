# UI: Scrollbars appear only for scrolling content (U3530a–U3530c)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U3530a, U3530b, U3530c. Work order: [#3530](https://github.com/saadshahd/roundup/issues/3530).

The same rule applies to every App scroll area, including library-owned elements, portals and shadow roots. U3530b replaces U3747's hover trigger and U3748's hover observer; U3749's overlay requirement is strengthened to exclude a permanently reserved scrollbar gutter. Existing intentional hidden bars remain hidden. This specification delivers acceptance only; #3530 remains open for implementation.

**U3530a a Terminal scrollbar represents real scrollback.** Given an open Project and a newly opened Terminal using the real xterm 6 emulator, when its initial fit has settled and output contains fewer lines than its fitted row count, with no scrollback, then its vertical scrollbar is hidden even with the pointer inside the Terminal. When output grows beyond the row count and the user scrolls through the resulting scrollback, then its vertical thumb appears; when scrolling stops, then it fades and is hidden within one second of the last scroll, with the pointer still inside. The test must observe the visible thumb while scrolling before accepting its disappearance, so permanently hiding scrollbars cannot pass.

Observer: one planned browser test, `u3530a_terminal_scrollbar_tracks_scrollable_content`, in `apps/desktop/src/terminal/u3530_browser.test.ts`. Use the existing U26 harness, real emulator and App event seam (`window.__fake.writeOutput`), with short, non-wrapping numbered lines. Open the Terminal through the App; wait for the fitted screen and parsed output before scrolling. Derive the short and long outputs from the fitted rows, rather than a hard-coded screen height. Inspect xterm 6's `.xterm-scrollable-element` and its vertical `.scrollbar > .slider`, including effective ancestor visibility; a legacy `.xterm-viewport` or a native CSS scrollbar is not this observer. Read actual scroll-position movement as well as thumb visibility. Reuse the existing isolated browser infrastructure; no fake emulator, real vendor program, new App command or test-only RPC is needed.

Its visibility assertions are explicitly required by this work order. No duplicate jsdom, source-text, copied-duration or per-surface browser tests are commissioned. **U3530b one overlay rule applies throughout the App.** Given each scroll area inventoried under U3530c, when exercised in the rendered App, then the following acceptance holds (policy; the design critic observes these clauses, with U3530a providing the Terminal CI guard):

1. **No overflow, no bar.** With empty content or content that fits, neither a thumb nor a track is painted on that axis, at rest, on hover or after wheel input. A fitting Terminal has no scrollback and no scrollbar caused solely by fractional cell-height rounding or its initial fit/resize sequence. Do not discard legitimate output or disable scrollback to achieve this. Existing fit and scrollback behavior is held by `apps/desktop/src/terminal/emulator.ts`, `createXtermEmulators`, and `scenarios/ui.md`, headings U12, U13 and U34 on main.
2. **Scrolling shows only that area's bar.** With overflowing content, wheel, trackpad, keyboard and thumb-drag scrolling reveal that area's thumb while its scroll position changes. Hover alone does not reveal it or keep it visible. When the last scrolling input/movement ends, the bar fades to hidden within one second, even if the pointer stays inside. Scrolling again during the fade restores visibility from its current appearance. Scrolling an inner area does not reveal a stationary ancestor's bar. Programmatic output may move a Terminal to the bottom, but cannot leave a bar visible after the same idle bound. The prior trigger is held by `apps/desktop/src/scrollHover.ts`, `trackScrollHover`, and `apps/desktop/src/scrollbar.css`, `[data-scroll-hover]` and the xterm hover rule; replace that trigger rather than layering another permanent hover rule over it.
3. **Overlay means no reserved space.** On either axis the bar overlays content: showing, hiding or removing it changes neither the area's content dimensions nor its rows' boxes. Compare against the same area with its scrollbar disabled, not merely a transparent bar that always reserves space. No gutter or fit reserve exists solely for a scrollbar. A Terminal loses no column or row to a bar; visibility changes send no `terminal.resize`. The overlay acceptance is held by U3530b in this file, replacing retired U3749, and `apps/desktop/src/terminal/u3747_scrollbar.test.tsx`, test `u3747_showing_the_bar_over_a_terminal_sends_no_resize`; retain the latter's no-resize proof rather than adding a duplicate.
4. **One appearance and existing access.** Keep the slim Token-coloured thumb, no track or arrows, no new tab stop or accessible role, and the intentionally hidden bars from `scenarios/ui-scrollbar.md`, heading U3747, clauses 1, 3, 4 and 6. Wheel, trackpad, keyboard and thumb dragging still reach the full content. Use the existing `--duration-scrollbar` and `--easing-out` Tokens for the fade; under reduced motion show and hide instantly at the same activity/idle boundaries. Main's Token definitions are in `apps/desktop/src/tokens.css`, `:root`; the reduced-motion contract is in `scenarios/ui-visual.md`, heading U135. Fade is state feedback, never geometry animation. Any idle setting is shared across surfaces, not copied into each caller.
5. **Scrolling remains contained.** At either end, further scrolling moves no ancestor or the App window. Main's holders are `scenarios/ui-no-window-scroll.md`, headings U3785 and U3786, and `stylelint.config.mjs`, the scroll-rule overscroll guard. This work changes scrollbar visibility, not the existing containment contract.
