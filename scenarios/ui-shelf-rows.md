# UI: one row design for the Shelf's Todos and Pads (U359, U360)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U359, U360.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #673, "the todos spacing and alignment is not designed at all. Same for pads too", read on `tree-40` at 1280 by 820, dark, in a 163 px Shelf column: its rows start at six different x (`apps/desktop/src/todos/rowButton.styles.css:49-62` pads the list and each row by `--space-2`, `:83` and `:100` indent the Todo's lines by `--space-4` and `--space-5`, and a Pad has no such indent); a Todo is a `--ground` card of `--radius-row` (`rowButton.styles.css:58-65`) while a Pad and a Rail row (`apps/desktop/src/rail/styles.css:12`) are plain; the page font is `var(--mono)` on `:root` (`apps/desktop/src/styles.css:3`), so every word of the Shelf is mono, against `docs/design-system.md`'s "Mono is for the Terminal, paths, ids and code"; a Todo title wraps without bound while a Pad name is cut to one line (U29). D1 to D11 read Token values, not alignment, so no Check caught it. Narrows U151, U153, U154 and U172's Item and Groups clauses, and supersedes U29's "one line" for the Shelf. #400 and PR #658 (U172, U173) own the group heads and the Rail; this scenario owns the rows under them. #621 sets the Rail's alignment rule, which these rows take; #622 makes the Shelf resizable, so every clause holds at any Shelf width of 163 px or more.

**U359 a Todo row and a Pad row are one designed row.** Given the Shelf's `Todos` and `Pads` tabs (U15, U18, U373) on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run` (U26), light and dark, at 1280 by 820 and 700 by 800, with a fixture added for the test holding a 300-character Todo title, a 120-character Pad name, a Todo with ten open blockers and a Todo whose Home is a Workstream with a 60-character name (U154), when a test reads the rendered tree, computed style and boxes, then:

- **One row.** A Todo's `[data-shelf-row]` and a Pad's share one component and one rule set. A row holds, left to right, a 16 px Glyph (U132; a Todo's `·` or `⏸` of U15, a Pad's owner mark of U18) and a text column. The text column holds, top to bottom, the title (a Pad's name), then at most one quiet meta line. The meta line holds, in order and only those present: the Todo's `#<id>`, its `waits on #<id>, #<id>` (U35) and its Home line `in <home>` (U151); a Pad's `owned by <owner name>`. U151's "`<glyph> #<id> <title>`" and U153's `2ch` indent change to this order, and the implementing PR rewrites `u15_`, `u151_`, `u153_` and `u154_` to say so and names them in its body. Test: `u359_a_todo_and_a_pad_share_one_row`.
- **Plain.** A row has `background-color` transparent over the Shelf's `--sunken`, no border, no shadow and `border-radius` `--radius-row` only through its hover and selected wash (U172's Selection: `--hover` on hover, `--selected` with a 2 px `--accent` leading bar when its Drawer is open, a background and never a border, with no transition). A row has no filled ground, so a Todo is never a card while a Pad is plain. Test: `u359_no_row_is_a_card`.
- **Left edges.** In each list, every row's Glyph has one left edge and every row's first word has one left edge, within 1 px; the first word's edge is the Glyph's edge plus 16 px plus `--space-2` (`--space-5`, 24 px). The two lists' Glyph edges are the same x, and it is also the left edge of both group heads' 16 px mark and of the `+` add row (U172). The Shelf draws no other left edge for a row: the list's own padding is `0`. Test: `u359_each_list_has_one_glyph_edge_and_one_text_edge`.
- **Hanging lines.** Every line after a row's first (a wrapped title line, the meta line) starts at the title's left edge within 1 px, so a second line hangs under the title and never under the Glyph. Test: `u359_lines_after_the_first_hang_under_the_title`.
- **Rhythm.** Row height is at least 28 px (U131) and a row's padding-block is `--space-1`. Rows touch with a gap of `0` between them (their padding is the rhythm); a group head sits `--space-1` above its first row; the two lists are `--space-3` apart (`apps/desktop/src/styles.css:106`). The numbers are the same in the `todos` list and the `pads` list, measured from the row boxes. Every margin, padding and gap is `0` or a space step (D2). Test: `u359_the_gaps_are_space_steps_and_the_same_in_both_lists`.
- **Type.** A title and a Pad name are `--font-ui` `--text-body` in `--text`; the meta line is `--font-ui` `--text-caption` in `--grey`, except a `#<id>` and a Home path or project root name, which are `--font-mono` (U131). No other word of the Shelf is `--font-mono`. Test: `u359_words_that_are_not_ids_or_paths_use_the_ui_font`.
- **Long text.** A title and a Pad name each show at most two lines and then `…` (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2` with `overflow: hidden`, as `.item-description` does at `rowButton.styles.css:95-105`); the meta line shows at most one line and then `…`. The full text is the row's `title` attribute and shows whole in the row's Drawer (U17, U20). No row is wider than its list and a hover or focus changes no row's top edge, height or wrapping (U112). U154's "no text is cut with an ellipsis" changes to this clause, and the implementing PR rewrites `u154_` to say so. Test: `u359_a_long_title_and_a_long_pad_name_end_the_same_way`.
- **Kept.** Every action of U152 keeps its pointer and keyboard path, the out-of-flow `complete` and `move` words of U35 and U157 never move a row, and drag (U155) and `⌥↑`/`⌥↓` still work; the `u152_`, `u155_`, `u156_` and `u157_` tests pass with no change but their selectors. Hit areas stay at least 24 px (D6) and `window.__checks()` passes D1 to D11 on every seed above in both schemes (U136), with no Check passing on `main` regressing.

The row is designed with the `emil-design-eng` skill: the Rail's row (Glyph, name, quiet meta; `rail/styles.css:12`) and the Solo sidebar of #400 are the reference, one design with the least ink (no card, no fill, no border), an even rhythm from Tokens, and a state change that answers in the same frame (U133). The implementing PR names, for each of its choices, the Rail row it follows.

**U360 three Checks measure the rows' alignment, type and rhythm.** Found by #673: no Check read alignment. Narrows U137 and U136. Given `window.__checks()` (U137), then it returns three more entries, `D12`, `D13` and `D14`, each `{"status", "value", "selector"}` for what is on screen now, in the colour scheme the page is in, and `docs/design-system.md`'s table of checks gains the three rows, which `apps/desktop/src/testing/checks.ts` implements (its header, `CHECK_IDS` and `loop/rules.sh delta` read D1 to D14). - **D12 left edges.** In each of the `Todos` and `Pads` tabs, the Glyphs' left x are within 1 px of one another, the first words' left x are within 1 px of one another, and the left x of every line after a row's first is within 1 px of its row's title. The value is the largest difference measured, in px, with the selector of the row it was read on. Fails today: `tree-40` at 1280 by 820 has a Todo Glyph at 1140 and a Pad Glyph at 1117.
- **D13 font role.** In the Shelf, every text node's font family is `--font-ui` unless its element is an id (`#<id>`), a path (a Home line's path) or code; one that is `--font-mono` and none of those fails. The value is the count of offending nodes. Fails today: all of the Shelf's words are mono.
- **D14 row shape.** In the Shelf, no row has a non-transparent `background-color`, a border or a shadow at rest; the gap between neighbouring rows, and between the tablist and the list's first row is `0` or a space step and is equal across the two lists (U359's Rhythm; one list shows at a time, U373); no title or name shows more than two lines, and none overflows its row. The value is the first measure that fails. Fails today: a Todo row has `background-color: var(--ground)` and `border-radius: var(--radius-row)` (`rowButton.styles.css:63-64`).
