# UI: a right-button drag pulls a bubble out (U421)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U421. Found by the user's report #813 (2026-10-11): "make the terminal a bubble that I can pull with right click from anywhere at any point". The user picked "Hold and drag out" over pulling the Bubble to the pointer with a plain right-click and over a right-click menu, and kept minimise, restore and transparency as U164 says. Narrows U164 and U165; changes no contract, App seam command or Token. Adds the term **Pull** to `GLOSSARY.md`.

**U421 a right-button drag pulls the selected Workstream's Terminal out as a bubble.** Given an open Project whose selection resolves to a Workstream (U145), then:
- **Start.** Anywhere in the window (the Rail, the Shelf, the header, the centre pane, a Drawer, a Terminal's emulator, a bubble), a mouse press of the right button followed by pointer movement with the button held starts a Pull once the pointer is 8 px or more from where it was pressed (Euclidean, in CSS pixels). 8 px is engineering call 1: past the 3 to 4 px a hand wobbles while it clicks, and short enough that the bubble follows at once. Only a mouse's right button counts; the left button, touch and pen do not, and a press with the `⌘`, `⌃`, `⌥` or `⇧` key held does not start one. A press that ends under 8 px is a plain right-click (below). Test: `u421_a_right_drag_of_8_px_starts_a_pull_and_7_does_not`, `u421_left_touch_pen_and_modified_presses_start_no_pull`, `u421_a_pull_starts_over_the_rail_the_shelf_the_header_a_drawer_and_a_terminal`.
- **Which Terminal.** The Workstream's bubble last focused, else its latest started (U164's order); a minimised one is restored by the Pull. With no Terminal in the Workstream, the Pull calls `terminal.open` for it once (U165's call and its default place do not apply: the bubble opens where the pointer is, and takes focus as U165 says). While that call is in flight the Pull waits, moves the bubble to the pointer as soon as the bubble exists, and calls nothing more. A failed call shows U9's `✕ <message>` line (U80) and opens nothing. Test: `u421_the_pull_takes_the_last_focused_bubble_else_the_latest`, `u421_a_minimised_bubble_is_restored_by_the_pull`, `u421_a_workstream_with_no_terminal_opens_one_with_one_call`, `u421_a_failed_open_shows_the_failure_line_and_opens_nothing`.
- **Follow and land.** While the button is held the bubble's header grip is under the pointer: the pointer holds the bubble at the middle of its header, and the bubble moves with the pointer, written at most once per frame (U164's rule for a drag), kept inside the centre pane (U164: a bubble never leaves the pane, so a pointer outside the pane holds the bubble at the nearest edge). Releasing the button ends the Pull and the bubble stays where it is, with its size, and that place is kept for the Daemon's life as U164 keeps a moved bubble's (engineering call 2 of `scenarios/quick-terminal.md`). A bubble that was hidden by the selection (U164) is not pulled. The pane under it does not resize. The Pull moves no selection and no Rail row, sends no `terminal.write`, and a Terminal's mouse reporting (U11) receives nothing from a press that became a Pull. Esc during a Pull ends it where the bubble is. Test: `u421_the_bubble_follows_the_pointer_and_stays_where_it_is_released`, `u421_the_bubble_stays_inside_the_pane_when_the_pointer_leaves_it`, `u421_a_pull_moves_no_selection_and_writes_nothing_to_the_terminal`, `u421_esc_ends_the_pull_in_place`, `u421_a_pulled_place_survives_a_reload_and_is_forgotten_at_daemon_exited`.
- **A plain right-click keeps today's behaviour.** _Amended by U2694e: a right-click that never reaches 8 px opens the native menu (U2694a) on release for a Rail row, a Project tab, a Terminal tab, a Pad or a Todo, and a right-button drag past 8 px pulls and opens no menu; `u2694e_` has a test for each._ A press and release that never reaches 8 px does nothing of its own: the Rail's menu opens as U56 says, a Terminal's own right-click reaches it as before, and a right-click elsewhere is the browser's as before. Once a press becomes a Pull, the `contextmenu` that follows its release is cancelled (`preventDefault`), so no Rail menu and no browser menu opens at the end of a Pull, and none shows while it runs. A Pull that returns to under 8 px of the press still ended as a Pull. Test: `u421_a_plain_right_click_on_a_rail_row_still_opens_the_menu`, `u421_a_plain_right_click_in_a_terminal_reaches_it`, `u421_the_release_of_a_pull_opens_no_menu`.
- **Nothing to pull.** With nothing selected, an empty Project, a selection that does not resolve to a Workstream, a `terminal.open` already in flight from `⌘T` or another Pull, or after `daemon-exited` (U25), a right-button drag does nothing and its release is a plain release (its `contextmenu` is not cancelled). A Rail menu (U56) or the switcher (U51) open when the press lands: the press closes it as a click elsewhere does and starts no Pull. Test: `u421_with_no_workstream_selected_a_right_drag_does_nothing`, `u421_a_pull_during_an_open_call_or_after_daemon_exited_does_nothing`, `u421_an_open_rail_menu_closes_on_the_press_and_starts_no_pull`.
- **The keys and the rest hold.** `⌘T`, `⌘B`, `⌘.` and `⇧⌘⌫` (U165), the header drag and the slider, minimise and restore, and the transparency and contrast of U164 are unchanged; a Pull is one more way to move a bubble, not a second way to open one beside `⌘T`. The gesture has no keyboard form of its own: U164's arrow keys on the header move a bubble (U31), and `⌘T` opens one. Policy, covered by U164 and U165's tests, which keep passing.
