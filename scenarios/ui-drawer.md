# UI: a Drawer never moves the layout (U111)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U111, U388, U409, U430.

Reported by the user (screenshot): opening a Pad opened the Drawer far wider than usual, the terminal was pushed off the left edge and the Rail was covered; `close` did not bring the layout back. This breaks U24 ("a Drawer never covers the Rail") and U5 ("the terminal pane's size never changes") as the user sees them, and no test noticed.

**U111 opening and closing a Drawer leaves the layout where it was.** Given an open Project and any Drawer (a Todo, a Pad, or one opened by `⌘K`), when it opens and then closes, by `close`, Esc (U28) or a new selection (U64), then the Rail, the Shelf and the terminal pane are at the same horizontal position they had before it opened, and the panel that holds them cannot be scrolled sideways at all, by the user, by a script or by the browser moving focus into the off-screen Drawer. Two things are observable without a display: the element that holds the Rail, the terminal and the Shelf has the computed `overflow-x` value `clip` (not `hidden`, which a focus change can still scroll), and every `focus()` that U40 makes into or out of the Drawer is called with `{preventScroll: true}`. Narrowing U40 and U5: `u40_drawer_focus` and `u5_drawer` keep their clauses and gain these. Also while a Drawer is open its width is the value U24 gives (`min(560px, 70%, calc(100% - var(--rail-width)))`, the Rail's width as U389 sets it), and the Drawer's `getBoundingClientRect` is read in the real-browser harness check of U26 where one exists; in jsdom only the declared values and the two checks above are asserted.
Law: Opening or closing a Drawer never moves the Rail, the terminal or the Shelf. Check: y2_

Tests (`u111_`) fail on main today: a spy on `HTMLElement.prototype.focus` records its options during open and close and finds none with `preventScroll`; the computed `overflow-x` of the columns element is `hidden`. The cause is confirmed by ux-auditor-1 (`artifacts/ux/findings.md`, F1): `.columns` is `overflow: hidden`, the closed Drawer at `translateX(100%)` adds 560 px of scroll width, and U40 focuses the Drawer while it is still off screen, so `scrollLeft` reaches 560 during the slide at 1600, 1280 and 1000 px wide, for both the Todo and the Pad Drawer, and stays there in the real WKWebView window (800 by 600) though Chrome returns it to 0. The fix keeps U40's "focus moves into it". No earlier test could fail: U24's test reads only the declared width, U5's reads the transform and the size observer, U40's reads `document.activeElement`, and jsdom neither lays out nor scrolls on focus. Where the real-window check runs, `scrollLeft` of the columns element is 0 at every frame of the slide in and out.

**U388 the Pad Drawer's ask field answers rest, hover and focus like the other fields.** Found by a Feedback report: on `tree-40` with `pad-3` open, `Ask or edit this pad` is a bare `input` (`apps/desktop/src/pads/styles.css:176`) with the browser's `2px inset rgb(118,118,118)` border, a white fill, a height of 21px, no radius and no hover change, while `.spawn-prompt-field` (`apps/desktop/src/rail/styles.css:194-213`) has a `--hairline` border, a 24px height and a hover wash. Narrows D6; changes no contract, App seam command or Token. Out of scope: the Pad's content, `send`, the exchange list. Given the `tree-40` seed with a Pad Drawer open (U40), then `.pad-ask input` has:
- **Rest.** `border-style` is not `inset`, `border-color` is `--hairline`, `background-color` is `--ground`, `color` is `--text`, and its height is at least 24px. Asserted in `u388_the_ask_field_rests_as_a_token_box`.
- **Hover.** `background-color` under `:hover` is `--hover`, which differs from rest; under `:active` it is `--selected`. Asserted in `u388_the_ask_field_washes_on_hover`.
- **Focus.** Under `:focus-visible` it draws a `2px` `--accent` outline (the ring `apps/desktop/src/styles.css:21` gives every control), not clipped by the Drawer. Asserted in `u388_the_ask_field_shows_the_accent_focus_ring`.
- **Kept.** The field keeps its label, width and behaviour (U190); every length is `0` or a Token step (D2).

Observer: `u388_` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/` reading computed style with the stylesheets loaded. `agent-browser` in the U26 harness captures the field at rest, hovered and focused to `artifacts/ux/U388/`, with `window.__checks()` read for each.

**U409 a Drawer covers the Shelf from the window's top to its foot.** Found by the user's report #1102 (a Todo's Drawer beside the Shelf: "so slopy and undesigned"); the user chose "wider, full height" over taking the Shelf's exact place and over opening in the centre pane. Narrows U5 ("slides in from the right over the terminal and the Shelf"), U24 and U111 (the Drawer's width), and U369 and U371 (the Shelf's box and its place below a Drawer). Given an open Project with the Shelf open (U370), and a Todo, a Pad or a Work order Drawer open (they share `DrawerHost`), at 1280 by 820 and at 700 by 800, then:
- **Full height.** The Drawer's box top is `0` and its bottom is the window's bottom; the Shelf's box, its `Todos` / `Pads` bar and its hide button lie wholly inside the Drawer's box, so nothing of the Shelf shows above or beside the Drawer. With the Shelf closed the Drawer is the same height.
- **Wider than the Shelf.** _Overturned by U430 (the centre narrows)._ The Drawer is wider than the Shelf by at least one Token step (D2), so exactly one vertical edge of the Drawer, its leading edge, falls in the centre pane; that edge is the 1px `--hairline` border with `--shadow-drawer` (D5 holds for it) and no other Drawer edge or shadow is drawn. Its width stays no more than the window less the Rail's width (U24, U111), so it never covers the Rail.
- **One close.** The Drawer holds one `close` and the Shelf's hide button is not visible while the Drawer is open (it is covered, and `inert` or not focusable behind the Drawer); the `close` is in the Drawer's top corner, at the window's top.
- **Kept.** Opening and closing slide as U5 says (about 180 ms, ease-out, instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`); Esc closes (U28), focus follows U40 with `{preventScroll: true}` (U111), the read on opening is unchanged, the Rail, the Terminal and the Shelf do not move (U111), and Help and the switcher (U51, U54) still float over the Drawer. Only Tokens are used (rule 8).

Observer: `u409_` tests in `apps/desktop/src/drawer/` assert the declared rules (the `.overlay` layer spans both rows, the `.drawer` width and border) and, in the real-browser harness of U26 (`agent-browser`, headless), the boxes above at both viewports with `window.__checks()` read, captured to `artifacts/ux/U409/`. Moves D2, D5, D6 and D8. Scope: `apps/desktop/src/drawer/`, `app/Layout.tsx` and the `.overlay` and `.drawer` rules of `styles.css`; no contract, App seam command or Token changes.

**U430 an open Drawer narrows the centre and covers nothing.** Found by the user's report #1109: "while a todo/pad is open I should be able to still keep them open copy from them or highlight in them and inject that into the agent prompt I'm making". U409 made the Drawer wider than the Shelf, so it covers part of the centre and part of the input the user types into; asked, the user chose "the centre narrows" over a Drawer that stops above the input, and plain copy and paste over an add-to-input button or dragging. Overturned by U430: U409's "Wider than the Shelf" clause (its leading edge in the centre pane, over it) and U5's "slides in over the terminal and the Shelf". U409's full height, one close and its edge styling stay. Given an open Project with the Shelf open (U370) and a Todo, a Pad or a Work order Drawer open, at 1280 by 820 and at 700 by 800, then:
- **Covers nothing.** The Drawer's box overlaps no box of the Rail, the Shelf, the centre pane or the centre's input (U409's full height stays: top `0`, bottom the window's). The centre pane's right edge is the Drawer's leading edge, and the centre's width is the window less the Rail and the Drawer (less the Shelf where U24 leaves it beside the centre), no more and no less. The Drawer's width is at most 70% of the centre's width before it opened, so the centre keeps at least 30% of it. The Rail and the Shelf do not move (U111). Asserted in `u430_the_drawer_covers_nothing`.
- **Input whole.** The Door's composer, a Takeover's input and a Terminal each keep their whole box inside the narrowed centre: none is clipped or scrolled sideways, and `elementFromPoint` at the centre of each returns that element, not the Drawer. Asserted in `u430_the_centre_input_stays_whole`.
- **Select and copy.** Text in the Drawer is selectable (`user-select` is not `none` on it or its text) and `copy` of a selection in it puts that text on the clipboard; the Drawer's selection survives focus moving to the centre's input. Asserted in `u430_drawer_text_selects_and_copies`.
- **Click and type in the centre.** With a Drawer open, a click in the centre's input focuses it, and typed keys and a paste of the copied text reach it (the Terminal's through U12 and U385, the composer's as text); the Drawer stays open. Narrows U40: focus leaves the Drawer without closing it. Asserted in `u430_the_centre_takes_focus_with_the_drawer_open`.
- **Close.** Only `close` and Esc outside a field close it (U28); a click or a key in the centre, the Rail or the Shelf does not. Asserted in `u430_only_close_and_esc_close_the_drawer`.
- **Motion.** Opening and closing keep U5's timing and reduced-motion rule (about 180 ms, ease-out, instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`); the centre narrows and widens over the same duration and curve, so at every frame the Drawer's leading edge and the centre's right edge are the same x (no gap, no overlap). Asserted in `u430_the_centre_and_the_drawer_move_as_one`.
- **Terminal.** A Terminal in the centre refits to its narrower width, and its program gets one resize at the end of the motion, not one per frame; U5's "the terminal pane's size never changes" no longer holds for the width, and the Terminal's rows and the Rail's and Shelf's sizes do not change. Asserted in `u430_the_terminal_refits_once`.
- **Typing speed.** With a Drawer open, keystroke-to-render stays under 16 ms p95 (rule 7), measured by `just perf`. Asserted in `u430_typing_with_a_drawer_open_stays_fast`.

Observer: `u430_` tests in `apps/desktop/src/drawer/` assert the declared layout rules and, in the real-browser harness of U26 (`agent-browser`, headless), the boxes above at both viewports with `window.__checks()` read, captured to `artifacts/ux/U430/`. Moves D2, D5, D6 and D8. Scope: webview only, `apps/desktop/src/drawer/`, `app/Layout.tsx` and the layout rules of `styles.css`; no contract, App seam command or Token changes.
