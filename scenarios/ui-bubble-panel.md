# UI: the bubble is a transient panel on top that minimises on an outside press (U2307a, U2307b, U2307c)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U2307a, U2307b, U2307c. Work Issue: #2307. Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the owner: "there is a bug when I click outside the bubble the bubble loses focus but still overlays it should get minimised / also the bar of the bubble needs proper bubble window design / the open bubble should have the highest zIndex". For the bar the owner picked "macOS panel" over the other bars offered. Narrows U164, U523, U2236 and U2237; keeps one bubble shown at a time (U2236), the U469 bottom bar and U421's Pull. Observer: Vitest against the fake App and the isolated Chromium harness (U26) at one viewport, 1512 by 950, light and dark for behavioral observers. Captures and critic Check readings use dark only (U3725). Never a native Mac capture.

The bubble is a transient floating panel. While open it is the topmost layer and owns focus; a press outside it minimises it; one open or minimised state drives its layer, its dismissal and its bar.

Engineering calls, each made here so the implementation run asks nothing:

1. **One state.** The bubble's `open` or `minimised` state (U164, U469) is the single source of its layer, its dismissal and its bar. No second flag, and no z-index number raised to win a stacking fight.
2. **The library owns the layer.** The open bubble is a non-modal `@ark-ui/solid` Dialog (or Popover) with its dismissable layer and a Portal: the open bubble is in the browser top layer (`popover="manual"`, or a non-modal dialog shown in the top layer; WebKit supports both), which paints above every `z-index`, so no other layer's `z-index` changes and U449 holds, and the library's dismissable layer handles its outside press; its Escape handling is off (`closeOnEscape: false`), since `esc` belongs to the Terminal. Reuse (rule 9): `@ark-ui/solid` (already a dependency; the Dialog and the Popover are its maintained layer parts) and Zag's `@zag-js/dismissable` it is built on; custom document listeners and a `z-index` number fail both, since the library already has the layer stack and a numeric z-index on the bubble cannot out-rank the existing numeric layers (`.overlay` 2; toasts, switcher, menus and ui layers 3), whereas the top layer does.
3. **Outside.** Outside is every node that is not the open bubble: the Rail, the Door terminal behind it, the header, a menu, the Drawer. The bubble's own tab or chip in the U469 bar is inside: it toggles the bubble as U469 says, and a press on it never minimises first and restores second. Only a primary-button `pointerdown` dismisses; a press that starts U421's Pull, a U2236 resize or a header drag begins inside the bubble and never dismisses it.
4. **Click-through.** The dismissing press is not swallowed: its target receives the same press and the click that follows (the library's non-modal layer blocks no page pointer events; U2742a adds a pointer-transparent native backdrop).
5. **Run bubbles.** A Run's bubble still appears open without taking focus (U164): it is the topmost layer from then on, and the first outside `pointerdown` minimises it like any open bubble. Only a user-opened or restored bubble takes focus (U165).
6. **Layers arriving later.** A toast, a menu or the Drawer that opens while the bubble is open (by an event or the keyboard, since a pointer press on its trigger already minimised the bubble) sits beneath it: the bubble's layer is the last one pushed on restore and the toast region is below it.

**U2307a an outside press minimises the open bubble.** Amends U164 (a bubble minimises only by `Minimise` and `⌘.`; it now also minimises on an outside press). U165 is unchanged: every key, `esc` included, reaches the Terminal. Given an open bubble in an open Project, then:

- **Outside press.** A primary `pointerdown` on the Rail, on the Door terminal behind the bubble or on the header minimises the bubble to its chip once, and the same press then reaches its target: a Rail row is selected, the Door terminal takes focus, a header control activates (call 4). A press inside the bubble (header, emulator, `Minimise`, `Close`) or on its own tab or chip does not dismiss it. Test: `u2307a_an_outside_press_minimises_and_still_reaches_its_target`, `u2307a_a_press_inside_or_on_its_own_tab_does_not_minimise`.
- **Escape is the Terminal's.** `esc` typed in the bubble goes to the Terminal (`terminal.write`, U165) and leaves the bubble open and focused; so does every other key. Test: `u2307a_escape_and_other_keys_still_reach_the_terminal`.
- **Once.** A minimised bubble stays minimised on a later press; its Terminal runs on and its place and size survive (U164, U2236). Restoring it from its tab, chip or `⌘.` opens it again with focus, and focus returns to what held it before (U40). Test: `u2307a_a_minimised_bubble_keeps_its_place_and_size_and_restores`.

Tests: `u2307a_an_outside_press_minimises_and_still_reaches_its_target`, `u2307a_a_press_inside_or_on_its_own_tab_does_not_minimise` and `u2307a_escape_and_other_keys_still_reach_the_terminal` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u2307_panel.test.tsx` drive the real Panel through jsdom. The design critic observes the rendered band/inset hit targets. Moves: D6.

**U2307b the open bubble is the topmost layer.** Narrows U164 and U523. Given an open bubble, then:

- **Library layer.** The bubble renders through the Dialog or Popover and Portal of call 2: its root is a child of the Portal, not of the centre pane, it is in the browser top layer (`:popover-open` or `dialog[open]` non-modal), and no stylesheet or inline style under `apps/desktop/src/` gives the bubble's own node a numeric `z-index`. Other layers keep their `z-index` (U449 unchanged). Test: `u2307b_the_bubble_is_a_library_layer_and_no_z_index_orders_it` (Vitest, reads the rendered DOM and the computed styles; it pins the layer boundary).
- **Unchanged.** Moving into a Portal changes neither the bubble's place and size nor the pane under it. Test: `u2307b_the_portal_keeps_the_place_and_the_pane_size`.

Tests: `u2307b_` as named. Moves: D2, D8.

**U2307c the bar is a macOS panel bar.** Amends U164's header (its text `Minimise` and `Close`, and its state words `Running` and `Exit <code>`, which U3704 later removes) and U523's header controls. The state words become a dot (later removed). Given a shown bubble, then:

- **Dots.** At the left, in this order, a close dot then a minimise dot: small circles of ink only, no box, no outline, no hover or press fill (U510: hover moves the ink, press dims it). Close calls `terminal.close` once (U164); minimise folds the bubble (U164). Each is a native button with an accessible name (`Close`, `Minimise` from `copy.ts`), Tab reaches it and it draws no focus ring (U31, U133, U3528; policy).
- **Title.** The Terminal's name (U164, renamed in place as U511a says) sits on the bar. No status follows it (U3704: a plain Terminal shows no state).
- **Unchanged.** The header is still the drag handle, a double-click on its empty space still fills and restores (U2237), and the arrow keys on the focused header still move and resize it (U164). Test: `u2307c_the_bar_keeps_drag_fill_and_keyboard_moves`.

Test: `u2307c_the_bar_has_close_then_minimise_dots` (Chromium, `u2307c_browser.test.ts`: the buttons' order), `u2307c_the_dots_name_and_call_close_and_minimise`. Moves: D2, D5, D6, D8.

Narrowing summary, for the review run: U164's header bullet and its minimise bullet, U523's header controls, and `docs/design-system.md`'s bubble sentence (the bar). The implementation run rewrites each sentence it names as it ships. Retire the U164 and U523 assertions that read the header's `Minimise` or `Close` text or the `Running` and `Exit` words in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u164_bubbles.test.tsx` and `u523_panel.test.ts`; the U2307c tests prove their then-clauses.

**U2742a an active Bubble obscures only the background pane.** Given the actual App with a dense changing Agent terminal and a shown Bubble, when the user activates and focuses the Bubble, then one `.bubble-frame::backdrop` covers only the centre pane, dims with `--scrim` and blurs with one semantic Token whose default is 12px. The foreground ground, text, header and controls remain opaque and unfiltered. Returning to the Agent removes the backdrop in the same render; neither direction has an entry/exit animation or delay. A new unfocused Run leaves Agent focus and the clear background unchanged. Behavioral test: `u2742a_an_unfocused_run_does_not_activate_or_steal_focus` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`, using the actual App and fake Daemon. Rendered observer below proves backdrop activation/removal and foreground clarity, not a CSS declaration alone (policy).

Policy: derive backdrop activation from existing shown/focus state, never a separately persisted mode. Its pane origin/extent use the existing geometry and `pointer-events: none`; outside dismissal still activates the intended target once. Filters never nest. Reduced transparency removes blur from elements and `::backdrop` while retaining dimming. Narrow only U5/U523's background-Agent blur prohibition; no other terminal surface gains translucency. Implementation narrows `apps/desktop/src/app/u135_schemes.test.tsx`, test `u135_reduced_transparency_removes_every_backdrop_filter`, to permit only this default pseudo-element exception, reject default blur everywhere else and prove reduced transparency clears both elements and backdrop; audit cuts under rule 10.

**U2742b fresh placement leaves outside breathing room.** Given a Terminal with no saved Place, when its Bubble first opens, then on each axis `size = min(paneExtent, max(existingMinimum, paneExtent - 2 * 96))` and `position = (paneExtent - size) / 2`. The nominal outer inset is 96 CSS px per side (three `--space-6` steps), not terminal body padding; retain the existing 24-column/6-row minimum and clamping. No cascade or 75%-width/50%-height default remains. Every saved rectangle, including an old default, is preserved subject to existing clamping: storage cannot distinguish defaults from deliberate resizes. Reload, restore, drag, resize and double-click fill/restore retain their meanings; opening never resizes the Agent pane. Rendered observer below proves geometry, saved placement and fill/restore (policy); existing U164/U2236/U2237 behavioral proofs remain, without new layout assertions.

**U2742c one shortcut switches focus both ways.** Given an open connected Project, selected Agent with a main emulator and an eligible existing Terminal, when a non-repeated ⌘B is pressed inside the active Bubble, then minimise that Bubble and focus the selected Agent emulator through shared `Screens`, never its tab/header. From the Agent emulator the same chord restores and focuses the last-focused eligible Terminal, else the newest-started eligible Terminal; from a terminal tab restore and focus that tab's Terminal. Show only the selected Bubble. Rail selection and the selected Agent stay unchanged. Test: `u2742c_round_trips_type_only_into_the_focused_terminal` (actual App, fake Daemon, headless real-browser harness under `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`), repeated round trips typing into a writable Door or already-controlled Agent and the Bubble; assert only the intended `terminal.write` target and no `terminal.open` or `terminal.close`, including no chord bytes written. Close remains separate.

Given multiple eligible Terminals, a hidden other-Workstream Terminal, removal of a target or a Project switch, when switching, then use the tab/last-used/newest fallback above within the current selection and Project only. With no main Agent terminal, no eligible Terminal or a disconnected Project, do nothing; never create a Terminal, focus a disconnected node, change selection or act across Projects. Test: `u2742c_targets_stay_eligible_in_the_current_project` in the same App browser observer.
