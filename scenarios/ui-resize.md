# UI: the Rail and the Shelf resize (U389 to U391)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U389, U390, U391.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #622: "the sidebar rail and pads/todos should be width resizable", "+ new workstream on the rail should be sticky at the bottom button full width of the rail bottom not in the rail" and "the sidebar rail width should be a bit wider and more and should be resizable". Observer: rendered-page checks in Chromium on `just harness` (U26) against the fake App, light and dark, on `tree-40` and `agents-10` at 1280 by 800, and at 1280 by 400 (U85). Never a native Mac capture.

Engineering calls, each made here so the implementation run asks nothing:

- **Widths.** Two Tokens set the widths: `--rail-width` (default 288 px, minimum 252 px, maximum 480 px) and `--shelf-width` (default 320 px as U368 to U373 set it, minimum 240 px, maximum 560 px). 252 px is today's Rail and the floor at which its row words fit (U23); 288 px is 36 px wider, the "bit wider" the user asked for. Neither is a `clamp` of a window percentage any more: U23's `20%` and `clamp(252px, 20%, 320px)` go.
- **Room.** At supported window widths of at least 1280 px (U453), the Rail and Shelf use their full user resize ranges without window-dependent clamps. Their maximums sum to 1040 px, so the Shelf leaves the Rail visible.

- **Per Project.** The two stored widths are kept as U100 keeps layout and U373 keeps the open tab: the webview's saved state, keyed by Project path, restored in the frame that first shows the tree, with no animation. A stored value that is not a finite number means the default; a finite one is clamped to its limits at render and stored as given, so restoration honours the stored width within its resize limits.
- **The Edge.** `GLOSSARY.md` gains **Resize edge**: the 1 px `--hairline` line between two regions that the user drags or keys to change a width. It is `role="separator"`, `aria-orientation="vertical"`, `tabindex="0"`, with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` in px and the name `Resize Rail` or `Resize work items`. Its drawn line stays the hairline D8 allows; its hit area is 8 px wide over the full height of the region's edge, centred on the line (so it takes pointer, not layout), with `cursor: col-resize`, and a hover or focus shows one `--accent` 2 px bar over the line. It is not a button, so D6 and D7 skip it as they skip a `textarea`; a focus ring is its `--accent` bar (D5's 3:1).
- **Drag.** `pointerdown` on the Edge captures the pointer; while it is down the width follows the pointer, at most one style write per animation frame (rule 7), clamped to the limits and the room above, and nothing else re-renders. `pointerup` ends it; `Escape` while it is down restores the width it began with and ends it. A drag moves no row, scroll position or selection, and keeps U111 true: the element holding the Rail, the pane and the Shelf keeps `overflow-x: clip` and no focus call scrolls it.
- **Keyboard.** With focus on an Edge, `←` and `→` change the width by `--space-4` (16 px), with `⇧` by 64 px; `Home` and `End` set the minimum and the maximum. `→` widens the Rail and narrows the Shelf (the Shelf's Edge is its leading edge). Each press is one commit.
- **Reset.** A double-click on the Edge, or `Enter` with focus on it, sets the default.
- **The Terminal.** The Rail's width changes the pane's width, so U13's `terminal.resize {id, cols, rows}` follows, but not per frame: the emulator is not refitted while a drag is down, and when it ends (`pointerup`, a key press or a reset) the Terminal is fitted once, so a drag sends at most one `terminal.resize` per shown Terminal, and none when the fitted `cols` and `rows` are unchanged. The Shelf is an overlay and never resizes the pane: changing its width sends no `terminal.resize`, no `rail.*` call and no other call (U370's rule); the fake App records none.

**U389 the Rail's width is the user's.** Narrows U23 (the Rail's `20%`, never under 252 nor over 320 px, and "the terminal is never under 690px": the `minmax(690px, 1fr)` track at `apps/desktop/src/styles.css:97` becomes `minmax(388px, 1fr)`), U24 ("the Rail stays at 252px" under 942 px), U85 and U172's density clause (the Rail "stays 252 px wide"), which become the Tokens above. Given an open Project on `tree-40` and `agents-10`, then:

- **Default.** With nothing stored, the Rail is 288 px wide at 1280 by 800, wider than the 252 px it was.
- **Edge.** A separator named `Resize Rail` sits on the Rail's trailing edge, with the attributes above; `aria-valuenow` is the Rail's width in px.
- **Drag.** Dragging the Edge from x 288 to x 360 makes the Rail's `getBoundingClientRect().width` 360 and the pane's right edge unchanged; dragging past 480 stops at 480, and dragging under 252 stops at 252. During the drag the Terminal receives no `terminal.resize`; after `pointerup` it receives one when its fitted size changed.
- **Keys and reset.** `→` from 288 gives 304, `⇧→` 352, `Home` 252, `End` 480; a double-click and `Enter` give 288.
- **Persist.** After a drag to 360, closing and reopening the webview on the same path (U100) shows 360 in the first frame the tree appears; a second Project keeps its own; a stored `"wide"`, `NaN` or `null` means 288 and a stored `9999` renders 480.
- **Unmoved.** U111's `overflow-x: clip` and `{preventScroll: true}` hold during and after a drag; selection, fold (U8) and scroll position are the same before and after; the Drawer's width is `min(560px, 70%, calc(100% - <the Rail's width>))` (U24, U111).

Moves: D2, D5, D8, D10. Owns `apps/desktop/src/styles.css`, `tokens.css` and a new `resize/` directory.

**U390 the Shelf's width is the user's.** Narrows U369 to U371 (`--shelf-width`, "capped at `calc(100% - 252px)`") and U372's "the Shelf's width never causes a horizontal scrollbar". Given the Shelf open on `tree-40`, then:

- **Default.** With nothing stored, the Shelf is 320 px wide at 1280 by 800; at 1280 by 400 it is 320 px (the Rail's default 288 px plus 320 px leaves 672 px of pane visible beside it).
- **Edge.** A separator named `Resize work items` sits on the Shelf's leading edge, replacing its `--hairline` there (U369: the Edge is that hairline), with the attributes above and the same hit area, drag, keys, reset and persistence as U389 over 240 to 560 px; dragging left widens it, and `←` widens it.
- **Room.** Its user resize range stops at 560 px; at supported window widths the Rail is never covered, even at both maximum widths.
- **Overlay.** A drag moves the Rail, the pane and its Terminal not at all, and sends no call: the fake App records none (U370, U371). The Project tab strip (U369) ends at the Shelf's moving leading edge, its right edge equal to the Shelf's left within 1 px during and after a drag.
- **Closed.** With the Shelf closed (U370) the Edge is not in the layout and not in the Tab order; the stored width is kept and applies on open.

Tests (`u390_`): `u390_default_is_320`, `u390_the_edge_is_a_focusable_separator`, `u390_a_drag_widens_leftward_and_clamps`, `u390_the_rail_is_never_covered`, `u390_a_drag_sends_no_call`, `u390_the_strip_follows_the_edge`, `u390_closed_has_no_edge`, `u390_the_width_survives_a_restart_per_project`, `u390_keys_reset_and_escape` (U389's key rows with the Shelf's direction).

**U391 `+ Workstream` is fixed to the Rail's bottom edge.** Narrows U9, U62 and U191's "pinned line", and `docs/design-system.md`'s **add** kind (PR #789). It stays kind **add** (U170 keeps its five kinds; none is added): the `+` mark and label, quiet, but the Rail's one such button now fills the Rail's width, set by layout (a component sets layout: U170), not by a new kind, and the kind's row in the table reads "trails a list or group, or, as the Rail's `+ Workstream`, fills the Rail's bottom edge". Given the Rail on `tree-40` and `agents-10`, then:

- **Place.** The button is a direct child of a footer that is outside the scrolling list: the footer is the Rail's last child, not inside the element that scrolls (`overflow-y: auto`), and its box's bottom equals the Rail's bottom and its left and right equal the Rail's, within 1 px, at every Rail width of U389 and at 1280 by 800 and 1280 by 400.
- **Sticky.** Scrolling the list to its top, middle and end leaves the button's `getBoundingClientRect()` unchanged; the list's visible area ends at the button's top, so no row is hidden under it at scroll end (U172's `scroll-margin-bottom`), and the last row can be scrolled fully above it.
- **Look.** Full width, the label left-aligned at the Rail's row inset (`--space-4`), hit height at least 28 px (U131's row height, D6), `--grey` at rest and `--text` on hover with `--hover`, pressed and focus as U171 gives the kind; one `--hairline` above it (D8, U172's blocks), no shadow, on `--sunken`. D2 and D5 hold.
- **Behaviour.** Unchanged: it calls `rail.createWorkstream` once, selects the returned Workstream and starts its Door (U9, U62), refuses repeats while pending and after `daemon-exited` (U25) with `aria-disabled`, and shows a failure in the `✕ <message>` line above it (U9). It is the Rail's only add button (U191).
- **Error line.** The `✕` line sits directly above the footer, outside the list, and is visible with the list scrolled anywhere.
