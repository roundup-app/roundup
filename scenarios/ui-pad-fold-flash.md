# UI: folding a list of Pads does not flash (U7643a)

Module: `apps/desktop/src/pads/`, `apps/desktop/src/ui/fold.ts` (the webview). Id: U7643a. Work Issue: #7643. Journey stage: Understand (`.agents/data/journey.md`). Source: the user's report #7643: "When I collapse a list of pads the content flashes". Amends U6398a to U6398d only by naming a Pad list's fold as a surface they must keep free of any flash. Webview only: no RPC method, App command, contract, code term or Token. Terms: Pad, Shelf, Rail (`GLOSSARY.md`).

**U7643a a Pad list folds without a flash.** Given the Shelf's Pads with a Home group and a closed-then-open fold (`internal` or `idle`, U7499a), and a Rail Agent row with Pads (U41), at 1512×950 in dark with reduced motion off, when the user folds or unfolds the list with the pointer or a key, then:
- **No frame shows content it should not.** Across every composited frame from the press to the fold's end, a row that stays shows the same text at the same opacity, no row appears at full opacity in a place it has left or not yet reached, and a row the fold hides never reappears after it has faded out. The folding rows leave or arrive through `opacity` only once (U6398a), never fade out and in again.
- **The kept rows are not rebuilt.** The Pad rows that stay visible keep their DOM nodes through the fold: the fold neither unmounts nor remounts them, so their selection, focus and scroll offset hold. Asserted in `u7643a_a_fold_keeps_the_rows_that_stay_as_the_same_nodes` (Vitest against the fake App: the node of a Pad above the fold is the same object before and after the press, and the folded rows are gone after the fold ends).
- **One path.** The Pad fold toggles through the fold helper of U6398a, as the other surfaces do; a key press folds at once (U6398c). The U6398a and U6398c tests stay green, and a duplicate of their proof is cut (rule 10).
- **Unchanged.** What the fold hides or shows, the remembered state (U7499a: both folds start closed), Selection and RPC calls do not change.
