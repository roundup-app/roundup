Ids: AI7152a–AI7152e. Work order: [#7152](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7152). Terms: Channel, Fork, App, Daemon (`GLOSSARY.md`). Principles served: P5 (`PRINCIPLES.md`). Needs `scenarios/channels.md` (CH7077a, CH7077c), `scenarios/data-fork.md` (FK5977d, `ForkColour`), `scenarios/fork-app.md` (FA5980a, FA5980b) and `scenarios/ui-fork-sheet.md` (FS5979c, FS5979d). Contract change (rule 4): three new App seam commands, `fork_list`, `fork_rename` and `fork_recolour`, whose one caller is the Fork sheet's list; `scenarios/app.md`'s seam table and every host and fake caller change in the same PR; `data.fork` gains only a refusal, no field. Decided by the owner (2026-11-15): "an app fork can't have the same name or color/icon as an existing source/fork/app".

## Checks on main

1. `scenarios/channels.md`, CH7077a: Stable, Dev and Source are three Channels with distinct identifiers.
2. `scenarios/channels.md`, CH7077c: Dev runs on the Fork named `Dev`, made with `--colour violet`.
3. `scenarios/fork-app.md`, FA5980a: the icon recolours the plate of `crates/desktop/icons/artwork.png` to a `ForkColour`'s hue.
4. `scenarios/data-fork.md`, FK5977d: `fork.json` holds `name` and `colour`; `name` is 1 to 40 characters.
5. `scenarios/ui-fork-sheet.md`, FS5979c: the sheet's eight swatches, one per `ForkColour`, `teal` chosen first.

**AI7152a each Channel holds one fixed name and colour.** Given the three Channels, then Stable is named `Roundup` with the `orange` plate (`artwork.png`), Dev is named `Roundup Dev` with the `violet` plate, and Source is named `Roundup Source` with the `blue` plate. The three names are pairwise different under the comparison of AI7152b and the three colours are pairwise different. Dev's and Source's bundles carry their own `.icns`, the FA5980a render of their colour, so neither shows Stable's orange plate; Stable's bundle keeps `artwork.png`. Each Channel's `CFBundleName` and `CFBundleDisplayName` equal its name, and a running Dev or Source sets its Dock icon to its own render (FA5980c's native call, fed the Channel's colour). Tests (`ai7152a_`): one function maps each Channel to its name and colour, and the three are distinct; the Dev and Source bundles' plist names and `.icns` plate hues (within 6° of the Hue column of `docs/design-system.md`) and Stable's `.icns` equal to its source; the Dock-icon decision for each Channel. Policy, Mac observer: Dock, ⌘-Tab and Spotlight show three entries that differ in name and plate.

**AI7152b one comparison, one set of held names.** Given a candidate name, then it collides with a held name when the two are equal after trimming leading and trailing whitespace and lower-casing (Unicode simple case folding), so `main`, `Main ` and `MAIN` collide. The held names are the reserved names `Roundup`, `Roundup Dev`, `Roundup Source`, `Dev` and `Source`, plus the `name` of every Fork under `$ROUNDUP_HOME/forks` (FK5977d). The held colours are the Channel colours of AI7152a plus the `colour` of every such Fork. A Fork folder with no readable `fork.json` holds nothing. The one function is the sole comparison: `data.fork`, `fork_make` and `fork_list` call it. Tests (`ai7152b_`): `main`, `Main `, `MAIN` and a tab-padded `main` collide with a Fork named `main`; `ROUNDUP`, ` dev ` and `source` collide with the reserved names; `roundups` and `Mainline` do not; an unreadable `fork.json` holds nothing.

**AI7152c `data.fork` refuses a held name or colour.** Given the held names and colours of AI7152b, when a caller sends `data.fork` whose `name` collides or whose `colour` is held by a Channel or another Fork, then it answers `INVALID_PARAMS` whose message names who holds it (`name "main" is held by the Fork at <path>`, `colour violet is held by Dev`) and writes nothing, the live data folder untouched (FK5977e). A name and a colour both held name both holders. The refusal happens before any copy, so no partial `path` exists. Dev's own Fork is the one exception: the `Dev` name and the `violet` colour are free to `data.fork` only when `path` is `<home>/forks/dev` and no Fork is there yet (CH7077c's command), so Dev's first make works. `rup data fork` prints the message and exits with the refusal's code. Tests (`ai7152c_`): each refusal against a fixture of the live folder and two Forks, naming its holder, with no file written and the live checksum unchanged; a free name and colour still copy; the Dev command's first make succeeds and a second, at another path, is refused; the CLI's output.

**AI7152d the Fork sheet offers only free colours.** Given the open sheet (FS5979c) and the held colours reported by `fork_list`, then its swatches show only the free colours, and the first free one, `teal` first and then in `ForkColour` order, is chosen at first. A name that collides marks the field invalid with the holder's name from the refusal text and Enter calls `fork_make` no more. When all eight colours are held, the swatch row is replaced by one line saying every colour is in use and that a Fork's colour must be freed by recolouring or deleting one, the name field stays, and Enter makes no Fork. `fork_make` itself refuses a held name or colour through AI7152c, and the sheet stays open with that message (FS5979e). Tests (`ai7152d_`): with `violet`, `blue` and `orange` held, five radios, `teal` chosen; with `teal` held, the first free colour is chosen; a colliding name leaves `fork_make` uncalled and shows the holder; eight held shows the line, no radio and `fork_make` uncalled; a `fork_make` refusal keeps the sheet open. The design critic judges looks at 1512×950 (rule 10).

**AI7152e a Fork made before this rule keeps working and is marked.** Given a Fork whose `name` or `colour` collides with a Channel or an older Fork, then it opens and runs as before: its data, `.app`, Dock icon and window are unchanged and no refusal touches it. `fork_list` (no params) returns each Fork under `$ROUNDUP_HOME/forks` as `{path, name, colour, collides}`, where `collides` names the other App it collides with, or is null; of two older Forks that collide, the later `created_ms` is the one marked (the Channels never are). The Fork sheet's list shows each Fork with its name and colour and marks a colliding one with the holder's name; each row offers `Rename` and `Recolour`, which rewrite only that Fork's `fork.json` (a name or colour that AI7152b holds is refused with AI7152c's message, the Fork's own current values excepted) and remake its `.app` (FA5980b), and the mark goes when the collision does. Neither action touches the Fork's data or the live folder (FK5977e). Tests (`ai7152e_`): a colliding Fork still resolves as the data folder and its `fork_app::make` succeeds; `fork_list` marks only the later of two colliding Forks and no Channel; rename and recolour rewrite only `fork.json` and the `.app`, drop the mark, and are refused when they collide anew; the sheet's list shows the mark and calls the seam once per action.

Policy: Mac observation is the user's. With Stable, Dev, Source and two Forks open, the Dock and ⌘-Tab show five entries that differ in name and plate, and a Fork made before this rule shows the mark and clears it after a rename. Policy, Reuse (rule 9): the case-insensitive comparison uses the standard library's or an already installed crate's Unicode case folding; the implementation PR carries the `Reuse:` section naming the candidates it considered.
