# UI: one flat tab style for Project tabs and Terminal tabs (U2231 to U2235)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview: `apps/desktop/src/header/`, `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`, the shared tab component in `apps/desktop/src/ui/`, `apps/desktop/src/styles.css`). Ids: U2231 to U2235. Terms: Project tab, Tab mark, Terminal, Run (`GLOSSARY.md`). Direction: #2210. The owner on the Project tabs: "the borders and design of the tabs is too annoying not elegant and not native". Shown four drafts (Chrome, Zed, VS Code, Safari compact), the owner picked Zed.

Policy: this scenario supersedes the bordered box and the grey selected ground of U440 and U441, the `--hairline` ruled header of U442, the box in U469's tab chips and the pill in `scenarios/ui-terminal-rename.md`'s scope line. Their other clauses stay acceptance: the Tab mark (U443), one centre line, the 24 px hit height, U382's clip, U183 and U511a's rename, U184's overflow edges, U469's one-Bubble control and U534. Webview only: no RPC method, contract or App seam command. The surfaces use existing Tokens (`docs/design-system.md`, Tokens) with no new colour literal; U441's scoped header Token table is replaced by the roles below, and the implementation run rewrites its rows in the same PR as the Contract recipe says (`.agents/builder.md`). Chromium shows the webview against the fake App (as U131 does), so the observers are rendered pages; the user reports what they meet on the Mac.

**U2231 one tab component.** Given the Project strip and the Terminal tab region (U469), then both render their tabs, their `+` and their close control with one component in `apps/desktop/src/ui/`, wrapping the Ark UI tabs part (rule 9: `@ark-ui/solid` is already the UI library); no stylesheet outside that component sets a tab's border, ground, radius or inactive text colour, and `header/` and `bubbles/` pass it only content, selection, a Tab mark and handlers. Asserted by `u2231_project_and_terminal_tabs_share_one_component` (both surfaces carry the component's hook and compute equal border, ground, radius and font for the same state) and `u2231_no_other_stylesheet_styles_a_tab` (`loop/rules.sh tokens` passes and no tab selector outside the component's stylesheet sets those four properties).

**U2232 flat bar, selected tab joins the page.** Given the strip with one, three and eight Projects and the Terminal region with one and with eight Runs, in light and dark, with increased contrast, at 1512 by 950, the one supported size (U453), then:

- **Flat.** The bar's ground is the window's secondary surface Token (`--sunken`); the bar, every tab, the `+` and the close control compute a `0` border radius. Asserted by `u2232_the_bar_is_flat_and_square`.
- **Selected.** The selected tab's ground is the page's surface Token (`--ground`), the ground of the content below it, and its computed border is 1 px `--hairline` on its left and right sides only, with no top or bottom border. The bar's bottom hairline (1 px `--hairline`) runs under every other tab and breaks under the selected one: a point at the selected tab's bottom edge has the page ground, not the hairline, and the tab's left and right borders meet the rule. With no Project open (U2), the rule runs under the `+`. Asserted by `u2232_the_selected_tab_joins_the_page` and `u2232_the_rule_breaks_only_under_the_selected_tab`.
- **Inactive.** An inactive tab's label is `--grey` on the bar's ground, with no border, background, box-shadow, pill or divider at rest, hover and press. Asserted by `u2232_inactive_tabs_have_no_box`.
- **Contrast.** Inactive labels keep 4.5:1 against the bar's ground and the selected label against `--ground` (D5), in both schemes. Asserted by `u2232_tab_text_keeps_contrast`.
- **Still.** Selecting or hovering a tab moves no tab, and the Tab mark, the close control, U382's clip and the 24 px hit height are unchanged (U112). Asserted by `u2232_hover_and_selection_move_no_tab`.

**U2233 hover, the plus and focus.** Given the same states, then:

- **Hover.** Hovering an inactive tab changes its label to `--text` and nothing else: no ground, border or shadow appears. The `+` is a plain icon (no box or ground at rest and hover) in `--grey` that takes `--text` on hover by the same rule. Asserted by `u2233_hover_moves_only_the_label_colour`.
- **Focus.** A tab, the `+` and the close control reached by keyboard show a `--accent` focus ring of at least 2 px that stays inside the bar's box (a clipped ring fails); a pointer click shows none (`:focus-visible` only). Asserted by `u2233_keyboard_focus_draws_a_ring_and_a_click_does_not`.
- **Checks.** D1 to D8, D10 hold for these controls with rest, hover, focus and pressed states (D6, D7); the only border is 1 px `--hairline` (D8). Asserted by `u2233_flat_tabs_pass_the_moved_checks`, a run of the design-system Checks over the strip and the Terminal region.

**U2234 rename stays inside the tab.** Given a Project tab (U183) or a Terminal tab (U511a) in rename, then the field sits inside the tab with the tab's own font family, size, weight and line height, has no border, outline offset, ground or radius of its own beyond the focus ring of U2233, and the tab keeps its box (width, height and place) as U183 says, in the selected and an inactive tab. Asserted by `u2234_a_rename_field_has_the_tab_type_and_no_border` for both surfaces.

**U2235 many Terminal tabs stay inside the centre pane.** Narrows U534 only in tab look; every clause of U534 and U469's Dense layout stays. Given the selected Agent with 8 Runs (seed `bubbles`) and an open Shelf, at 1512 by 950, the one supported size (U453), in light and dark, then every tab and every close control lies inside the region (`.terminal-tabs-host`), which ends before the Resource values and the Shelf; extra tabs scroll by wheel and by keyboard focus, each tab's full name stays its accessible name, shown by the shared Tooltip when cut (U477 allows no new `title=`), and the selected tab's joined look (U2232) holds while scrolled, including its left and right borders, which are not clipped by the region. Asserted by `u2235_flat_tabs_scroll_inside_the_pane_and_stay_reachable` (boxes, `document.elementFromPoint` on each tab and close control after scrolling) and `u2235_the_selected_tab_keeps_its_borders_while_scrolled`.

**Retirements and updates (policy).** The implementation run changes the checks that pinned the box or the pill, each naming the test that still proves its then-clause:
- `u440_a_tab_is_one_bordered_box` becomes U2232's `u2232_the_selected_tab_joins_the_page`; `u440_boxed_tabs_keep_passing_checks`, to `u2233_flat_tabs_pass_the_moved_checks`.
- `u441_hover_colours_the_whole_box`, `u441_dark_selection_is_no_brighter_than_the_rail` and `u441_painted_names_keep_both_insets` are retired with the box and the grey selected ground, replaced by `u2232_inactive_tabs_have_no_box` and `u2232_tab_text_keeps_contrast`; the `u441_token_*` tests are rewritten to the Token roles above.
- `u442_a_hairline_runs_under_the_header` and `u442_the_rule_shows_with_no_project` become `u2232_the_rule_breaks_only_under_the_selected_tab`.
- `u440_name_close_and_plus_share_a_centre_line`, `u440_a_tab_has_no_project_icon`, `u440_clip_overflow_and_rename_fit_the_box`, `u441_hover_and_selection_move_no_other_tab`, `u443_the_mark_is_a_small_dot_by_the_name`, `u441_tab_marks_use_root_ink`, and the `u469_` and `u534_` tests keep passing and are edited only where they assert a border, radius or ground of the old look.
