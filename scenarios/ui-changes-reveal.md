# UI: a fold's line count reveals the lines around a change (U7692a–U7692c)

Module: `apps/desktop/src/changes/` (`DiffReader.tsx`), the fake App and its fixtures. Ids: U7692a, U7692b, U7692c. Work order: [#7692](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7692), blocked by #7675. Owner, 2026-11-18, picked "Show hidden lines": the diff folds the unchanged lines between changes, and a reader checking a change often needs the lines around it. No Daemon method, Message or contract change, and no new read. The reader already passes both file bodies with its patch to `processFile` (`DiffReader.tsx`, `processFile(patch, { oldFile, newFile })`), so `@pierre/diffs` 1.6.1 holds the whole file and no switch from a patch to two bodies is needed. The implementation PR's `Reuse:` section names `@pierre/diffs` (its separator's own click, `expandHunk`, `revealLine`, `expansionLineCount`) and `registerKeys`, and says it adds no machinery. The reader sets no `hunkSeparators` or `expansionLineCount`, and records no reveal: the click and its reveal are the library's own. Where it leans on a library capability: Pierre's separator controls are not focusable (`div role=button`) and its custom separator is deprecated, so the keyboard path calls `expandHunk`, the library's public call, and builds no separator. Size: about 300 changed lines (reader 100, tests 200). Observer: the rendered reader in the isolated Chromium harness (U26) for the click and the count of U7692a, because the library draws its separator and handles the click itself and the jsdom tests of this view drive `VirtualizedFileDiff`'s callbacks and never read it (rule 10: no jsdom test fails for this break); the same browser test file for V in U7692b; Vitest against the fake App, with the mounted instance's `revealLine` spied (`vi.mock("@pierre/diffs", { spy: true })` as `u2540a_reader.test.tsx` does), for the keys list, the typing guard and a note's line; the capture for U7692c. Never a native Mac capture.

**Precedence.** A design check (the critic, a D-check) that reads a decision here as a defect changes how it is drawn, never the decision. When both cannot hold, `ask` (`flag:needs-user`).

**Words.** A fold is the run of unchanged lines the diff hides between two changes, before the first or after the last. The fold's line count is the library's own "N unmodified lines" text; the rendered test reads it from the shadow root of `diffs-container` as `u2540_rendered.test.ts` does, never layout or looks (rule 10).

**U7692a clicking a fold's line count reveals the lines around a change.** Given the rendered reader (U26 harness; a fixture whose text diff holds folds of 450, 150 and 3 unmodified lines) and the fake App, then:
- **Reveal.** Clicking a fold's count reveals up to 100 of its lines at each end, the library's `both` step (`expansionLineCount` defaults to 100; for the fold before the first change or after the last, one end is the file's edge), and the count shows what remains hidden. One click on the 450 fold reads "250 unmodified lines"; the 150 and 3 folds open whole and show no count. The library's other fold controls (its arrows, Expand all, Shift-click) stay as it draws them.
- **No new read.** The click makes no `agent.compareFile` call and no other Daemon call: the test counts `window.__fake.app.calls` before and after. Asserted, with Reveal, in `u7692a_a_click_on_the_count_reveals_100_lines_at_each_end_and_a_short_fold_opens_whole`, in `u7692_rendered.test.ts` beside `u2540_rendered.test.ts`.
- **Stay.** A file keeps its revealed lines while it stays the open file, through a note save, edit or remove and an equal refresh (U5646a, the Issue's own reading). A switch to another file folds it again, and so do Reload (U7675b), a changed comparison (a new `blob`, U7673a) and closing the view, which start a fresh reader: U7675d's scroll and line come back on a file switch, reveals do not, and the reader keeps no record to replay. Asserted in `u7692a_reveals_stay_across_a_note_and_a_refresh_and_fold_again_on_a_file_switch` (the file switch) and `u7692a_reload_folds_the_file_again` (the fake App answers the file with a new `blob`, then Reload), both in `u7692_rendered.test.ts`; a changed comparison and closing the view take the same fresh-reader path.
- **Kept.** A note made on a revealed line keeps its file, side and line (`u2540b_pointer_and_keyboard_select_lines`). The coarse fallback (U2540d, U5646c) has no fold and is untouched.

**U7692b the keyboard reveals, and a note's line is never hidden.** Given the diff holding focus (U7675e), then:
- **V.** V reveals the fold nearest the focused line, the first one after it, else the one before it, by the click's step; each further V reveals more of that fold until it is whole, then the next nearest. V on a file with no fold, or one shown by the coarse fallback, does nothing, and no V makes an `agent.compareFile` call. The focused line stays focused. Asserted in `u7692b_v_reveals_the_nearest_fold_by_the_click_step`, in the browser test file: the count falls by the click's step, and a file of the fixture with no fold does nothing.
- **Keys list, typing.** V is listed on the ? list (U7675e: N, V and R are not listed until the Issues that build them do; #7675 reserves them for those Issues, #7676 builds N, this order builds V, and no other open Issue names V). Asserted in `u7692b_the_keys_list_gains_v`. V never fires while a note field, text input or the editor holds focus: `u7675e_keys_do_not_fire_while_typing_in_a_note_field`, edited to press V (#7676 edits the same test for N, E and Delete; the build that comes second adds its keys). V is a plain key scoped to the focused diff, no App chord (U6171c).
- **A note's line is never hidden.** When a saved note's line lies inside a fold, as after Reload or reopening the view, the reader reveals it with the library's `revealLine`, which opens that line and up to 100 lines past it, from the nearer change. A note on an unchanged line is a new-side note, the side the library gives a context line in the unified diff the reader draws, so `revealLine`'s new-side number is the note's. Asserted in `u7692b_a_fold_holding_a_notes_line_is_revealed_with_reveal_line`.
- **No motion.** A reveal is instant (U7675e). Policy; the design critic judges it (U7692c).

**U7692c the rendered folds at 1512 by 950.** Given the U26 harness, dark App, dense fixture (`u2540.html?dense`), then a fold before and after a reveal draws, and D2, D5, D6 and every Check passing on `main` still pass. Run `just capture U7692c`; evidence under `artifacts/ux/U7692c/`, uncommitted. Policy; the design critic judges layout and looks, never a Vitest test (rule 10).
