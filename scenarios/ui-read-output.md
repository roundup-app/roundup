# UI: selecting an Agent or a Terminal shows its output unobstructed (U2224)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U2224. Found by the owner's goal (#2224): "switches tonight from the Solo app to roundup and wants their top five jobs as fast as in Solo", job 3, read a terminal's recent output. Solo selects a process with one chord and its output fills the pane. Narrows U164 and U165; changes no contract, App seam command or Token. Resizing a bubble and one-bubble-at-a-time belong to #2211 and are out of scope: this id holds whichever of the two model states is on `main`.

**U2224 one selection shows its output with nothing over it.** Given an open Project with an Agent selected or about to be, and one or more Terminal bubbles of its Workstream shown (U164), then:
- **Selecting an Agent uncovers its output.** Selecting an Agent row (a click, or U32's keys) minimises every bubble shown over the centre pane to its chip (U164's fold, the Terminal runs on), so no bubble overlaps the Agent's output or its Watching banner. It is one step: no second chord or click is needed to read. A minimised chip stays in the dock and restores as U164 says; a bubble the user restores after the selection stays shown until the next selection. Test: `u2224_selecting_an_agent_minimises_every_shown_bubble`, `u2224_a_bubble_restored_after_the_selection_stays_shown`.
- **Selecting a Terminal shows its output and nothing else over the pane.** Activating a Terminal's chip (a click, or U165's `⌘B` then Enter) restores that bubble and leaves no other bubble shown over it. Test: `u2224_activating_a_chip_shows_that_bubble_alone`.
- **Nothing else moves.** The selection, the focus rule of U165 (a bubble opened by the user takes focus; selecting an Agent moves focus as U40 says), the pane's size (U5: its size observer reports nothing), each Terminal's process and each bubble's place and size (U164) are unchanged by the fold; no `terminal.close`, `terminal.write` or `terminal.resize` is sent for it. A new Run's bubble (U164) still appears unfocused and does not fold on its own. Test: `u2224_the_fold_closes_nothing_writes_nothing_and_keeps_place_and_size`, `u2224_a_new_run_bubble_does_not_fold_on_its_own`.
- **Rendered.** Observer: the U26 harness, isolated agent-browser at 1280 by 800, light and dark (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), on the `bubbles` seed (U164) and its densest case: with a bubble shown at the pane's top left, one click on an Agent row leaves no bubble rectangle intersecting the pane's output region (`window.__checks()` reads zero overlap) and the first output line and the Watching banner are visible. A shot before and after goes to `artifacts/ux/U2224/`; the design critic judges D1, D2, D5, D6 and D8 on those shots (AGENTS.md rule 10). Moves: D6. Design-critic observer; the redundant browser fold assertion is retired under T3779c, with behavior proved by `u2224_selecting_an_agent_minimises_every_shown_bubble` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u2224_read_output.test.tsx`.
