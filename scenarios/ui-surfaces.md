**U3704 state rides with the name, apart from the actions.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Rule: a *state* tells what a thing is doing (a Kind glyph, the needs-you count, the folded Agents' glyphs, a plain Terminal tab's unseen-output dot); an *action* is what the user can do to it (`+ Agent`, clear, Retry, close, minimise, Collapse). State sits beside the name; actions group at the trailing edge, apart from state; state never looks or behaves like a control. Narrows every earlier scenario that places a state in a row's trailing group (the Workstream's Door glyph in `.row-tail`, U2340d's tab bar, U164's Bubble bar); their other clauses stand. Given the U26 seeds `tree-40`, `agents-10`, `door-stopped`, `tabs-8` and `bubbles` at 1512×950, light and dark for behavioral observers and dark only for captures and critic Check readings (U3725), with a Rail row of every Kind, a Workstream collapsed and expanded, a failed Door, the Shelf, the Terminal tab bar, the Bubble bar and the switcher open, then for every row, tab, bar and entry holding a state, in rest, hover and focus:

2. *With its name.* No action's box lies between a state's box and its name's, and the horizontal gap from the name's box to the state's is at most `var(--space-2)`.
3. *Apart.* A state's distance to the nearest action in that row is at least twice the gap between two adjacent actions in the same row (the row's own spacing when it has one action), and at least `var(--space-3)`.
4. *Actions trail.* Every action's box lies right of every state's box. Two exceptions: the leading disclosure chevron, and a native tick box whose value is the state. A Workstream's own state sits after its chevron, before its name, in the column its Agents' states take (same left edge, same size), so a Workstream's state and name line up with those of its Agents. 5. *Never a control.* A state has no `button` role, no tab stop (`tabindex` absent or `-1`, and not a button, link or input), computed `cursor` other than `pointer`, an unchanged `background-color` on hover, and a box whose width and height differ from the row's icon buttons' (24 px hit area) so a state never reads as one.
