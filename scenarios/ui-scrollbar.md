# UI: Scrollbars stay slim and show on hover (U3747)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Id: U3747. The owner's words (#3205): scrollbars are too annoying; they want a slim, light, elegant design that shows only on hover over its own scrolling element, never always visible.

Amended by U3530a–U3530c in `ui-scrollbar-overflow.md`: scrolling replaces hover as the visibility trigger in clauses 2 and 5 and their rendered observer; a fitting area shows no bar, and an overflowing area's bar fades after scrolling stops even with the pointer inside. Overlay reserves no gutter, including while hidden. The remaining appearance, access, hidden-bar and reuse clauses stand.

**U3747 one slim scrollbar that shows only over its own element.** Given any scrolling surface of the App (the Rail, the Shelf's lists, a Drawer, a Pad, Changes, a dialog, a menu, a select list and a Terminal's scrollback), then it draws one scrollbar look from one shared style or component, using only Tokens:

1. *Slim overlay.* The thumb is about 6 px wide, has no track and no arrows, and takes no layout width: an element's `clientWidth` and every row's box are the same with the bar shown and hidden, so content never shifts.
2. *Hover only.* At rest the bar is not drawn. While the pointer is over a scrolling element its own bar shows, and no other element's bar does (a Drawer over the Shelf shows one bar). Scrolling without the pointer over the element (keyboard, focus moves, a programmatic scroll) shows none. Leaving the element fades the bar out within about 300 ms; with `prefers-reduced-motion` it shows and hides with no fade.
3. *Still works.* Dragging the thumb, the wheel, a trackpad and keyboard scrolling scroll as before; the bar adds no tab stop and no accessible role.
4. *Reads in both appearances.* The thumb colour is a Token that is visible against `--ground` and `--sunken` in light and dark and is not an accent, amber or red.
5. *Terminals match.* A Terminal's scrollback (U34) shows the same thumb, hover rule and no layout width; the fit (U12, U13) still measures the full pane, so no extra column or row is lost and no `terminal.resize` is sent when the bar shows or hides.
6. *Hidden on purpose stays hidden.* The tab strip, the Bubbles row (`bubbles/styles.css`) and the Rail's hidden bar (`rail/styles.css`) keep no bar.
7. *Reuse (rule 9).* The PR's `Reuse:` section weighs OverlayScrollbars and SimpleBar against plain CSS (`scrollbar-width: thin` with `scrollbar-color`, `::-webkit-scrollbar`) with release evidence, and keeps what meets 1 to 3 on every surface at the smallest cost (rule 7).
