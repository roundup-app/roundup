# A Pad plays as a walk: one step per heading, the default view of every Pad (PW1 to PW13)

Module: `apps/desktop/src` (`pads`, `shelf`, `app`), `crates/agents` (Brief). Ids: PW1, PW2, PW3, PW4, PW5, PW6, PW7, PW8, PW9, PW10, PW11, PW12, PW13. Acceptance for [#7372](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7372), for PW1, PW3, PW8, PW9 and PW10 the redesign [#7571](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7571), and for PW11 to PW13 [#7945](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7945) (the owner, 2026-11-19, on the walk of PR #7382: "this view is awful and needs fixing and redesigning"; "the reading width needs to relax it's too narrow today and that makes it a bit unreadable"). Terms: Pad, Agent, Door, Terminal, Message, Bench (user label Shelf), link (`GLOSSARY.md`). Source: the owner, 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-26: "how does the user walk pads? there is no way or button? walk should be the default view, and the pad edit view is on edit pad"; "I don't have any way to view the pad as walk me through it we said that should be the default read view"; the owner, 2026-11-17: "roundup agents should be able to ... walk users through stories in the app shelf ... to ensure the user is comperhending"; "should be a simple as one click with clean view and animation and flow focus - interactive convenience"; at Finish, "Focus moves to the Door's terminal". No implementation is claimed by this specification.

Policy, Must not: no in-app browser or webview; no chat view written by roundup (a walk is not one); no model call opens a walk or chooses its path, only the user's click does; the walk adds no Daemon method and no `crates/contracts` type beyond what PW5's Message needs (a contract change follows the Contract recipe of `.agents/builder.md`). Steps change with `motion`, no hand-written state machine (rule 9). The outline is a plain list of the App's `ui/` buttons whose current step is the walk's one signal; `@ark-ui/solid` Steps is not installed and PR #7382 rejected it for its size, and `@ui-sdk/solid` ships no stepper, so no library is added. Each step is drawn by `PadRichEditor` with `readonly`, the Pad's one surface (rule 9), never a second renderer.

**PW1 a Pad splits into steps at its section level.** Given a Pad, when it plays as a walk, then the level is the shallowest heading level (`#` to `######`) the Pad uses at least twice, and each heading of that level with the text under it up to the next one is one step; one heading above that level at the very top is the Pad's title and makes no step; text before the first section is the first step, titled "Overview"; a Pad with no heading level used twice is one step; a heading inside a fenced code block never counts or splits. Asserted in `pw1_a_pad_splits_into_steps_at_its_section_level` beside the walk's step parser in `apps/desktop/src/pads/`, over a Pad of `##` sections under a `#` title, a Pad of `#` sections, a Pad with text before its first section, a Pad with no level used twice, and headings inside `` ``` `` and `~~~` fences.

**PW2 one click on a walk link opens step 1.** Amended by PW11: a walk link is one way a walk opens, and it alone opens at step 1 whatever PW4 saved. Given a Terminal, Inbox or Overview text holding `roundup://walk/<pad>?project=<key>` (LK1's form with `walk` as a sixth `<kind>`, whose id is a Pad's name), when the user plainly clicks it (AL3's rules for click, hover, drag and mouse reporting), then the Pad opens in the Bench as a walk at step 1 of its steps, with no further click; `link.read` of a walk link reads the Pad (LK2). A walk link of another Project, or of a missing Pad, shows AL4's not-found line and opens no walk. Asserted in `pw2_one_click_on_a_walk_link_opens_step_1` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx` and `pw2_a_walk_link_is_a_link_form` in `crates/rup/tests/e2e/identity.rs`.

**PW3 one key or click moves one step, with an animated change.** Given a walk at step N of M, when the user presses Right or clicks Next, then step N+1 shows, and when they press Left or click Back, step N−1 shows; Back at step 1 does nothing and Next at step M reads Finish and finishes (PW5). The change animates (`motion`), is skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and a key pressed mid-change shows its step at once. The steps indicator shows N of M. Next is a filled primary button; Back, the ← → key hints and Next sit in a bar under the step. Asserted in `pw3_keys_and_buttons_move_one_step` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`; the look and the motion are the design critic's.

**PW4 the walk keeps its step per Pad across restarts.** Given a walk of Pad `notes` at step 3 and one of Pad `plan` at step 2, when the App restarts and the user opens either, then each opens at its own step; a step beyond the Pad's current step count opens at the last step; Finish clears the Pad's step. Asserted in `pw4_the_step_survives_a_restart_per_pad` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`, over the App's existing persisted-state store (no new store).

**PW5 Finish focuses the sender's Terminal and sends one Message.** Given a walk opened by a link in the Terminal of Agent A, when the user finishes it (Next at the last step, or Enter there), then focus moves to A's Terminal and A gets exactly one Message that the user finished the walk of the Pad, naming the Pad by its link; finishing again after reopening sends one more, never two for one finish. Given a walk opened with no link (PW11: from the Bench, the Pad list or a `roundup://pad/` link), when the user finishes it, then focus returns to where it was before the walk opened, no Message is sent, and the Pad stays open as a walk at step 1 (the step is cleared as PW4 says). Given the sender is the Door, A is the Door and the focus goes to the Door's Terminal. A sender that no longer exists is PW7. Asserted in `pw5_finish_focuses_the_senders_terminal_and_sends_one_message` and `pw5_a_walk_opened_without_a_link_returns_focus_and_sends_nothing` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`.

**PW6 the walk is read-only and the Brief hands out its link.** Given a walk, when it shows, then the Pad is read-only: no answer box, no editor and no Message is sent but PW5's. Given the Brief of the Door and of an ordinary Agent, when built, then it says that where it would send a Pad's sections one by one, it gives the Pad's walk link, `roundup://walk/<pad>?project=<key>` built from the Pad's `link` (AL7's reply field) with `pad` replaced by `walk`, never made up; AL7's `link` field stays the Pad's. Asserted in `pw6_the_walk_has_no_answer_box` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx` and `pw6_brief_says_to_hand_a_walk_link` in `crates/agents/tests/launch/guidance.rs` for both roles, pinning the instruction token. Design critic: the walk passes at 1512×950 in dark mode (observer: the design-critic run).

**PW7 Finish with the sender gone returns focus and sends nothing.** Given a walk opened by a link in the Terminal of Agent A, when A no longer exists (removed or closed) and the user finishes the walk, then focus returns to where it was before the walk opened, no Message is sent to any Agent, and the walk's step is cleared as at any Finish (PW4). Asserted in `pw7_finish_with_the_sender_gone_returns_focus_and_sends_nothing` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`, in the App's existing test harness with no browser or Daemon started; if the walk sends or errors when the sender is gone, the same PR fixes it. Acceptance for [#7397](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7397).

**PW8 an outline lists every step and opens one on a click.** Given a walk of M steps at step N, when it shows, then a column on the left lists the Pad's name and every step's title in order, each step before N marked done, step N marked current (`aria-current="step"`) and numbered, and a click on a title shows that step (PW3's change, PW4's saved step). The step's title and body sit on the right. When the outline and the step column do not fit side by side (a Narrow column in a narrow pane), the outline folds into one row above the step reading "Step N of M · title" that opens the list, and a click on a title in it shows that step and closes it. Asserted in `pw8_the_outline_lists_every_step_and_opens_one` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx` (roles, `aria-current`, the opened step, and the folded row when the pane is narrow); the look is the design critic's.

**PW9 each step is drawn as the Pad draws it, at the Pad's width.** Given a walk, when a step shows, then its body is drawn by `PadRichEditor` with `readonly` (the Pad's `.reading.pad-body` surface), so a callout (`[!NOTE]`), a ruled table, a list with its numbers, colour, highlight, a Mermaid block and a drawing look as in the Pad and nothing is editable (PW6), and the step column's width follows the Pad Width setting (Narrow, Wide, Full; default Wide), not a fixed column. The Pad view and `plain/Markdown` do not change. Asserted in `pw9_a_step_is_drawn_by_the_pads_readonly_editor_at_the_pad_width` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`: a step holding a callout, a table and a numbered list mounts the read-only editor with those blocks and no editable surface, and each Pad Width value sets the column's width class; the looks are the design critic's.

**PW10 a long step scrolls and fades at the bottom while more is below.** Given a step taller than the walk, when it shows, then the step scrolls inside the walk, a fade shows at its bottom edge while more lies below and is gone at the end of the scroll, and a step that fits shows no fade. Asserted in `pw10_a_long_step_scrolls_and_fades_while_more_is_below` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx` (the fade's state follows the scroll position, with the scroll metrics set on the element); the look is the design critic's. Design critic: the walk passes at 1512×950 in dark mode (observer: the design-critic run).

**PW11 opening a Pad shows its walk.** Given a Pad with text, when the user opens it from the Bench, the Pad list or a `roundup://pad/<pad>` link, then it shows as its walk (PW8's outline, PW9's renderer and Pad Width), read-only (PW6), at the step PW4 saved or step 1, with no editor mounted; a Pad with no heading level used twice reads as one step (PW1). A `roundup://pad/<pad>#<heading>` link opens the walk at the step that holds that heading, and a `roundup://walk/` link opens step 1 (PW2). Asserted in `pw11_opening_a_pad_shows_its_walk_and_no_editor` and `pw11_a_pad_link_with_a_heading_opens_the_step_that_holds_it` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`, over the fake App, for each of the three ways to open. Policy: no setting turns the walk off, and no second read-only renderer is added beside the walk's (PW9).

**PW12 Edit shows the editor, Done returns to the walk.** Given a Pad shown as a walk, then its bar holds Edit, and export, Changes and delete (the Pad's actions of today) stay beside the Pad's name; rename is in Edit only. When the user presses Edit, or `E` while focus is in the walk and no text field holds it, then today's editor shows (autosave, drafts and undo as U2540 and the Pad clauses say) with Done in Edit's place. When the user presses Done, then what the editor holds is saved first (`pad.write` as in Edit today), and only once it is saved the walk shows the Pad's new text at the step that holds the caret's heading, or the step it left when the caret is above every heading; a save that fails keeps the editor open with today's failure line. Asserted in `pw12_edit_shows_the_editor` and `pw12_done_saves_then_shows_the_walk_at_the_carets_step` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx` (the fake App sees one `pad.write` before the walk shows); the placement and look are the design critic's. Design critic: a Pad opened from the Pad list as its walk, and the same Pad in Edit, pass at 1512×950 in dark mode (U26's capture; observer: the design-critic run).

**PW13 an empty or kept-draft Pad opens in Edit.** Given a Pad whose text is empty (a new Pad), when it opens, then it shows the editor (PW12's Edit), not a walk. Given a Pad with a kept draft (unsaved text restored after a restart), when it opens, then it shows the editor holding that draft, so the user sees it, and Done then follows PW12. A Pad with text and no draft opens as a walk (PW11). Asserted in `pw13_a_new_empty_pad_opens_in_edit` and `pw13_a_pad_with_a_kept_draft_opens_in_edit` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.tsx`.
