# A Pad plays as a walk: one step per heading, one click to open (PW1 to PW6)

Module: `apps/desktop/src` (`pads`, `shelf`, `app`), `crates/agents` (Brief). Ids: PW1, PW2, PW3, PW4, PW5, PW6. Acceptance for [#7372](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7372). Terms: Pad, Agent, Door, Terminal, Message, Bench (user label Shelf), link (`GLOSSARY.md`). Source: the owner, 2026-11-17: "roundup agents should be able to ... walk users through stories in the app shelf ... to ensure the user is comperhending"; "should be a simple as one click with clean view and animation and flow focus - interactive convienience"; at Finish, "Focus moves to the Door's terminal". No implementation is claimed by this specification.

Policy, Must not: no in-app browser or webview; no chat view written by roundup (a walk is not one); no model call opens a walk or chooses its path, only the user's click does; the walk adds no Daemon method and no `crates/contracts` type beyond what PW5's Message needs (a contract change follows the Contract recipe of `.agents/builder.md`). Steps change with `@ark-ui/solid` steps or tour and `motion`, no hand-written state machine (rule 9).

**PW1 a Pad splits into steps by its top-level headings.** Given a Pad, when it plays as a walk, then each top-level (`#`) heading with the text under it up to the next top-level heading is one step, text before the first heading belongs to step 1, and a Pad with no top-level heading is one step. Headings inside a fenced code block do not split. Asserted in `pw1_a_pad_splits_into_steps_by_top_level_heading` beside the walk's step parser in `apps/desktop/src/pads/`.

**PW2 one click on a walk link opens step 1.** Given a Terminal, Inbox or Overview text holding `roundup://walk/<pad>?project=<key>` (LK1's form with `walk` as a sixth `<kind>`, whose id is a Pad's name), when the user plainly clicks it (AL3's rules for click, hover, drag and mouse reporting), then the Pad opens in the Bench as a walk at step 1 of its steps, with no further click; `link.read` of a walk link reads the Pad (LK2). A walk link of another Project, or of a missing Pad, shows AL4's not-found line and opens no walk. Asserted in `pw2_one_click_on_a_walk_link_opens_step_1` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.ts` and `pw2_a_walk_link_is_a_link_form` in `crates/rup/tests/e2e/notes.rs`.

**PW3 one key or click moves one step, with an animated change.** Given a walk at step N of M, when the user presses Right or clicks Next, then step N+1 shows, and when they press Left or click Back, step N−1 shows; Back at step 1 and Next at step M do nothing (Next at step M reads Finish, PW5). The change animates (`motion`), is skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and a key pressed mid-change shows its step at once. The steps indicator shows N of M. Asserted in `pw3_keys_and_buttons_move_one_step` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.ts`; the look and the motion are the design critic's.

**PW4 the walk keeps its step per Pad across restarts.** Given a walk of Pad `notes` at step 3 and one of Pad `plan` at step 2, when the App restarts and the user opens either, then each opens at its own step; a step beyond the Pad's current step count opens at the last step; Finish clears the Pad's step. Asserted in `pw4_the_step_survives_a_restart_per_pad` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.ts`, over the App's existing persisted-state store (no new store).

**PW5 Finish focuses the sender's Terminal and sends one Message.** Given a walk opened by a link in the Terminal of Agent A, when the user finishes it (Next at the last step, or Enter there), then focus moves to A's Terminal and A gets exactly one Message that the user finished the walk of the Pad, naming the Pad by its link; finishing again after reopening sends one more, never two for one finish. Given a walk opened with no link (from the Bench or the Pad list), when the user finishes it, then focus returns to where it was before the walk opened and no Message is sent. Given the sender is the Door, A is the Door and the focus goes to the Door's Terminal. If the sender no longer exists, focus returns where it was and no Message is sent. Asserted in `pw5_finish_focuses_the_senders_terminal_and_sends_one_message` and `pw5_a_walk_opened_without_a_link_returns_focus_and_sends_nothing` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.ts`.

**PW6 the walk is read-only and the Brief hands out its link.** Given a walk, when it shows, then the Pad is read-only: no answer box, no editor and no Message is sent but PW5's. Given the Brief of the Door and of an ordinary Agent, when built, then it says that where it would send a Pad's sections one by one, it gives the Pad's walk link, `roundup://walk/<pad>?project=<key>` built from the Pad's `link` (AL7's reply field) with `pad` replaced by `walk`, never made up; AL7's `link` field stays the Pad's. Asserted in `pw6_the_walk_has_no_answer_box` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/pw_browser.test.ts` and `pw6_brief_says_to_hand_a_walk_link` in `crates/agents/tests/launch/guidance.rs` for both roles, pinning the instruction token. Design critic: the walk passes at 1512×950 in dark mode (observer: the design-critic run).
