**U5644a tree and group facts occupy linear space.** Given one owner list containing a chain of 2,000 Todos, each after the first naming its predecessor as its first blocker, when the production tree, collection and group derivations run on the accepted list and again after its last open descendant completes, then:

- One preorder row array holds each Todo once, with parallel parent-index and exclusive subtree-end columns. A root has no parent index. Each descendant group is the half-open range immediately after its parent through that parent's subtree end; the parent itself is excluded. The chain has 2,000 rows and 1,999 nonempty group ranges. The production retention observer asserts these counts and that no per-group descendant arrays are retained. Test: planned `u5644a_chain_retains_linear_topology_and_group_facts` in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3708_nesting.test.tsx`.
- Current topology, child-range metadata, Ark collection nodes, group aggregates, disclosure choices and retained previous animation facts together hold at most `64 * N` entries for this single-scope fixture at rest and while completing the last child. Count each scalar/reference slot in arrays, object records, sets and maps, including nested containers, rather than counting a container as one; do not traverse the original Todo payloads, Solid/Ark framework internals or DOM. A retained row reference counts even if the same object occurs elsewhere. Previous facts may hold scalar done/blocked/count/visibility information and pending identities; they may not duplicate every group's descendant rows or ids. Test: `u5644a_chain_retains_linear_topology_and_group_facts`.
- Building the owner-list topology, application-owned child structure supplied to Ark, and all groups' completion/visibility facts uses at most `64 * (N + B)` row/parent/blocker/child-slot inspections per accepted revision; `B` is the number of blocker ids in that list, including missing ids. Record inspections at the production reads, including copies and nested iterations, not merely helper invocations. Include a wide tree with one parent and 1,999 children to catch sibling-array copying. No all-row scan for each parent or subtree slice for each group remains in construction or in the child enumeration used by `Todos.tsx`. This count excludes Ark internals, rendering, and the separate blocker menus. Test: planned `u5644a_chain_and_wide_tree_build_without_repeated_scans` in the same file.

Given small chains, a wide tree, multiple roots, secondary blockers, missing/out-of-owner blockers, done parents/children and stale cycles, when topology and Ark child enumeration are read, then the preorder `(id, parent, level)` triples and collection child ids match the current behavior: choose the first in-list blocker other than self, keep sibling/root T10 order, keep done members, and emit every row unreachable from a root once at level 1 in input order, including rows downstream of a rootless cycle. Parent-index/subtree-end facts agree with that output. Test: planned table-driven `u5644a_topology_preserves_order_membership_and_cycle_fallback` in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3708_nesting.test.tsx`, with explicit expected outputs established from main, not a second implementation of the optimized algorithm.

Policy: completion totals, first-open-descendant and visibility facts are folded over the topology. Public consumers read ranges/aggregates or enumerate actual immediate children, rather than reconstructing descendant-slice maps. Previous facts remain a genuine previous accepted state: mutating or replacing the current input must not silently make a comparison compare the current state with itself. No history of whole list revisions is retained for these derivations.

Reuse `u3809_membership_counts_each_descendant_once_across_completion`, `u3809_group_and_scope_progress_have_distinct_membership` and `u3809_ticking_a_parent_keeps_its_dependency_group` in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3708_nesting.test.tsx`; extend their fixtures only for a missing clause.

- Final-child completion keeps visible finishing rows until their existing effects settle, closes the completed group, and opens at most the first later newly unblocked incomplete group per owner. Hidden updates settle without replay or opening an ancestor. Reopening preserves explicit choices. No completion, reopen or blocker RPC is caused by disclosure. Reduced motion settles without waiting for an animation event. Tests: existing `u3809_disclosure_toggles_only_its_inert_contents`, `u3811_final_child_keeps_its_finish_and_repairs_only_hidden_focus`, `u3811_an_agent_finish_keeps_focus_outside_the_closing_group` and `u3811_reduced_motion_settles_groups_without_waiting_for_motion` in the same file; existing `u3770_a_preference_change_mid_motion_settles_it` in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3768_progress.test.tsx`.

**U5644c both blocker menus share adjacency and bounded walks.** Given a selected Todo and an accepted Todo list, when its two relation menus are prepared and opened, then one forward/reverse adjacency build serves that list revision and at most one walk in each direction starts from the selection. Each walk expands each reachable known vertex at most once and examines each outgoing adjacency entry at most once in its direction, even with cycles or converging paths. Candidate filtering is one pass per menu, with at most `8 * (N + E)` total candidate/direct-edge membership probes for both; `E` counts every blocker-id occurrence in the list, including missing ids. Candidate sorting is outside this linear bound. No per-candidate graph map or visited set remains. Test: planned `u5644c_both_menus_bound_graph_work` in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3728_blocker_menu.test.tsx`, exercising the production preparation through `TodoMeta` and counting reads/visits across mounting and opening both menus, so eager work is included.

Run that cost observer on chains of 80, 160 and 2,000 Todos, a 2,000-Todo wide graph, a converging diamond, a stale cycle and missing blocker ids. Count adjacency builds and blocker-id reads separately: at most one build and `2 * (N + E)` source blocker-list/id reads per list revision, independent of the number of candidates. Reopening or typing a menu query on the same revision performs no new adjacency build or reachability walk. Selecting a different Todo recomputes reachability for that selection without rebuilding the same list's adjacency. After accepted list replacement changes an edge or removes a Todo, one new adjacency build uses the new list and candidates immediately reflect it; old reachability must not survive. Test: `u5644c_both_menus_bound_graph_work`, using large fixtures at the production calculation seam and small mounted fixtures for opening, filtering, selection and refetch; do not mount 2,000 DOM rows to observe an algorithm's work.

Given deliberately non-id input order, a done Todo, an existing direct edge, secondary blockers, an unrelated stale cycle, a cycle containing the selection and missing blocker ids, when both menus' related and offered candidates are read, then their ids and order match main:

- “Blocked by” related rows are its known direct blockers, including done ones, sorted by id; offered rows exclude self, existing direct blockers and any Todo transitively waiting on the selection, and are sorted by id.
- “Blocking” related rows are Todos directly naming the selection as blocker, including done ones, sorted by id; offered rows exclude self, existing direct dependents and any Todo the selection transitively waits on, and retain input Todo order. Related rows precede offered rows in both menus. A missing id supplies no row and does not stop traversal of other edges; done rows still participate in cycle prevention.

Test: planned table-driven `u5644c_candidates_match_both_existing_directions` in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3728_blocker_menu.test.tsx`, with explicit expected ids/order for chains, wide graphs, diamonds, cycles and missing blockers. Keep existing `u3515c_blockers_exclude_self_existing_and_transitive_cycles` and `u3728_blocker_menu_filters_picks_and_escapes`; extend the latter to both directions to assert keyboard/pointer selection sends exactly the existing `todo.setBlockers` target and edge update, Escape sends none, and focus returns as before. A related-row activation still removes that direct edge. Test: planned `u5644c_both_menu_directions_keep_edge_calls_and_focus` in that file, sharing the existing harness rather than duplicating it.
