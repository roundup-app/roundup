# UI: a Todo's title and body edit in place (U445)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview: `apps/desktop/src/todos`). Ids: U445.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method, no `contracts/` file and no Token changes. Found by the user's report #1204: "editing a todo title or body should happen in place without layout/font/size change". On `main` `todos/EditableText.tsx` swaps the shown `<p>` for a bare `<input>` (title) or `<textarea rows={6}>` (body) that no style sets, so the webview draws its own font, size, border, blue ring, a width of about 20 characters and a fixed six rows. Narrows U17 (double-click edits), U139 (Tab and Enter edit) and U133 (the focus ring of a title and body). #1103 later redesigns the Drawer around this field; the field's look stays in `EditableText`'s own styles.

**U445 a Todo's title or body is edited where it is shown.** Given a Todo's Drawer (U17) on `tree-40` and a Todo with a 300-character title and a 40-line body (U359's worst case), light and dark for behavioral observers (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), at 1280 by 800, when the user double-clicks the title, or the body, or focuses it by Tab and presses Enter (U139), then:

- **Same box and type.** The edit field's bounding box (x, y, width, height) is within 1 px of the shown text's box before the edit, and its computed `font-family`, `font-size`, `font-weight`, `line-height`, `letter-spacing`, `color`, `text-align`, `white-space`, `overflow-wrap`, padding, margin and border widths equal the shown text's. Nothing else in the Drawer moves: every other element's box is unchanged, measured before and during the edit. The design critic judges these matches and boxes (AGENTS.md rule 10).
- **Wrap.** A long title wraps in the field on the lines the shown title wraps on, and the field has no horizontal or vertical scrollbar. Enter in the title commits and inserts no line break (U17, U139). Test: `u445_a_long_title_wraps_as_shown` (Enter and pasted line breaks become spaces); the design critic judges the wrap and scrollbars (AGENTS.md rule 10).
- **Grows with its text.** The body field's height equals its text's height at every keystroke: typing a new line grows it by one line height, deleting one shrinks it, and it never scrolls inside itself. An empty body is as tall as the shown `no body` line (`min-height: 1.4em`). In the body, Enter adds a line (U139). The design critic judges these heights (AGENTS.md rule 10).
- **One quiet focus mark.** The field shows a caret in `--accent` and a `--hover` wash over its own box, drawn from Tokens, with no border, no outline and no shadow and no change to any box size. The blue ring of the webview goes. U133's 2 px ring and D6/D7 do not apply to this field while it is edited, as they skip a `textarea` (U353); the shown text keeps U133's ring on Tab focus. The wash has D5's 4.5:1 for the text over it in dark. The design critic judges the wash, ring and boxes (AGENTS.md rule 10).
- **Kept.** Leaving the field commits once and only when the text differs from where it started; a failed commit reopens the field with the typed text, in the same place (U17, U152). Each field keeps its accessible name `title` or `body`. Tests: the `u17_`, `u139_` and `u152_` cases for editing, run unchanged.
