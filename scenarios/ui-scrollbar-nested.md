# UI: Nested scrollers show one bar, and a bar takes no width (U3748, U3749)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview), `src/scrollbar.css`. Ids: U3748, U3749. Work order: [#3220](https://github.com/saadshahd/roundup/issues/3220). These narrow check 1 and check 2 of U3747 in `ui-scrollbar.md`, which stand as written.

**U3748 hovering a nested scroller shows only its own bar.** Given a scrolling element inside another scrolling element (a list inside a scrolling pane, the Shelf's lists, a Drawer over the Shelf), when the pointer is over the inner one, then the inner element's bar is drawn and the outer ancestor's is not. When the pointer is over the outer element outside the inner one, then the outer bar is drawn and the inner is not. The hover rule matches the element under the pointer, not its ancestors. Test: `u3748_hover_shows_only_the_innermost_scroller_bar` (agent-browser in the U26 harness at 1280 by 800, `tree-40` and `agents-10` with a Drawer open, dark and light: hover the inner scroller, then the outer, and read the computed `scrollbar-color` of both; policy for jsdom, which computes no hover, AGENTS.md rule 10). Moves: D2, D5, D6.

**U3749 a bar takes no layout width on any platform.** Given a scrolling element on a platform with classic scrollbars (Chromium on Linux or Windows, a Mac with a mouse or "always show scrollbars"), then its `clientWidth` and every row's box are the same with the bar shown and hidden, so U112 and U154 measurements do not move. Where the thin transparent bar still reserves width, `src/scrollbar.css` takes that width out of layout. Test: `u3749_client_width_is_equal_with_and_without_hover` (agent-browser in the U26 harness, `tree-40` and `agents-10` with a Drawer open: read `clientWidth` of the Rail, the Drawer and the Shelf's lists before and during hover, with classic scrollbars forced). Regression: U112, U154, U3747. Moves: D2, D5, D6.
