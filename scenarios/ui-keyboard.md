# UI: the keyboard reaches everything (U138 to U141, U358)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U138 to U141, U358. Found by ux-auditor-1's keyboard sweep of main (harness `tree-40`).

**U138 completing a Todo by keyboard keeps focus.** Given an open Todo row whose `complete` has focus (U35, reached by Tab from the row), when the user presses Enter or Space on it and the Todo leaves the open list, then focus moves to the row that took its place: the next open Todo's row, else the previous one, else the `+` button of the `todos` list. Focus is never left on the page body. A failed `todo.complete` keeps focus on `complete`. Tests (`u138_`): the middle, the last and the only open Todo each, and the failure case.

**U139 a Todo's title and body edit by keyboard.** Given a Todo's Drawer (U17), then its title and its body are each reachable by Tab, and Enter on the focused text starts editing it exactly as a double-click does (U17: the field takes focus, leaving it commits once). The title's own Enter still commits (U17); in the body field Enter adds a line and leaving commits, as with the pointer. Policy: the new tab stop draws no focus ring, and editing retains its caret (U3528). Tests (`u139_`): the title and the body are tab stops, and Enter on each opens its field with focus in it.

**U140 F2 renames the focused row.** Narrows U41 ("`F2` edits the selected name"). Found: after `↓` moves focus from the selected row to another, F2 renamed the selected row, which the user was no longer looking at. Given focus on a Rail row, then `F2` edits the name of the focused row in place, as double-click does (U9), whether or not that row is selected; with a click, focus and selection are the same row, so nothing changes there. With focus on a row's `Collapse` or `promote` button, or in the pane, `F2` does nothing to the Rail, as U41 says. `u41_f2_edits_the_selected_rows_name_even_when_a_different_row_has_focus` is replaced by `u140_f2_edits_the_focused_rows_name_even_when_another_row_is_selected`, and `u41_f2_does_nothing_with_no_selection` by `u140_f2_edits_the_focused_row_when_nothing_is_selected`; `u41_shift_f2_does_nothing` and the other F2 tests keep passing as written.

**U141 retired.** Policy: the append-only UI is gone; all Pads use U147 editing and U36 draft/conflict rules. Retain ordinary incoming-change scroll behavior under U20.


**U358 ⌘⌫ removes the selected Rail row.** Narrows U32 ("a ⌘ chord … never reaches a Terminal") for this one chord, and extends U41 and U54. Given an open Project with focus in the Rail (U41), when the user presses `⌘⌫`, then the selected row is removed by the path of the row menu's `remove` (A16, `rail/menu/model.ts`): one `rail.remove {id}`, with the same confirm when the menu's `needsConfirmation` holds (a running Terminal, or an Agent that is `working`, `needs-you` or `blocked`), where `Enter` removes and `Escape` cancels and calls nothing. With nothing selected the chord does nothing. After a removal the selection moves to the next row, else to the previous row when the removed one was last, and focus stays in the Rail, so a second `⌘⌫` removes that row. A failed `rail.remove` shows the menu's `✕ <message>` line and the row stays (A16). With focus in the pane, a Terminal bubble, an open Drawer, Help or the switcher, `⌘⌫` removes nothing and a Terminal still receives it as the shell's delete-to-line-start (`quick-terminal.md` closes a bubble with `⇧⌘⌫`); `⇧⌘⌫`, `⌃⌫`, `⌥⌫` and plain `⌫` do not remove a row, and Caps Lock does not change it. A held chord acts once, as U32 says: a key repeat (`event.repeat`) calls nothing, is still handled by the webview, and the next press after release acts as new. U54's Help lists `⌘⌫  Remove` under the Rail keys, after `⌘2  Focus the pane`, and its drift test covers it. Tests, in `apps/desktop/src/rail/u358_cmd_backspace_removes.test.tsx` against the fake App on `tree-40`: `u358_cmd_backspace_removes_the_selected_idle_agent_and_selects_the_next_row`, `u358_cmd_backspace_on_a_working_agent_confirms_first_and_escape_cancels`, `u358_cmd_backspace_on_the_last_row_selects_the_previous_row`, `u358_cmd_backspace_with_focus_in_the_pane_calls_nothing`, `u358_a_key_repeat_of_cmd_backspace_calls_nothing_more`, `u358_a_failed_remove_shows_the_error_line_and_keeps_the_row`, `u358_other_modifier_sets_remove_nothing`, `u358_help_lists_cmd_backspace_remove_under_the_rail_keys`.
