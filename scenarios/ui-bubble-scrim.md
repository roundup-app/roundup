# UI: A shown Bubble dims the centre only

Acceptance for #3278, from the user's report: "The terminal when opens covers whole screen - it should cover the center only not the rail nor the sidebar". Module: `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`. Ids: U3761–U3763. Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method, nothing under `contracts/`, no new Token. Narrows U2892a for the Bubble's own backdrop; every dialog and the Shelf Scrim keep U2892a's whole-window backdrop, and U2892b, U2892c and U2237 stay.

**U3761 a Bubble dims the centre, not the Rail or the Shelf.** Given the actual App with an open Project, the Rail and the Shelf shown and a Bubble holding focus, when the Bubble's dim and blur show, then they cover the centre column and nothing else: the Rail and the Shelf are undimmed and unblurred, and stay clickable, scrollable and keyboard-reachable while the Bubble is shown; the Terminal bar at the centre's foot (the Terminal tabs, `+ Terminal`, the memory and CPU figures) is App chrome and stays undimmed, unblurred and usable, so one click on another Terminal's tab shows that Terminal and `+ Terminal` opens a new one, and no frame, even after a fill, overlaps the bar. Policy: the native `::backdrop` of the top-layer frame always spans the window, so the dim is drawn in the centre column's own layer instead; no timer and no second backdrop state, and reduced transparency keeps the dim and drops the blur as U2892a says. Observer: the design critic on `just capture U3761` at 1512×950, dark, `tree-40` with dense Terminal output, comparing the Rail and Shelf pixels with the Bubble hidden and shown, with failure text visible on the densest seed (no test asserts colour or geometry, rule 10).

**U3762 the Bubble keeps working at the centre's size.** Given U3761's state, when the user types into the Bubble, resizes it or fills it by double-click, then input reaches the Terminal, `terminal.resize` is called once per frame with the Bubble's fitted size within the centre pane and never the window's, and a second double-click restores the prior placement. Tests: the existing `u164_a_resize_calls_terminal_resize_once_per_frame` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u164_bubbles.test.tsx`, `u2237_a_second_double_click_restores_the_last_size` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u2236_resize.test.tsx` and `u2237_fill_and_restore_refit_once` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u2236_browser.test.ts`, retained unchanged.

**U3763 closing returns the centre.** Given U3761's state, when the user closes the Bubble, or presses outside it so it minimises, then the centre shows what it showed before the Bubble opened, with no dim left, and focus returns as U2892c says; a press on a Rail row or a Shelf item while the Bubble is shown reaches that row or item (U2307a). Tests: the existing `u2307a_an_outside_press_minimises_and_still_reaches_its_target` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/u2307_panel.test.tsx`, extended with a Rail row as a target; the dim's removal is the U3761 capture of the closed state.
