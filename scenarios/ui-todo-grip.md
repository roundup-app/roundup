# UI: Todo grips share the header edge

Acceptance for Issue #4710, Decision 6 of the Pad "Todo list design". Module: `apps/desktop` (webview), after U4707 (`ui-row-inset.md`) and U4709a–c (`ui-rows-state.md`). Journey stages: Understand/Review — read the scoped work, move a Todo and reopen that same item. This specification delivers acceptance, not the implementation; #4710 stays open.

**U4710a the persistent grip ends under the header close button.** Policy — rendered acceptance: given the Drawer's Todos and Pads lists with root/child/grandchild Todos, open/blocked/done rows, a 300-character Todo title, a 120-character Pad name, ten blockers and a 60-character group name, when viewed in dark at 1280×820 and 1512×950, then:

- The right edge of every draggable Todo's grip hit area aligns with the header close button's right edge within 1 CSS px, 16 CSS px inside the panel's right edge. Compare hit-area border boxes, not the grip SVG's narrower painted strokes. Tree depth, title length, hover, focus and selection do not move that column.
- The Todo list has no extra 32px trailing strip reserved for sideways nesting; remove that reservation from `.todo-list` and its add-row counterpart. Ordinary panel and control spacing uses existing Tokens only. Keep U4707's leading inset and dependency elbows.
- Todo and Pad age right edges agree within 1 CSS px across rows of each list, including different depths and age widths. The ages and grips use the same trailing alignment rule: the age column has a fixed Token-derived offset before the Open/grip controls, rather than shifting with the title or depth. U4709c's age-before-Open order remains; age text and grip hit areas never overlap. Open stays before the rightmost grip.
- Retain the persistent grip and Todo Open arrow, completion/disclosure access, 32px rows on a 24px line, one-line ellipsis and live updates. A non-draggable row gains no inert grip. Keep priority and other metadata in the opened Todo; no body excerpt or tag enters the row. Solo's header is outside this change.

Observer (policy): the design critic compares same-seed main/head captures at both viewports, at rest, hover, keyboard focus, selection and during a drag. Record panel, close-button, grip and age rectangles with row identity/depth and resolved Tokens. Geometry and exact appearance belong to this observer, never UI test assertions (AGENTS.md rule 10).

**U4710b horizontal drag distance selects nesting without a free strip.** Given U4710a's trailing grips and U3708's dependency tree, when a pointer drag activates through the installed shared Sortable, then let `S` be the resolved `--tree-step` used by that tree, `dx` the current pointer x minus its pointer-down x, and `d` the dragged row's starting depth (root = 0). The requested depth is `d + round(dx / S)`, clamped to the legal depths at the destination gap. It depends on distance from lift, never the pointer's absolute x, row width or trailing padding. Keep the existing nearest-step rounding and legal-parent/descendant exclusions. A vertical-only drag keeps its starting requested depth; the destination gap may still clamp it.

Extend the existing real-pointer observers in `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3710_browser.test.ts` retaining their scenario ids and prefixing the extended test names with `u4710b_` (for example `u4710b_u3710_a_sideways_drag_calls_set_blockers_and_a_vertical_one_calls_reorder`):

- `u3710_a_sideways_drag_calls_set_blockers_and_a_vertical_one_calls_reorder`: at the same sibling gap, `dx = +S` nests under the preceding eligible Todo and `dx = -S` un-nests a child, with exactly one `todo.setBlockers` call and the exact resulting blockers; `dx = 0` during a vertical reorder calls only `todo.reorder`. Retain other blockers when changing the nesting blocker. Exercise distances on both sides of the nearest-step boundary without pinning a tie: `0.49S` keeps depth and `0.51S` changes it. Use the resolved Token, not a private 24px/32px copy.
- `u3710_a_pointer_can_nest_two_levels_from_the_trailing_grip`: `dx = +2S` reaches a legal grandchild depth without any reserved strip. A grip beside the header's right edge must still work: horizontal travel past the list's inline edge must not cancel a drop while its vertical position and the library's destination still identify that list and no external target is selected. Measure the actual pointer displacement, even when the library constrains its drag decoration. A release outside a valid destination, or Escape, still cancels.
- `u3710_depth_is_bounded_by_the_row_above`: the first gap stays root; excessive positive/negative displacement clamps to the gap's legal range. Preserve the exact parent/blocker assertions. Repeat a legal distance at different list widths to catch absolute-coordinate or padding-dependent projection.
- `u3710_a_drag_that_changes_both_sets_blockers_then_reorders`: a drop changing parent and order calls `todo.setBlockers` once, then `todo.reorder` once after success, for only the dragged Todo; descendants keep their blockers. A same-parent/same-index drop calls neither. Retain refusal and focus proofs in `u3711_a_refused_drop_shows_the_cause_and_changes_nothing` and `u3800c_a_%s_nest_returns_focus_to_the_moved_grip` in the same file.

Policy — precise amendment: this fixes U3710's sideways-offset interpretation to the formula above and replaces only its outside-list cancellation for horizontal overrun that still has this list as its destination. All other cancellation paths remain. It also replaces U535's absolute-horizontal-position wording (`ui-buttons.md`) with displacement from pointer-down; its exact parent/index, descendant, keyboard and callback requirements remain. Policy — landing preview: retain U3800a/U3710's single empty landing slot, original source position and projected target-depth inset. At root, child, grandchild, un-nest and clamped depths, the slot predicts the parent/order that release commits; it adds no second indent or persistent gap. The critic judges this geometry, including preview/accepted pairs and a cancelled drag. These are the #4535-family observers requested by #4710; the existing executable names are `u3710_`/`u535_`, not `u4535_`.
