# Clear a Workstream: a handoff, then a new iteration (A32, U350)

Module: `crates/agents`, `crates/contracts`, `crates/rupd`, `crates/rup`, `contracts/generated/` and `apps/desktop`. Ids: A32, U350. Found by the user's report #817: an Agent outruns the user and leaves more Todos, Pads and Subagents than they can digest, so they want to clear the Workstream, keep what they asked for and start over. Decided by the user: a clear keeps the old Thread, every removed Subagent's files, and the Todos and Pads the Door's handoff leaves after pruning; the user picked this over a clear that keeps only the handoff. Decided here (the issue left it to the spec): pruned Todos and Pads are **archived** into one Pad, never deleted outright, so no ask or note the user wrote is lost. Not rewinding to a checkpoint (#808).

`GLOSSARY.md` gains **Clear**, **Handoff** and **Iteration** in the implementing PR (Contract recipe). A clear starts the Door's next Attempt (A20) as a fresh conversation, never by typing `/clear` or any command into the Door (Principle P9 of `PRINCIPLES.md`).

**A32 clearing a Workstream writes a handoff, then restarts its Door.** Contract change (the Contract recipe of `.agents/builder.md`): methods `workstream.clear {id, reason}` and `workstream.handoff {id, summary, keep_todos, keep_pads}`, one Rail field `iteration` (an integer from `1`) and events `workstream.cleared {id, iteration}` and `workstream.clearFailed {id, code, message}`. `workstream.clear` never holds its call open: it returns `{pending: true}` at once and the clear ends with one of the two events. Given a Workstream with a running Door, two Subagents (one under the other, each with a Worktree and branch), Todos and Pads, then:
- **Reason.** `workstream.clear` with a `reason` empty after trimming, or longer than 2000 characters, is `INVALID_PARAMS` and changes nothing. An unknown id is `NOT_FOUND`, a Subagent or Terminal id is `INVALID_PARAMS`, and a second clear while one is pending is `CONFLICT`.
- **Ask.** A valid call changes nothing yet: it Steers the Door (H11) once with a message holding the user's `reason`, the user's own Messages of this Iteration in order, the Workstream's Todos and Pads with ids and owners, and the instruction to call `workstream.handoff`. The call returns `{pending: true}`; the Rail still shows every node.
- **Handoff.** `workstream.handoff` is allowed only to the Door of that Workstream while its clear is pending (any other Actor is `FORBIDDEN`, no pending clear is `CONFLICT`). `summary` is non-empty text of what happened and why the user cleared; `keep_todos` and `keep_pads` name the items kept. Every id or name that is not in the Workstream is `INVALID_PARAMS` and the clear stays pending. The Door is told to keep only what the user's own words clearly asked for from the start, and to leave out what Agents added on their own; the Daemon does not judge this, it applies the list.
- **Then, in this order.** (1) Each Todo and Pad of the Workstream not kept is archived: its text is appended to the Pad `archive-<n>` (`n` is the Iteration that ended; created owned by the user, one section per item with its title, id, owner and text), then deleted with its usual Touch and event. A Todo another Todo is blocked by (T-blockers) is archived the same way and leaves the blocker lists of the kept Todos. (2) Every Subagent of the Workstream, deepest first, is stopped as `agent.stop` does and leaves the Rail; its Worktree directory and branch are never removed, whatever their state (this is not `agent.discard` and not G5's cleanup), and its Pads and Todos follow rule (1). (3) The Handoff text is the `summary` plus a list of every removed Subagent with its name, Worktree path and branch, and the name of the archive Pad. (4) The Door is stopped and its next Attempt starts a fresh conversation (not `--resume`, A22) whose first prompt is the Handoff text; `iteration` becomes `n + 1` and `workstream.cleared` and `rail.changed` are emitted once, after the new Attempt's `SessionStart`.
- **Nothing else.** The Workstream, its id, name, parent, order, Home of the kept items, its Terminals (X11) and every file outside the removed Subagents' Worktrees are unchanged. A kept Todo or Pad keeps its id, name and owner (a kept Pad owned by a removed Subagent becomes the user's, as `pad.setOwner` does).
- **No handoff.** If no valid `workstream.handoff` arrives within 120 seconds, the clear ends with `workstream.clearFailed` carrying code `NOT_ACKED` (no new code is allowed, G8) and everything stays as it was; a Door that is not running is `CONFLICT` from the call itself; `workstream.clear {id, reason, without_handoff: true}` then makes the Daemon write the Handoff itself from the `reason`, the removed Subagents' list and the archive Pad (every Todo and Pad is archived, none kept is lost) and runs the same steps. Nothing is deleted or stopped before the Handoff exists.
- **Failure.** If stopping a Subagent fails, the clear stops there: stopped Subagents stay stopped on the Rail, the rest and the Door are untouched, `workstream.clearFailed` carries the error and a second `workstream.clear` retries from that point with the same archive Pad (no item archived twice). If the Door's new Attempt does not start (`workstream.clearFailed`, `CONFLICT`), the Workstream keeps its Iteration, the Door stays stopped and `rail.startDoor` (A7) resumes the old conversation's Workstream as it does after any failed start.
- **Old Thread.** The Door's earlier conversation stays readable: `message.list {id}` returns the Messages of every Iteration in order, each carrying its `iteration`.

**U350 the user clears a Workstream from the Rail.** Narrows U143. Given a Workstream with a Door (running or stopped, any Kind), then:
- **Action.** `Clear…` is an item, icon and label, in the Rail row's menu and its `Clear` row icon (`Clear Workstream…`). It is absent for a Subagent and while a clear is pending. The Workstream header has no such item (U467 removed the Thread's header).
- **Reason prompt.** Choosing it opens, in place and without moving a row (U46), `Why clear?` (the prompt is labelled `Clear <name>`) with a text field focused, the line `The supervisor writes a summary, keeps what you asked for, archives the rest, and stops <n> Subagents; their files stay` (`stops 1 Subagent` for one), and `Clear and keep a summary` with `Keep` trailing on the same line. `Clear and keep a summary` is disabled while the reason is empty after trimming. Esc or `Keep` calls nothing. `⏎` in the field or `Clear and keep a summary` calls `workstream.clear {id, reason}` once.
- **Waiting.** While the call is pending the field and `Clear and keep a summary` are disabled and the line reads `The supervisor is writing the summary`; nothing leaves the Rail. A `NOT_ACKED` or `CONFLICT` (from the call or from `workstream.clearFailed`) shows `✕ <message>` and, for `NOT_ACKED`, a second light word `Clear without a summary` that calls the method again with `without_handoff: true`; the reason stays in the field.
- **Result.** The call returns `{pending: true}` at once; on `workstream.cleared` the prompt closes and the Subagents have left the Rail through `rail.changed`. The Door's Terminal starts from the Handoff. Todos and Pads left on the Shelf are the kept ones; the archive Pad is on the Shelf as the user's Pad. The old Iteration's Messages and the `Iteration <n+1> · cleared: <reason>` rule are no longer shown (U467).
- **Never silent.** No removal is shown before the Handoff exists; a failed clear shows the daemon's message and keeps the Rail as the daemon holds it.
