# UI: the centre spans from the Project tabs to the Terminal bar

Module: `apps/desktop/src/` (`App.tsx`, `settings/`, `styles.css`). Source: #6073, the owner: "nothing should ever come above the center and take from it / the center spans fully from the project tabs to the terminal bar". Ids: U6073a–U6073c. Journey: Understand and Intervene (`.agents/data/journey.md`). Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method, nothing under `contracts/`, no Token changes. This specification orders implementation; it claims no delivered behavior.

Policy: widens U4454a (`scenarios/remove-time-timeline.md`, "no navigation row is mounted above the centre content") and U4453a (`scenarios/ui-navigation-always.md`, the Agent's Terminal fills the height down to the Terminal bar) from navigation rows to every control and line. Amends U5242c (`scenarios/ui-app-settings.md`): after Back, no `Forward →` control shows outside Settings, so "a control `Forward` in the restored view's header (or `⌘]`)" becomes `⌘]` alone; Settings' own header keeps its Back and disabled Forward. The owner chose keyboard-only over a floating Forward. The Drawer narrowing the centre from the side (U430, #1109) stays. Floating uses the out-of-flow `overlay` of `ReservedLine` (`apps/desktop/src/ink/ReservedLine.tsx`), as `.todo-undo` does; no new overlay machinery.

**U6073a the centre's top edge meets the Project tabs and its bottom edge meets the Terminal bar, whatever shows.** Given an open Project with an Agent selected, when any control or line is shown or cleared above the centre (another Project's Rail failure, the native failure, the storage failure), then no element is mounted in flow between the Project tabs and the centre, the centre keeps its rectangle, and no `terminal.resize` is sent and the Terminal's rows do not change. Nothing that floats covers the Project tabs or the Terminal bar. Asserted by planned connected-App test `u6073a_nothing_above_the_centre_resizes_the_terminal` in `apps/desktop/src/terminal/`, which shows and clears each in turn and asserts zero `terminal.resize` calls and the centre's DOM position among its siblings, never pixel layout. Policy: the rule holds for any control or line added later; review rejects a PR that mounts one in flow above the centre.

**U6073b no Forward control shows outside Settings.** Given Settings opened from an Agent (U4686b) and closed with Back, when the view is restored, then no control named `Forward` exists outside Settings, and `⌘]` reopens Settings on the page last shown with its exact unfinished field text; opening any other view clears it (U5242c). Settings' own header keeps `Back` and the disabled `Forward`. Asserted by planned connected-App test `u6073b_no_forward_control_after_back_and_cmd_bracket_reopens` in `apps/desktop/src/settings/`, which opens Settings, presses Back, finds no `Forward` by role, asserts zero `terminal.resize` calls, then presses `⌘]` and finds Settings open on the same page. `u5242c_forward_reopens_settings_after_back_and_clears_on_other_navigation` is rewritten to press `⌘]` instead of the control and keeps its other clauses.

**U6073c each failure floats and keeps its text, actions and announcement.** Given another Project's Rail failure, the native failure and the storage failure shown in turn, when each shows and clears, then it floats over the centre's top edge, out of flow, with its text, title and announcement unchanged, and its Open and Dismiss (Rail failure) or Retry (storage failure) reachable by role. Dismiss and Retry act as before. Asserted by planned connected-App test `u6073c_failures_float_with_actions_and_announcement` in `apps/desktop/src/`, asserting roles, live-region text, call counts and zero `terminal.resize` calls.

Policy — rendered observer: `just capture U6073a,U6073b,U6073c` at 1512×950 in dark mode, after Back from Settings and then with each failure shown, leaving images under `artifacts/ux/<id>/` uncommitted. The design critic judges D2, D5 and D6: the centre's rectangle matches the capture with none of them, nothing covers the Project tabs or the Terminal bar, and no Check passing on main regresses.
