# UI: a Glyph designed for its Kind (U396 to U398)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U396 to U398.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method, no `contracts/` file and no Token changes; every colour is a Token already in `apps/desktop/src/tokens.css` and every size a space step. Found by the user's report #1066: the Glyph of an Agent's Kind is "undesigned and unfit". Seen: `needs-you` and `working` both draw the `circle` icon (`apps/desktop/src/ink/glyph.ts:10,13`), told apart only by fill and tone, and the Tab mark (`apps/desktop/src/header/tab.ts:18`) is a third circle for the same two Kinds. Narrows U4 (Glyph tones), U132 and U86; D4's "one of the six Glyphs" now means the six shapes of U396. `GLOSSARY.md` changes in the implementing PR (Contract recipe): **Glyph**, **Ink** and **Tab mark** (U396, U398).

**U396 each Kind has its own Glyph shape; solid shapes are Ink.** Rule: a Glyph is drawn on one 16 px box with a 12 px live area and one 1.75 px stroke, in `currentColor`; the two urgent Kinds are solid masses and the other four are strokes, so urgency is read from weight before colour. Given a Rail with every Kind (seed `agents-10`, U26), light and dark, at 1280 by 820 and 1280 by 800, when a test reads the rendered tree and computed style, then:

> Overturned by U401: the six shapes are replaced by the ring-fill Glyphs; its geometry, aria and call-site clauses stay.

- **Six shapes.** `KindGlyph` renders one inline `svg` per Kind, `glyphOf(kind).shape` and `data-shape` on the `.glyph` span holding: `error` `square-x` (a solid rounded square with a cut-out `x`, red); `needs-you` `disc-bang` (a solid disc with a cut-out `!`, amber); `blocked` `bars` (two stroked vertical bars, grey); `working` `arc` (an open three-quarter ring with a round cap, accent); `idle` `dot` (a 4 px solid dot, grey); `done` `check` (a stroked check, grey). No two Kinds share a `data-shape`, so `needs-you` (solid, amber) and `working` (open, accent) differ in silhouette at 16 px and in a greyscale capture, not by tone alone (D4). The cut-outs are `fill-rule: evenodd` holes, so no extra colour is drawn. Test: `u396_every_kind_has_its_own_shape`.
- **Ink follows mass.** `hasInk` holds for exactly the two solid shapes, `square-x` and `disc-bang`; `.glyph.ink` is on them and on no other Kind (GLOSSARY.md's Ink stays "colour, never weight, only `error` and `needs-you`", with "solid shape" added). Test: `u396_only_solid_shapes_are_ink`.
- **Tones.** Tones are U4's: red, amber, accent, and grey for `blocked`, `idle`, `done`; every drawn pixel colour is `currentColor`, a Token (D1); 3:1 against the Rail's ground, the selected band and the Shelf's ground in both schemes, `done` exempt (D5); with no `error` or `needs-you` row the page draws no red and no amber (D3). Test: `u396_tones_are_tokens_and_hold_contrast`.
- **Accessibility kept.** `KindGlyph`'s `role="img"` and `aria-label` equal to the sentence-case Kind (`Working`, `Needs-you`, etc., U416), and `decorative` giving `aria-hidden="true"` with neither, are unchanged. Test: `u396_aria_label_and_decorative_are_kept`.
- **Geometry.** The `.glyph` box stays `--space-4` square (a Rail row's `--space-5` wide column, U359's 16 px Shelf column), so every Glyph's left x in a list is within 1 px (D12), no row's height changes (U112) and no click target's hit area changes (D6). Test: `u396_the_glyph_box_and_row_geometry_do_not_move`.
- **Motion.** None: the Glyph changes instantly when the Kind does, as `docs/motion.md` says of "Status glyph change", and no Kind loops (the calm rule). `working` does not spin; `needs-you` draws no pulse (the arrival pulse row of `docs/motion.md` stays deferred past the MVP). No duration, so D9 has nothing new to measure. Test: `u396_no_glyph_animates_or_transitions`.
- **Call sites.** `KindGlyph` keeps its props `{kind, decorative?}`, so its callers (`rail/RailRow.tsx`, `rail/Rail.tsx`, `todos/Todos.tsx`, `todos/TodoDrawer.tsx`, `pads/PadRichEditor.tsx`) need no change; `header/ProjectTab.tsx` is U398's. Test: `u396_every_caller_renders_the_new_shape` (a Rail row, a Todo row, the Todo Drawer head and a Pad failure).
