**U3774 the comments sit as one thread with the author on each.** Narrows U3741's list. Given a Todo with comments, when its Drawer opens, then the comments are one thread, oldest first, each block headed by its author: `Me`, or the Agent's name beside that Agent's Glyph (the Agent's Rail Glyph, or none when the Rail holds no such Agent). A comment sent through `Reply` on an Agent's comment shows in the thread as an ordinary comment after it (the contract holds no parent, policy). U3741's time, plain-text body, `Reply`, error and mark-read clauses hold. Test: `u3774_the_thread_shows_each_author_with_me_or_the_agents_glyph`. Moves: D2, D5, D6; observer `u3774_thread_rendered` in a dedicated headless agent-browser session on `tree-40`, dark mode only, at 1512×950, with a 300-character comment, ten comments and non-Latin text; `just capture U3774`, committing no image or video.

**U3775 retired.** #7312 retires the `::status` line: an Agent's comment folds its details with U7312a's `<details>` block (`scenarios/ui-item-view.md`), the one fold. Its tests `u3775_a_status_line_is_a_button_that_folds_its_details` and `u3775_only_an_agents_comment_body_makes_status_lines` go; `u7312a_an_agent_comment_renders_markdown_and_a_closed_fold` proves the fold, the escaping and that the user's comment stays plain.

**U3776 Enter sends, and a new reply arrives announced and in place.** Narrows U3741's send keys. Given the comment field with text, when the user presses Enter (no `⇧`, not composing) then `todo.comment` is called once with the text, as U3741's button does; `⇧Enter` adds a line, and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter still sends. An empty field sends nothing. Given the Drawer open, when a comment arrives by `todo.commented` or the user's own is added, then it is appended below the ones shown, a polite live region (`aria-live="polite"`) names its author and body once, and the new block appears at once, with no animation, while the comments above it keep their position (no layout shift, #3294). The comments present when the Drawer opens are not announced. Opening a comment's fold (U7312a) animates nothing beyond its body showing. No shimmer or looping motion (#3298, policy). U3741 and U3742 keep their behaviour.
