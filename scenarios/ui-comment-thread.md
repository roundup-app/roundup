# UI: the Todo's comments read as one thread (U3774 to U3776, U3796)

Module: `apps/desktop/src/todos/` (`TodoComments.tsx`, `copy.ts`). Ids: U3774, U3775, U3776, U3796. Work order: [#3311](https://github.com/saadshahd/roundup/issues/3311). Journey: Review. Restyles U3741's section after [AffectUI's Comment Thread](https://www.affectui.com/components/comment-thread) (free, MIT); its CSS may be copied into Solid, credited in the PR and set in Tokens only. Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method, nothing under `contracts/`, no new Token. `todo.comment`, `todo.commented`, `todo.markCommentsRead` and how an Agent's comment reaches the Inbox (U3742) stay as written. No @mentions: one that reaches an Agent is new behaviour and needs its own ask.

**U3774 the comments sit as one thread with the author on each.** Narrows U3741's list. Given a Todo with comments, when its Drawer opens, then the comments are one thread, oldest first, each block headed by its author: `Me`, or the Agent's name beside that Agent's Glyph (the Agent's Rail Glyph, or none when the Rail holds no such Agent). A comment sent through `Reply` on an Agent's comment shows in the thread as an ordinary comment after it (the contract holds no parent, policy). U3741's time, plain-text body, `Reply`, error and mark-read clauses hold. Test: `u3774_the_thread_shows_each_author_with_me_or_the_agents_glyph`. Moves: D2, D5, D6; observer `u3774_thread_rendered` in a dedicated headless agent-browser session on `tree-40`, dark mode only, at 1512×950, with a 300-character comment, ten comments and non-Latin text; `just capture U3774`, committing no image or video.

**U3775 an Agent's status lines fold open to their details.** Given an Agent's comment whose body holds a status line, written by the Agent as a line starting `::status ` and the summary (`::status Ran tests: 42 passed`), with the detail lines that follow it indented by two spaces, then the thread shows the summary as a button with `aria-expanded="false"` and no detail text visible; activating it by click, Enter or Space sets `aria-expanded="true"` and shows the details as plain text, and activating it again hides them. A status line with no detail lines is plain text, not a button. Every other line of the body stays plain text in order. The webview derives no status line from a Terminal's output or from any source but the comment body (B19, B20, policy), and a `::status` line in a comment by `Me` is plain text. Folding calls nothing and is the user's act (policy). Tests: `u3775_a_status_line_is_a_button_that_folds_its_details`, `u3775_only_an_agents_comment_body_makes_status_lines`. Moves: D2, D5, D6; observer `u3774_thread_rendered` with an Agent comment of three status lines.

**U3776 Enter sends, and a new reply arrives announced and fading in once.** Narrows U3741's send keys. Given the comment field with text, when the user presses Enter (no `⇧`, not composing) then `todo.comment` is called once with the text, as U3741's button does; `⇧Enter` adds a line, and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter still sends. An empty field sends nothing. Given the Drawer open, when a comment arrives by `todo.commented` or the user's own is added, then it is appended below the ones shown, a polite live region (`aria-live="polite"`) names its author and body once, and the new block fades in once (opacity only, at most 200 ms) while the comments above it keep their position (no layout shift, #3294). The comments present when the Drawer opens do not fade and are not announced. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the block appears at once. Folding a status line animates nothing beyond the details showing. No shimmer or looping motion (#3298, policy). U3741 and U3742 keep their behaviour. Tests: `u3776_enter_sends_once_and_shift_enter_adds_a_line`, `u3776_a_new_comment_is_announced_politely_and_the_opening_ones_are_not`, `u3776_a_new_comment_fades_once_and_is_instant_under_reduced_motion` (reads the animation the CSS gives, jsdom computes none, policy: agent-browser in the U26 harness). Moves: D2, D5, D6.

**U3796 the thread is observed in a real browser.** Closes the observer gaps of U3775 and U3776. Given the app served to a dedicated headless agent-browser session on `tree-40` at 1512×950 in dark mode, when the Drawer opens on a Todo whose Agent comment holds three status lines each with details, then each summary is a button with `aria-expanded="false"` and its details are not visible, activating one shows its details, and the thread and the comment field fit the Drawer without clipping (D2, D5, D6). Given the Drawer open, when a comment arrives by `todo.commented`, then the new block runs one CSS animation on `opacity` alone of at most 200 ms with one iteration, the blocks above it keep their offsets, and under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the same arrival runs no animation. Tests: `u3774_thread_rendered` (U3775's and U3774's observer, `apps/desktop/src/todos/u3774_rendered.test.ts` driving `u3774_browser.test.py`), `u3776_a_new_comment_fades_once_and_is_instant_under_reduced_motion` (the same pair, reading `getComputedStyle` animation properties in the browser), both next to `u3741_rendered.test.ts` and run by `just capture U3774` and `just capture U3776` only, committing no image or video. Moves: D2, D5, D6.
