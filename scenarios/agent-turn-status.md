# An Agent stays working for the whole turn

Module: `crates/agents` (`claude_code/mod.rs` Adapter, Observation intake) with `crates/messages` (the wake path). Ids: TS1–TS4. Work order: [#7173](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7173). Terms: Status, Observation, Signal, Message, Wake request (`GLOSSARY.md`). Refines A1 (`scenarios/agents.md`) and DTC2 (`scenarios/door-turn-cost.md`).

Source: on 2026-11-15 a Door's child ran a turn that began from a Door note and compacted its context, while `agent_digest` and `[wake]` notes reported it `idle` with its previous turn's last line. Each false `idle` fired the Door's `agent_wake` and cost a model turn. Policy: no new RPC, Message kind, Status kind or contract change.

**TS1 a turn is working from its prompt to its Stop.** Given an Agent whose turn the vendor runs, when a prompt or Message reaches it as its next prompt, then its Status is `working` from that Observation until the Stop of that turn, through any thinking, tool calls, background work and compaction; no title, tick or Stop of an earlier turn makes it `idle` in between, and the Message's delivery is read as a turn start. Test: `ts1_a_turn_stays_working_from_prompt_to_its_stop` in `crates/agents/tests/scenarios/`, an Adapter with an injected clock replaying Signals and titles, covering a Message delivered as the next prompt, long silence past `SPINNER_HOLD` with no title, a ✳ title with no `PreToolUse` before it, and `PreCompact` to `PostCompact` with a ✳ title between them.

**TS2 only a Stop of the current turn ends it.** Given a working Agent, when a `Stop` arrives for an earlier turn or with no turn identity while a later prompt is running, then Status stays `working` and no completion is published; the matching `Stop` makes it `idle` once. Test: `ts2_only_the_current_turns_stop_ends_it` in `crates/agents/tests/scenarios/`.

**TS3 audit of every turn-end and turn-start source.** Policy: the implementation PR lists each path below, with the test that holds it, and fixes any that ends a turn early or never starts one: the Stop hook, the ✳ title read as idle (`retitle`), the spinner hold (`tick`), the parked star after `PreToolUse`, `SubagentStop` and background-work release, `StopFailure` and `PostToolUse` recovery, Message delivery to the inbox as a turn start, and `PreCompact` and `PostCompact`. Each either ends a turn only on evidence of that turn's end, or leaves Status unchanged.

**TS4 a false idle wakes no one.** Given a Door whose child is in a turn that compacts its context, when the child's turn is held open across a compaction notice, then no Wake request fires for that child, no `[wake]` note or Digest shows it `idle`, and the Wake request fires once at the turn's Stop. Test: `ts4_no_wake_fires_while_a_compacting_turn_runs` in `crates/agents/tests/scenarios/`: a fake Claude receives a Message as its next prompt, holds the turn across a compaction notice, and the test asserts Status `working` and no wake until the Stop. It uses the production Adapter and wake path, no PTY and no real `claude`.
