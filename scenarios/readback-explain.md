Decisions this specification takes (the Issue's "My reading" points, each kept and tested):
- **One method, no Daemon state.** `agent.explain {id, to?, path, side, start, end, form}` runs one call and answers with its result; the Daemon keeps none, so nothing of an explanation survives a Daemon restart, and closing the view cancels nothing the Daemon holds. `side` is `old` or `new`, the side the lines are numbered on; `form` is `words` or `diagram`. The Daemon handles each request apart from the others, so the view stays usable while it waits.
- **Input.** Every hunk of the diff as RB1 names them (`{id, path, text}`), the selection `{path, side, start, end, text}` and the form; no flows, no last message, nothing of the conversation (#7687 allows the Agent's last message for gaps only, and an explanation has none).
- **Credit and place.** The answer shows under the last line of the selection as a read-only block credited "Read back from the diff". It is not a note, joins no batch and no Message, and its text is never sent to the Agent. The user may turn any of its words into a note by hand (#7676).
- **Keys and buttons.** W asks in words and D as a diagram, from the focused diff (no field or editor holding the key); each file's header holds two icon buttons for the same two asks. The ask reads the file's current selection (U7675d): the focused line, a Shift-selected range or a drag's range. A drag still opens the note field (U7676a) and keeps doing so; the ask does not close it. R, N, V, E, J, K and X keep their meaning.
- **Stale.** An answer is stale when its file's blob id in the listing is no longer the one it was asked on (U7673a's blob id, the change signal of RB7); a write to another file leaves it. A stale answer stays, dimmed, with "Changed since readback" and an icon button named "Ask again".
- **Kept for the session.** Each Agent's answers are kept with its Changes view's other places (U7675f) for the connection's session, so closing the view does not lose a paid answer; a restart of the App or the Daemon drops them.

**RB13 only the user's call starts an explanation, and its input holds the hunks, the lines and the form.** Given an Agent with a Worktree of its own whose diff is a new `a.rs` and a changed `seed.txt`, and a last message `MARKER-LAST`, when `agent.explain {id, path: "a.rs", side: "new", start: 1, end: 1, form: "words"}` is called, then the fake `claude` is launched once and its stdin holds each hunk `{id, path, text}` (`a.rs#1`, `seed.txt#1`), `selection` `{path, side, start, end, text}` with `text` the line read from the hunk, `form`, and neither the last message nor an earlier turn's; with the `old` side and the changed line of `seed.txt` the text is the deleted line. An Agent actor's call is refused with `FORBIDDEN` and a shared checkout fails with `no_worktree` (RB2), each with no launch. Every other method launches nothing, as `rb2_only_the_start_method_launches_the_call` proves. Asserted in `rb13_an_explanation_reads_the_hunks_the_lines_and_the_form_only` (Rust, fake `claude` that records stdin) and `rb13_an_agent_actor_and_a_shared_checkout_have_no_explanation`.

**RB14 the answer has the shape of its form.** Given a read run, then `agent.explain` answers `{state: "explained", form, text}`: for `words` two to four sentences, for `diagram` the parts and calls as text, one call per line as `A -> B` (a chain `A -> B -> C` allowed), a line `Before` or `After` starting each panel where the shape changed, the format `parseDiagram` reads (policy: the prompt asks for it, the Daemon never fails a call on it, as RB3). The system text of each form tells the model its form and the input's shape. An empty answer fails with kind `badAnswer` and a cause in words. Asserted in `rb14_each_form_answers_its_shape_and_an_empty_answer_fails`.

**RB15 lines outside the diff, a diff over the cap or a failed call fail with their cause and no result.** Given the same Agent, then:
- **Outside.** Lines with none of their side in a hunk of `path` fail `{state: "failed", kind: "outOfDiff", cause}`, the cause naming the path and the lines, and the fake `claude` is launched zero times; lines partly in a hunk send those it holds. Asserted in `rb15_lines_outside_the_diff_fail_naming_them_and_launch_nothing`.
- **Cap.** A diff over RB8's cap fails with kind `tooLarge` and the same words that name the cap, launching zero times. Asserted in `rb15_a_diff_over_the_cap_fails_naming_the_cap`; RB8's under-cap test (`rb8_a_diff_under_the_cap_is_sent_whole`) stands for the other side, and the cap is one function both calls use.
- **Call.** A call that fails answers `{state: "failed", kind: "call", cause}` with the Role's call error and no token, price or usage figure: the same call as RB5, whose `rb5_a_failure_cause_carries_no_usage_figure` stands. Policy.

**U7698a only the user's action asks.** Given the Changes view on the Tree target with the fake App, then:
- **Not asked.** Opening the view, a turn that wrote, a Fetch, selecting lines (a click, a Shift-click, a drag or J and K) and a second of waiting call `agent.explain` zero times. Asserted in `u7698a_only_the_user_asks_and_a_comparison_without_a_tree_offers_none`.
- **Ask.** The two icon buttons of a file's header, named "Explain in words" and "Explain as diagram" (no visible text; their tooltips the same words), and W and D, each call `agent.explain` once with the Agent's `id`, the open `to`, the file's `path` and the selection's `side`, `start` and `end` and the form; an ask while that range reads calls nothing more. The ? list names W and D. Asserted in the same test and `u7698a_the_keys_list_names_w_and_d`.
- **Changes.** On the Changes target the buttons show and W and D send the open group as `group` (U7955a). Asserted in `u7955a_changes_reads_send_the_open_group_and_flows_traces`.

**U7698b every state is drawn under the lines.** Given an ask, then under the last line of the range:
- **Asking.** "Reading back…" with role status, and the view stays usable: J, K, the lens and the tree answer as without it. Asserted in `u7698b_asking_keeps_the_view_usable_and_the_answer_shows_under_the_lines`.
- **In words.** The credit "Read back from the diff" and the answer's sentences. Asserted in the same test.
- **As a diagram.** The credit and `ReadbackDiagram` of the answer: the figure with its text equivalent ("run calls load"), the Before and After panels, below 480 px of pane the words alone (U7688c). A diagram text with no call shows as the text it is. Asserted in `u7698b_a_diagram_answer_draws_its_calls_as_words`.
- **Failed.** The cause in words and an icon button named "Retry", which asks again once; the cause holds no token, price or usage figure. Asserted in `u7698b_a_failed_ask_says_why_and_retry_asks_once`.

**U7698c a write over the lines makes the answer stale.** Given an answer under a range, then a change of that file's blob id in the listing dims it and adds "Changed since readback" and an icon button named "Ask again", which calls `agent.explain` once more with the same range and form and replaces the answer; a change to another file leaves it as it was. Asserted in `u7698c_a_write_to_the_file_makes_the_answer_stale_and_ask_again_asks_once`.

**U7698d an explanation is a personal aid.** The view sends no Message and saves no note, Pad or Todo from an explanation, edits none of its text, and gates no Landing or Viewed mark on it; asking opens no note field, and a drag that opened one still does. Asserted in `u7698d_an_explanation_is_in_no_note_or_message` (the fake App's call list holds no `message.*`, `pad.*` or `todo.*` call and no note holds its text).

**U7698e answers outlive the view for the session.** Given an answer, when the user closes the Changes view and opens it again, then the answer shows under the same lines with no call; the Daemon is asked only by the user's ask (P9, P11: a paid answer is never lost to navigation). Asserted in `u7698e_an_answer_outlives_the_view_for_the_session`.
