# UI: jump to an Agent or Terminal by name and message it from the keyboard (U529 to U533)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U529 to U533. Work Issue: #2223. Journey stage: Intervene (`.agents/data/journey.md`). Found by the owner's goal "switches from Solo to roundup and wants the top five jobs as fast", job 1, type a message into a running Agent or Terminal. Measured on `tree-40` at 1280 by 800: by pointer 2 clicks, then the text and Enter; by keyboard no way (`⌘1` then `↓` did not reach an Agent 25 rows down in 12 presses; `⌘2` focuses a Watch pane (U375) whose keys write nothing and say nothing; Take control has no chord). Solo: `⌘E`, a few letters, Enter, `⌘→`, the text, Enter.

Webview only: no App seam command, no Daemon method, no `contracts/` file, no Token, no GLOSSARY term. Uses `takeover.begin` (B6) and `terminal.write` as U12, U107 and U375 do. Scope (policy): `apps/desktop/src/switcher/` (U51 never shipped; this builds it), `apps/desktop/src/keys/` (`chords.ts`, Help), `apps/desktop/src/terminal/` for the Watch band, one mount line in `App.tsx`. Reuse (rule 9) for the Switcher's list: the implementation PR names Ark UI's Combobox (`@ark-ui/solid`, already the wrapped primitive of `apps/desktop/src/ui/`) against at least one other maintained candidate; the filter field and list are a `ui/` primitive, not a hand-written state machine.

Chord budget (policy): a chord is one press with a modifier; the text and its Enter are not chords. Message an Agent: `⌘K`, the letters of its name, `⇧⏎`, the text, `⏎` is 2 chords and no pointer.

**U529 `⌘K` switches to an Agent or Terminal by typing part of its name.** Builds U51, narrows it (never a Terminal; calls no method of its own) and narrows U164 ("no Switcher entry"). Given an open Project on `tree-40`, when the user presses `⌘K`, then a Switcher opens over the centre pane with its text field focused and a list of every Agent, Door, Group and Terminal by name, each with its Kind mark and, for a Terminal, its Workstream's name in `--grey`. U51 holds for the order (`error`, then `needs-you`, then Rail order), the case-blind contains-filter, `no match`, `↑` and `↓` without wrap, Esc and `⌘K` closing and returning focus, no keystroke reaching a Terminal, and the chord's modifier rules. Typing `ag` shows the matches at once and the list never needs the pointer. `⏎` on an Agent, Door or Group selects it as a click does (U6) and puts focus in the pane; `⏎` on a Terminal selects its owner row, shows its bubble if hidden, restores it if minimised and puts focus in its emulator (U165). Look (D6, D7, policy): the field and rows are native-looking, no outline on the field, no hover box on a row; the highlighted row is a filled `--sunken` row, and the focus ring is U133's single ring on the field only. Tests (`u529_`, fake App, `apps/desktop/src/switcher/`): `u529_cmd_k_lists_agents_doors_groups_and_terminals_and_filters_as_you_type`, `u529_enter_on_an_agent_selects_it_and_focuses_the_pane`, `u529_enter_on_a_terminal_shows_its_bubble_and_focuses_its_emulator`, `u529_escape_returns_focus_and_calls_nothing`. The design critic judges D2, D5 and D6 with the Switcher open at 1280 by 800, light and dark (AGENTS.md rule 10); the `u529_` tests above are the kept behaviour tests. Moves D2, D5, D6.

**U530 `⇧⏎` in the Switcher takes control.** Narrows U51's "`⏎` selects" and its "calls no method of its own". Given the open Switcher (U529) with an Agent other than the Door highlighted, when the user presses `⇧⏎`, then the Agent is selected as `⏎` does, `takeover.begin {agent}` is called once (B6) and focus goes into its emulator, so the next keys reach `terminal.write` as U12 says; the Switcher is closed. `⇧⏎` on a Group, a Door (U187's Thread mode) or a Terminal acts as `⏎`. A `CONFLICT` leaves the Agent selected in Watch and shows U80's one failure line (as U375 says). Tests (`u530_`): `u530_shift_enter_selects_begins_the_takeover_once_and_focuses_the_emulator`, `u530_shift_enter_on_a_group_acts_as_enter`, `u530_a_conflict_stays_in_watch_with_the_failure_line`. Help (U54) lists `⌘K  Switch to a row by name` and `⇧⏎  Take control, in the switcher` and its drift test covers both.

**U531 `⌘⏎` takes control of the selected Agent.** Narrows U375's Take over clause: the chord is a second way besides the `Take control` button and `⌥1` to `⌥9`. Given a selected Agent other than the Door whose Terminal runs and is in Watch, when the user presses `⌘⏎` with focus anywhere but a text field, a Shelf row button or a Terminal that is not in Watch (a Shelf row button keeps `⌘Enter` for U458's toggle, so there the chord does not take control), then `takeover.begin {agent}` is called once, focus moves into the emulator and the overlay reads `You control` (U375). In a Takeover of the selected Agent, including from its Terminal's own input, the same chord calls `takeover.end {agent}` once and the overlay reads `Managed`. It does nothing with nothing selected, on an exited Terminal, and after `daemon-exited`; a key repeat acts once; `⇧⌘⏎`, `⌃⌘⏎`, `⌥⌘⏎` and a bare `⏎` do not take control (U32's rules). Tests (`u531_`): `u531_cmd_enter_begins_the_takeover_once_and_focuses_the_emulator`, `u531_cmd_enter_does_nothing_with_no_selection_or_after_daemon_exit`, `u531_cmd_enter_from_the_agents_terminal_toggles_control_both_ways`, `u531_a_held_cmd_enter_calls_begin_once`, `u531_other_modifier_sets_do_not_take_control`, `u531_cmd_enter_on_a_shelf_row_button_toggles_and_does_not_take_control`.

**U532 typing into a watched input says why nothing was sent.** Narrows U375's Default clause ("no key reaches `terminal.write`"), which stays true. Given a selected Agent in Watch with focus on its pane (`⌘2`, U41; the emulator takes no focus in Watch, U375), when the user presses a printable key, `⏎`, `⌫` or an arrow, then `terminal.write` is not called and the overlay's state text becomes `Managed: typing is not sent` until focus leaves the pane or the Takeover begins, after which the overlay reads as U375 says. No toast, no focus change and no sound. Chords that bind elsewhere (U32, U41) keep acting, and `⌘C` still copies (U101). Tests (`u532_`): `u532_a_key_in_a_watched_pane_writes_nothing_and_the_band_says_why`, `u532_the_band_reverts_when_focus_leaves_or_the_takeover_begins`, `u532_chords_and_copy_still_act_in_watch`.

**U533 two chords reach an Agent from the keyboard.** Given `tree-40` at 1280 by 800 with nothing selected and an idle Agent 25 rows down, when the user presses `⌘K`, types part of its name, presses `⇧⏎`, types `hello` and presses `⏎`, then `terminal.write` receives `hello\r` for that Agent's Terminal after one `takeover.begin`, with no pointer event and no more than 2 chords. Test: `u533_message_an_agent_in_two_chords_on_tree_40` in a headless real-browser harness test under `apps/desktop/src/keys/` (the isolated agent-browser session of `.agents/builder.md`; none attaches to the user's browser), recording the pointer events (zero) and the call order. A native Mac look is not asserted; the user reports what they meet on the Mac.

## Find actions by name (U2572a to U2572d)

Direction: Issue #2572, the owner's approved `new` search: Rail matches first, then `Actions` containing New Agent, New Agent with a prompt and New Terminal with their keys at the right. Journey: Enter (start Agents) and Intervene (find an action without leaving the current view). These clauses define implementation acceptance, not shipped behavior.

Scope (policy): `apps/desktop/src/switcher/`, `apps/desktop/src/keys/`, `apps/desktop/src/ui/Combobox.tsx` and its stylesheet, `apps/desktop/src/copy.ts`, and existing action registrations in `rail/Rail.tsx`, `terminal/Pane.tsx`, `bubbles/Bubbles.tsx`, `ask/AskBar.tsx`, `header/Strip.tsx` and `App.tsx`. Only sharing these handlers and their availability with the Switcher is in scope outside `switcher/`; no new RPC, App seam command, Token or domain term. Implementation Issue #2624 is blocked by specification Issue #2572; U2541's KeyChip is already supplied by completed #2550.

Mechanism (policy): extend the existing keyboard registration module to expose named actions through the same registered handler and availability predicate that the key uses. Identify each action by its `CHORDS` or `LEADER` row; labels and keys come from that row, never a parallel action catalogue. Owners expose their current guards, including guards currently inside `run`, for listing and execution. Do not synthesize or dispatch DOM key events to run an action. Capture the opening focus, selected row/Project and focused Bubble before the Switcher takes focus or its `onOpen` closes another surface. Restore the surviving opening focus before invoking an action; capture owner identity for actions whose key reads focus. Revalidate the identity and availability immediately before invocation. An unavailable or removed target runs nothing and leaves the Switcher open with its refreshed matches. Never fall back to a different Project, row or Bubble. Share this behavior at the existing registration seam; no second dispatcher or action implementation.

Eligibility (policy): include the three creation chords (`⌘N`, `⇧⌘N`, `⌘T`), Ask (`⌘/`), Remove (`⌘⌫`), Take control (`⌘⏎`) and leader J, ⇧J, M, W and S, only when the corresponding key has an effect in the opening context. `⌘N` remains available when it returns focus to an existing prompt field; otherwise the two Agent actions require Rail's `canSpawn`. New Terminal respects its existing owner-resolution and editor guards. Ask requires a live Daemon. Remove requires the originally focused Rail row; Take control requires U531's eligible Agent, live Terminal, Watch or Takeover, and focus exclusions. Leader M/W require the originally focused Bubble; J/⇧J require an attention target in their existing order; S requires its mounted Shelf. Exclude `⌘K` (arms the leader), leader K (opens this Switcher) and `⇧⏎` (acts on this Switcher's highlight). No Help-only or Terminal-native keys are added. Future table rows require an explicit owner availability registration before becoming searchable. The displayed Take control label remains the table's label in both Watch and Takeover, with U531's toggle semantics unchanged.

**U2572a search keeps destinations first and groups matching actions.** Given an open Project with Workstream `new-onboarding`, Agent `renew-token`, an owned Terminal named `new-shell`, and available creation actions, when leader K opens the Switcher and the user types ` new `, then the destination matches keep `entriesOf`'s U529 order, followed by one accessible group named `Actions` with New Agent, New Agent with a prompt and New Terminal in `CHORDS` order. Action matching uses the same trimmed, case-blind contains comparison against the table's `what` label as destination matching uses against its label. Blank input includes all eligible actions; matching CHORDS rows precede matching LEADER rows, each in table order. A query matching only actions still shows them; a query matching neither shows U51's no-match state and no empty Actions group. Test: `u2572a_searches_destinations_then_grouped_actions` under `apps/desktop/src/switcher/`, asserting option identities/order, group roles, mixed/only-action/empty results and case/whitespace behavior. Namespaced action option values cannot collide with Rail or Terminal values, asserted by this test using a destination named like an action.

**U2572b an action choice runs its key's behavior once in the opening view.** Given each eligible action in the policy above, when it is chosen by Enter, Shift+Enter or pointer, then the Switcher closes before the shared handler runs, the handler acts exactly once against the opening context, and focus and failures follow the same path as the corresponding key. Shift+Enter on an action is ordinary action selection; it does not additionally begin Takeover. Moving the highlight over destinations changes no selection before choosing an action. Test: `u2572b_actions_share_key_behavior_in_the_opening_view`, under `apps/desktop/src/switcher/`, comparing key and choice results for every included row with real mounted action owners: selection, call counts/arguments, prompt/Ask/Shelf state, focus destination, Bubble identity and failure roles. Include New Agent with a prompt then its submit, New Terminal with a rejected creation call, Remove with cancellation, and Take control in both Watch and Takeover. Test: `u2572b_action_choice_does_not_select_a_highlighted_destination`, proving creation uses the opening Workstream after highlighting a match in another Workstream. No action-choice keystroke reaches `terminal.write`.

**U2572c unavailable actions cannot run against another target.** Given openings from a Rail row, watched Agent pane, taken Agent input, Terminal Bubble, Terminal tab editor and ordinary text field, when actions are listed, then the eligibility policy includes exactly the actions whose keys act there, and excludes the three recursive or Switcher-local rows. When the opening row/Bubble disappears, the Daemon exits or an owner becomes pending while the Switcher is open, then affected actions disappear; a stale selection calls nothing, selects nothing else and keeps the Switcher open. Escape cancels without an action and restores surviving opening focus as U529 does. Test: `u2572c_actions_follow_opening_context_and_revalidate`, under `apps/desktop/src/switcher/`, covering those contexts, no attention target, no selection, pending Agent creation, exited Terminal and removed targets, including stale pointer selection and Project change. Existing modal layers continue to suppress underlying shortcuts; the Switcher does not bypass U479's ownership.

**U2572d each action shows its table key through KeyChip.** Given matching actions, when they render, then every action's key is drawn through U2541's `KeyChip` from that table row; a leader action shows the `CHORDS` leader key followed by its `LEADER` key. Test: `u2572d_action_keys_come_from_the_tables`, under `apps/desktop/src/switcher/`, checks table-to-option key content and shared KeyChip rendering for chord and leader actions. Policy for looks: key chips align at the trailing edge, one chip per key symbol as U2541 defines, with the label preceding them and the Actions heading distinct from destinations; no new key markup or Tokens. Moves Checks 2, 5 and 6: D2, D5 and D6 in `docs/design-system.md`, heading `The checks`. Observer: isolated headless harness at 1512×950 on `tree-40`, light, dark and increased contrast, empty search, `new`, action-only and no-match searches, plus the rejected New Terminal call with its failure text visible.
