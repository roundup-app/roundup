# Delete Reactions

Retirement record for #5740. On 2026-11-02 the owner asked “will we remove all the fluff and reactions that wasn't used and agents already do with their monitor ability?” Asked “Delete Reactions entirely (feature, Principle and screens)?”, the owner answered “Delete them”. This reverses the 2026-10-19 direction in #2775 and retires P13, NR1–NR11 and U4276a–c. Agents retain B41 wakes, their vendor's Monitor and its scheduled routines. This specification changes acceptance; it claims no runtime removal.
