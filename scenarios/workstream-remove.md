# Remove a Workstream: its finished work goes, its work in progress stays (A34, U367)

Module: `crates/agents`, `crates/rupd`, `crates/contracts`, `crates/rup`, `contracts/generated/` and `apps/desktop`. Ids: A34, U367. Found by the user's report #954: after a Workstream's work is finished, removing it left its Agents, Todos and Pads at the Project root. Decided by the user: removal deletes what is finished and keeps what is in progress; work whose branch the Base lacks is kept, not discarded (G5), and the user picked this over a box per Agent in the confirm; Pads follow their owner, and the user picked this over keeping every Pad and over ticking Pads in the confirm. **A34 removing a Workstream removes its finished work and keeps the rest.** Narrows A16 ("lifts its children", "Pads are never touched"), T13 and P12's removal clauses and G5 for a Workstream. Contract change (the Contract recipe of `.agents/builder.md`): `rail.remove` of a Workstream returns `{removed, kept}` where `removed` is a list of `{id, kind, name}` and `kept` a list of `{id, kind, name, reason}`; `reason` is `running`, `dirty`, `ahead` or `open`; `rail.remove` of an Agent or Terminal still returns `null`. The App seam passes it through. Given a Workstream with Subagents, Todos and Pads, then:
- **Agents.** Each Agent in the Workstream, at any depth, is judged by its Status. An Agent with a running program (its Kind is not `done` or `error`) is kept, moved to the Project root in its order, and its reason is `running`. An exited Agent with no Worktree, or whose Worktree is clean and not ahead of the Base, is removed as G5's clean path says: its Worktree directory and branch are deleted. An exited Agent whose Worktree is dirty or whose branch is ahead of the Base is kept at the Project root with reason `dirty` or `ahead` and nothing of its directory, branch or files changes. The Daemon checks a Worktree again after its Agent's stop, as G5 says, and keeps the Agent on a change.
- **Todos.** A done Todo whose Home is the Workstream is deleted, with its Touch and `todo.deleted`. An open Todo gets `home: null` as T13 says, with reason `open`. A Todo another Todo is blocked by is judged by its own `done`. A kept Todo whose blocker is deleted loses that blocker, as `todo.setBlockers` would.
- **Pads.** A Pad whose Home is the Workstream and whose owner is a kept Agent gets `home: null`, as `pad.move` does. Every other Pad of the Workstream, owned by the user, by a removed Agent or by no Agent, is deleted with its Touch and `pad.deleted`.
- **Terminals.** The Workstream's Terminals close as X12 says.
- **Order.** The Door stops first, as A16 says. Then the Daemon judges every Agent, Todo and Pad before it changes anything; if a judgement fails, the call returns the error and everything stays as it was. Then it removes and moves, and last deletes the Workstream. `rail.changed`, `todo.updated`, `todo.deleted`, `pad.changed` and `pad.deleted` follow once the Workstream is gone.
- **Failure.** A failed removal changes nothing, as A16 says, and a second call retries: nothing is removed twice and nothing kept is lost.
- **Unchanged.** Todos and Pads of other Workstreams and of the Project root, and every Worktree outside the Workstream, are untouched.
