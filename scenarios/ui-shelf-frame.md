# UI: the Shelf is a full-height overlay with two tabs (U368 to U373)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U368, U369, U370, U371, U372, U373.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #687: "This view looks to undesigned and unusable … I think it should be 2 tabs and the pad/todo side should start from the top and cover the upcoming tabs for projects … the pad should have shadow to reflect the scroll and overlay nature … that right sidebar should be collapsible … toggle it with a shortcut … open it to quickly scan and take a glance on what are we doing and what info did we acquire." Today the Shelf is the third column of the grid, `grid-template-columns: clamp(252px, 20%, 320px) minmax(690px, 1fr) clamp(160px, 14%, 280px)` (`apps/desktop/src/styles.css:55`), below the header, and it stacks the `todos` and `pads` lists in one column (U172); it cannot be hidden.

Observer: rendered-page checks against the fake App (U131, `.agents/data/harness.md`), light and dark, on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run` (U26) at 1280 by 820 and 700 by 800. Never a native Mac capture. The frame is designed with `emil-design-eng` and recorded in `docs/design-system.md` (Surfaces and depth, Click targets, Tokens) and `docs/motion.md`, in the same PR as this file.

Engineering calls, each made here so the implementation run asks nothing:

- **Overlay, not a column.** The Shelf is `position: absolute` against the window, from the top edge to the bottom, at the trailing edge, above the centre pane in paint order and below the Drawer. The grid is the Rail and the centre pane only, so the pane's width is the same open or closed and a toggle sends no `terminal.resize` (the Drawer's rule, U111).
- **Width.** `--shelf-width`, a Token, default 320 px, capped at `calc(100% - 252px)` so the pane keeps room at 640 px (S7, U85). #622 makes it user-resizable; it changes this Token's value, not this file's rules.
- **Chord.** `⌘\`. It is free on `main`: U32 (`⌘N`, `⌘T`, `⇧⌘N`), U41 (`⌘1`, `⌘2`), U51 and U186 (`⌘K`, `⌘J`, `⇧⌘J`, `⇧⌘[`, `⇧⌘]`), quick-terminal (`⌘B`, `⇧⌘B`, `⌘.`, `⇧⌘⌫`) and U54 (`?`) never use it, and `\` sits beside `]`, which U186 uses for tabs.
- **Tabs, not a heading pair.** `todos +` and `pads +` heads (U172) become two tabs; each tab's `+` add button (U170 kind **add**) moves into the open list's first line.

**U373 the Shelf has two tabs.** Narrows U15, U18 and U172's two head-groups. Given the Shelf, then it holds a `role="tablist"` with two `role="tab"` buttons, `Todos` then `Pads`, and one `role="tabpanel"` for the open tab; the other list is not in the DOM. The open tab has `aria-selected="true"` and full ink (`--text`), the other is `--grey` (U4's 4.5:1), with no box, border, background or underline; each tab shows a quiet count after its name in `--text-caption` `--grey`: `Todos` the number of open Todos the list shows (U159's scope applied), `Pads` the number of Pads it shows, and no count when it is `0`. `←`/`→` move between the two tabs when focus is on a tab, `Home`/`End` select the first and last, and the tabs are in the Tab order before the list. The list of the open tab keeps every clause of U15, U18, U152 to U158, U359 and U360, including its `+` add button as the list's first line and the `N done` fold of the Todos. A new Project opens on `Todos`. The open tab is kept per Project as U100 keeps layout (the webview's saved state, keyed by path), is restored in the frame that first shows the tree with no tab-switch animation, and a stored value that is neither tab means `Todos`. **U368 the scope control is designed and belongs to the Todos tab.** Narrows U159, and U170's quiet kind and the Click targets row of `docs/design-system.md` (a quiet button is surfaceless, except that a pressed one in a `role="group"` of `aria-pressed` buttons takes `--selected`). Given a Workstream selected on the Rail, then the `all` / `this one` choice shows as one segmented control on the first line of the `Todos` tab's panel, above the list and never in the `Pads` panel: a `role="group"` named `show Todos` holding two **quiet** buttons (U170; `data-button="quiet"`), `all` and `this one`, each with `aria-pressed`, exactly one `"true"` (`all` by default). The pressed one is `--text` on `--selected` with `--radius-control`, the other `--grey`; both are drawn by `buttons.css` from Tokens, with U170's rest, hover, pressed, focus and disabled states and a hit area of at least 24 px. `←`/`→` move focus between the two while it is in the group, `Space` and `Enter` press the focused one, and a press shows the narrowed or full list at once with no animation. The control is `--font-ui`. There is no `input[type=radio]` anywhere in the Shelf. Every other clause of U159 holds: the control is not rendered with no Workstream selected or with an Agent or Terminal selected (U159's own clause), `this one` lists what U159 says, and the counts of U373 follow it. **U369 the Shelf starts at the window's top edge.** Narrows U161 (the Shelf's top hairline) and U179. Given the Shelf open, then its box's top is `0` and its bottom is the window's bottom, at 1280 by 820 and at 700 by 800; the Project tab strip (U179) ends at the Shelf's leading edge, so the strip's right edge equals the Shelf's left edge within 1 px, and the strip scrolls sideways within that width as U184 says (its `+` stays in view at the strip's right end, which is now the Shelf's leading edge). With the Shelf closed (U370) the strip's right edge is the leading edge of the `show Shelf` button at the window's trailing edge of the header (U179's header gains that one button; the strip's `+` stays in view and is never covered), and the Shelf's box is not in the layout (`display: none` after the close ends). The Shelf's first line is the tablist of U373, aligned with the header's height; the Shelf has no top hairline, and one `--hairline` on its leading edge instead (D8 allows it, U371). At 700 by 800 the Shelf still overlays and the Rail and pane do not move. **U370 the Shelf closes and opens, by button and by chord.** Given an open Project, then a **icon button** (U170; `aria-label` `hide Shelf` when open and `show Shelf` when closed, `aria-expanded`) in the Shelf's tablist line closes it, and the same button, kept at the window's trailing edge in the header while the Shelf is closed, opens it. `⌘\` toggles it from the Rail, the pane, a Drawer, a text field or a Terminal alike, with U32's chord rules: handled by the webview and never reaching a Terminal, Caps Lock neutral, every other modifier set (`⇧⌘\`, `⌥⌘\`, `⌃⌘\`, `\` alone) does nothing, and a held chord acts once. Help (U54) gains `⌘\  show or hide the Shelf` after `⇧⌘J`'s line. Opening moves focus to the open tab, closing returns it to where it was (the Rail, the pane or the Terminal; the body when that is gone). `esc` closes an open Shelf only when focus is inside it and no Drawer or dialog is open. Whether it is open is kept per Project as U100 keeps layout; it is restored in the frame that first shows the tree, with no animation, and a new Project opens with the Shelf **open**. A toggle sends no `terminal.resize`, no `rail.*` call and no other call: the fake App records none. **U371 the Shelf is an overlay, and says so with shadow.** Narrows D8 and U134 ("only the Drawer has a shadow"). Given the Shelf open, then it paints over the centre pane's trailing part without changing the pane's box, a Terminal's size or the Rail (U111's rule for the Drawer, here for the Shelf), and below any Drawer, the switcher (U51), Help and a dialog, which cover it. Its leading edge casts one shadow, the Token `--shadow-shelf` (`0 0 24px rgba(0,0,0,.10)` light, `0 0 24px rgba(0,0,0,.45)` dark; the Drawer's `--shadow-drawer` is at `apps/desktop/src/tokens.css:17`), and the Shelf's ground is `--sunken`. Each list's scroll area shows a scroll shadow on an edge only where content is clipped there: a `--shadow-scroll` (`inset 0 8px 8px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.18)` light, `inset 0 8px 8px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.6)` dark, mirrored for the bottom edge) at the top once scrolled down, at the bottom while more is below, none at the top at scroll `0` or at the bottom at the end, and none when nothing is clipped; it is drawn without `backdrop-filter`. D8 becomes: no border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline`; only the Drawer and the Shelf have a shadow (`--shadow-drawer`, `--shadow-shelf`), and a list's scroll shadow is `--shadow-scroll` (a bubble has none); no `backdrop-filter` element contains another. D1 reads every one of these as a Token value, and `docs/design-system.md` records the three Tokens. **U372 what the Shelf shows follows #676's layout.** Narrows U359. Motion (`docs/motion.md`, `animate`): opening and closing the Shelf is a slide from the trailing edge, `transform: translateX`, **~180 ms ease-out** on `--duration-drawer`, the exit its reverse and interruptible; the shadow fades with it on `opacity` of the same duration. A tab switch is instant (seen constantly, like a Glyph change), and neither a restart nor `prefers-reduced-motion` animates anything. Check D9 reads it.
