# UI: the full-height Shelf keeps the centre readable (U369 to U373)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U369, U370, U371, U372, U373.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. U369 and U371's geometry is amended for #1612; their earlier fixed-pane overlay acceptance is replaced. The remaining tab, depth and motion acceptance stays in force.

Observer: isolated headless Chromium against the real U26 harness (`.agents/data/harness.md`), light and dark for behavioral observers (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), at 1512×950 on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run`. Measure boxes after the slide settles; allow at most 1 px rounding. The fallback is exercised at 1280×800 by resizing the Rail to 480 px and the Shelf to 560 px (U389, U390), not by introducing an unsupported window width (U453). No native Mac observation is required.

Engineering calls:

- **Room.** Let available width be the window's content width less the current Rail width and the current Shelf width. If available width is at least 388 CSS px, opening the Shelf reserves its width for the centre. Below 388 px it overlays the centre with a scrim. Equality uses the reserved-width mode. The minimum reuses U389's existing centre floor (`apps/desktop/src/styles.css:105` on main); implementation expresses it as a Token and uses that single value for the track and mode decision. A Drawer reserves its own width under U430; reservations are never added together.
- **Width.** `--shelf-width`, a Token, default 320 px, capped at the window less the Rail's width so the Rail is never covered (S7, U85); the user resizes it between 240 and 560 px (U390).
- **Chord.** `⌘K B` under U2239 (#4966); the previous leader S and earlier direct `⌘\` are retired. The scoped direct Terminal `⌘B` remains distinct.
- **Tabs, not a heading pair.** `todos +` and `pads +` heads (U172) become two tabs; each tab's `+` add button (U170 kind **add**) moves into the open list's first line.

**U373 the Shelf has two tabs.** Narrows U15, U18 and U172's two head-groups. Given the App, the window bar trailing end holds a `role="tablist"` with two `role="tab"` buttons, `Todos` then `Pads`; the Shelf holds one `role="tabpanel"` for the open tab; the other list is not in the DOM. The tabs remain in the bar whether the Shelf is open or closed, including when the Project connection fails (`u373_tabs_remain_available_when_the_project_connection_fails`). A click on a closed tab opens the Shelf on it; a click on the open tab closes the Shelf and returns focus as U370 says; a click on the other tab switches lists. Enter and Space act once as a click does, with no animation (U2580a). The open tab has `aria-selected="true"` and `aria-expanded="true"`; closed, all tabs have `aria-expanded="false"` and none is selected. Tests: `u373_a_bar_tab_opens_the_shelf_on_it`, `u373_the_open_tab_closes_it`, `u373_the_other_tab_switches`. There is no floating Open/Close the Bench button, open or closed (`u373_no_floating_toggle`). With no Project the tabs have no counts and open the empty Shelf (`u373_first_run_tabs_open_the_empty_shelf_without_counts`). A tab press with a Drawer open has the same effect as leader B (`u373_bar_tabs_toggle_with_a_drawer_open`). The bar renders one tab per `SHELF_TABS` entry; adding a future tab requires one entry, no plugin interface. Each tab shows a quiet count after its name: `Todos` the number of open Todos the list shows (U2744a scope, including items in folded groups), `Pads` the number of all Pads in the current Project, independent of Rail selection and including folded groups (U2744a), and no count when it is `0`. `u373_counts_follow_the_scope` in `apps/desktop/src/shelf/u373_frame.test.tsx` asserts the scoped Todo count and Project-wide Pad count across Overview, Workstream and Agent selection; extend it to Terminal selection and folded groups. The Pad count uses only the current Project, including when the Shelf is closed; first-run still has no counts. This amends only U373's Pad-count clause (#4533). `←`/`→` move between the two tabs when focus is on a tab, `Home`/`End` select the first and last, and Tab from the open bar tab reaches the Shelf’s first control (the add button, then the list and its disclosures), with Shift+Tab returning to the tab (`u373_the_tabs_come_before_the_list_in_the_tab_order`). The list of the open tab keeps every clause of U15, U18, U152 to U158, U359 and U360, including its `+` add button as the list's first line and the `N done` fold of the Todos. A new Project opens on `Todos`. The open tab is kept per Project as U100 keeps layout (the webview's saved state, keyed by path), is restored in the frame that first shows the tree with no tab-switch animation, and a stored value that is neither tab means `Todos`. Tests (`u373_`): `u373_two_tabs_one_panel`, `u373_the_other_tab_switches`, `u373_counts_follow_the_scope`, `u373_a_zero_count_shows_nothing`, `u373_arrow_keys_home_and_end`, `u373_the_open_tab_survives_a_restart_per_project`, `u373_a_bad_stored_value_means_todos`, `u373_add_keeps_its_path`.

**U369 the Shelf starts at the window's top edge.** Narrows U161 (the Shelf's top hairline) and U179. With the Shelf closed (U370) the Shelf's box is not in the layout (`display: none` after the close ends). The Shelf has no top hairline, and one `--hairline` on its leading edge instead (D8 allows it, U371). A closed Shelf is `hidden`. Tests (`u369_`): `u369_closed_shelf_releases_its_width`.


**U370 the Bench closes and opens, by bar tab and by chord.** Owner amendment #4966: Shelf remains the code term; its user label is Bench, with Open the Bench when closed and Close the Bench when open across menus, Help, hints and accessibility labels (policy: critic audits exact copy). Given an open Project, the window-bar tabs retain U373's open/switch/close behavior. `⌘K B` toggles from Rail, pane, Drawer, field or Terminal under U2239's guards, neutral to Caps Lock and acting once on repeat, with no Terminal input. `⌘K S` is retired, and scoped direct `⌘B` retains U2742c. Opening focuses the active tab; closing returns to prior focus, falling back to the surviving Rail/Ask target. Escape closes only with focus inside Bench and no Drawer/dialog or active text-field Escape owner; preserve U371's overlay exception. Per-Project open/closed persistence and restore timing remain unchanged; this rename changes no initial layout default. A toggle sends only the Terminal resize required by its mode, no unrelated RPC. Extend `u370_leader_s_toggles_from_every_focus`, `u370_leader_s_toggles_from_a_terminal_and_never_reaches_it` and `u370_leader_s_toggles_with_a_drawer_open` to B and rename their suffixes to `leader_b`, preserving their proofs; retain `u370_focus_returns_where_it_was`, `u370_esc_closes_only_from_inside`, `u370_the_state_survives_a_restart_per_project` and the U373 tab observers. U2239's unbound-key observer proves retired S performs nothing. No exact-label or layout assertions in UI tests.

U373 #4966 policy amendment: the Bench holds Todos and Pads, still one tabpanel at a time, and retains the existing `SHELF_TABS` extension point for later plugin tabs. No plugin interface, plugin tab or additional tab is commissioned. Existing tab counts, Project persistence, first-run behavior, add paths and Todo/Pad ownership remain unchanged. Retain `u373_counts_follow_the_scope`, `u373_the_other_tab_switches`, `u373_tab_navigation_stays_on_tabs` and `u373_the_open_tab_survives_a_restart_per_project` in `apps/desktop/src/shelf/u373_frame.test.tsx`. Rendered Bench labels and chord discoverability use U504's shared #4966 headless observer, 1512×950 dark, with an action failure visible; Moves D2/D5/D6, only Tokens and no passing-main regression.

**U371 the Shelf leaves the centre readable, with an overlay fallback.** Replaces its earlier unchanged-pane and no-Terminal-resize clauses, U370's no-`terminal.resize` clause for reserved-width mode and U370's inside-only Escape rule for overlay mode; U370's other calls, chord, persistence and focus-return acceptance remain. Given an open Project, then:

- **Reserved width.** With available width at least 388 px and no Drawer, a selected Terminal keeps every Check passing. The design critic judges the Checks here (AGENTS.md rule 10).
- **Boundary and resize.** Available widths of 387, 388 and 389 px select overlay, reserved width and reserved width respectively. Resizing either panel or restoring saved widths recomputes the mode; crossing the boundary preserves the selected Rail node. Tests: `u371_minimum_pane_width_selects_the_mode`, `u371_resizing_preserves_the_selection`.
- **Fallback.** Below the minimum, a scrim over the centre closes the Shelf on click. Escape closes it from the centre or Shelf, with no Terminal input sent; an active field inside the Shelf consumes Escape first as U370 says. With a Drawer or dialog open, that surface handles Escape first. There is no scrim in reserved-width mode or after closing. Tests: `u371_overlay_has_a_scrim`, `u371_scrim_and_escape_close_the_shelf`, `u371_drawer_and_dialog_keep_escape_priority`, `u371_escape_closes_the_drawer_before_the_shelf`.
- **Terminal.** In reserved-width mode a visible Terminal refits and receives exactly one `terminal.resize` after opening and one after closing, rather than one per animation frame (U430's existing convention). In overlay mode it receives none. No `rail.*` or unrelated RPC is sent. Tests: `u371_terminal_resizes_once_per_reserved_slide`, `u371_overlay_sends_no_terminal_resize`, `u370_a_toggle_calls_no_unrelated_rpc`.
- **Depth.** Each list's scroll area shows a scroll shadow on an edge only where content is clipped there: a `--shadow-scroll` (`inset 0 8px 8px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.18)` light, `inset 0 8px 8px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.6)` dark, mirrored for the bottom edge) at the top once scrolled down, at the bottom while more is below, none at the top at scroll `0` or at the bottom at the end, and none when nothing is clipped. D8 becomes: no border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline`; only the Drawer and the Shelf have a shadow (`--shadow-drawer`, `--shadow-shelf`), and a list's scroll shadow is `--shadow-scroll` (a bubble has none); no `backdrop-filter` element contains another. D1 reads every one of these as a Token value, and `docs/design-system.md` records the three Tokens. Tests for depth (`u371_`): `u371_scroll_shadow_top_bottom_and_none`, `u371_d8_passes_with_the_new_shadows`.

**Scrim**: The darkened surface over the centre while the Shelf overlays it.

**U372 what the Shelf shows follows #676's layout.** Narrows U359. Given Todo titles, Pad names and a Pad's text written by an Agent (Q2's list is the owner of which surfaces; the Shelf's are named there when #676's scenarios merge), then in the Shelf each renders as Q3 lays it out: one labelled part per field, no mono except an id, a path or code (D13), and a Pad's preview or any text beyond a row's two lines is shown whole in its Drawer, not in the Shelf. The design critic judges D12, D13 and D14 in the overlay (AGENTS.md rule 10); no test.

Motion (`docs/motion.md`, `animate`): opening and closing the Shelf is a slide from the trailing edge, `transform: translateX`, **~180 ms ease-out** on `--duration-drawer`, the exit its reverse and interruptible; the shadow fades with it on `opacity` of the same duration. A tab switch is instant (seen constantly, like a Glyph change), and neither a restart nor `prefers-reduced-motion` animates anything. U371 retains this Shelf slide and shadow fade. In reserved-width mode the centre releases or reserves width with the same duration and curve; in overlay mode it does not animate. Tests: `u371_overlay_scrim_fades_with_the_interruptible_slide`, `u371_reduced_motion_and_restore_are_instant`.
