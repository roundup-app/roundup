# UI: the full-height Shelf keeps the centre readable (U368 to U373)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U368, U369, U370, U371, U372, U373.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. U369 and U371's geometry is amended for #1612; their earlier fixed-pane overlay acceptance is replaced. The remaining tab, scope, depth and motion acceptance stays in force.

Observer: isolated headless Chromium against the real U26 harness (`.agents/data/harness.md`), light and dark, at 1512×950 on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run`. Measure boxes after the slide settles; allow at most 1 px rounding. The fallback is exercised at 1280×800 by resizing the Rail to 480 px and the Shelf to 560 px (U389, U390), not by introducing an unsupported window width (U453). No native Mac observation is required.

Engineering calls:

- **Room.** Let available width be the window's content width less the current Rail width and the current Shelf width. If available width is at least 388 CSS px, opening the Shelf reserves its width for the centre. Below 388 px it overlays the centre with a scrim. Equality uses the reserved-width mode. The minimum reuses U389's existing centre floor (`apps/desktop/src/styles.css:105` on main); implementation expresses it as a Token and uses that single value for the track and mode decision. A Drawer reserves its own width under U430; reservations are never added together.
- **Width.** `--shelf-width`, a Token, default 320 px, capped at the window less the Rail's width so the Rail is never covered (S7, U85); the user resizes it between 240 and 560 px (U390).
- **Chord.** `⌘\`. It is free on `main`: U32 (`⌘N`, `⌘T`, `⇧⌘N`), U41 (`⌘1`, `⌘2`), U51 and U186 (`⌘K`, `⌘J`, `⇧⌘J`, `⇧⌘[`, `⇧⌘]`), quick-terminal (`⌘B`, `⇧⌘B`, `⌘.`, `⇧⌘⌫`) and U54 (`?`) never use it, and `\` sits beside `]`, which U186 uses for tabs.
- **Tabs, not a heading pair.** `todos +` and `pads +` heads (U172) become two tabs; each tab's `+` add button (U170 kind **add**) moves into the open list's first line.

**U373 the Shelf has two tabs.** Narrows U15, U18 and U172's two head-groups. Given the Shelf, then it holds a `role="tablist"` with two `role="tab"` buttons, `Todos` then `Pads`, and one `role="tabpanel"` for the open tab; the other list is not in the DOM. The open tab has `aria-selected="true"` and full ink (`--text`), the other is `--grey` (U4's 4.5:1), with no box, border, background or underline; each tab shows a quiet count after its name in `--text-caption` `--grey`: `Todos` the number of open Todos the list shows (U159's scope applied), `Pads` the number of Pads it shows, and no count when it is `0`. `←`/`→` move between the two tabs when focus is on a tab, `Home`/`End` select the first and last, and the tabs are in the Tab order before the list. The list of the open tab keeps every clause of U15, U18, U152 to U158, U359 and U360, including its `+` add button as the list's first line and the `N done` fold of the Todos. A new Project opens on `Todos`. The open tab is kept per Project as U100 keeps layout (the webview's saved state, keyed by path), is restored in the frame that first shows the tree with no tab-switch animation, and a stored value that is neither tab means `Todos`. Tests (`u373_`): `u373_two_tabs_one_panel`, `u373_the_other_list_is_not_in_the_dom`, `u373_counts_follow_the_scope`, `u373_a_zero_count_shows_nothing`, `u373_arrow_keys_home_and_end`, `u373_the_open_tab_survives_a_restart_per_project`, `u373_a_bad_stored_value_means_todos`, `u373_add_keeps_its_path`.

**U368 the scope control is designed and belongs to the Todos tab.** Overturned by U470 for the control's placement, shape, labels and keyboard handling; U347 still owns its scope and default. Narrows U159, and U170's quiet kind and the Click targets row of `docs/design-system.md` (a quiet button is surfaceless, except that a pressed one in a `role="group"` of `aria-pressed` buttons takes `--selected`). Given a Workstream selected on the Rail, then the `All` / `This one` choice shows as one segmented control on the first line of the `Todos` tab's panel, above the list and never in the `Pads` panel: a `role="group"` named `Show Todos` holding two **quiet** buttons (U170; `data-button="quiet"`), `All` and `This one`, each with `aria-pressed`, exactly one `"true"` (`All` by default). The pressed one is `--text` on `--selected` with `--radius-control`, the other `--grey`; both are drawn by `buttons.css` from Tokens, with U170's rest, hover, pressed, focus and disabled states and a hit area of at least 24 px. `←`/`→` move focus between the two while it is in the group, `Space` and `Enter` press the focused one, and a press shows the narrowed or full list at once with no animation. The control is `--font-ui`. There is no `input[type=radio]` anywhere in the Shelf. Every other clause of U159 holds: the control is not rendered with no Workstream selected or with an Agent or Terminal selected (U159's own clause), `This one` lists what U159 says, and the counts of U373 follow it. Tests (`u368_`): `u368_no_radio_input_in_the_shelf`, `u368_two_quiet_buttons_one_pressed`, `u368_it_is_in_the_todos_panel_only`, `u368_arrow_and_space_press`, `u368_this_one_narrows_the_list_and_the_count`, `u368_absent_without_a_workstream`, `u368_ui_font_and_hit_area`.

**U369 the Shelf starts at the window's top edge.** Narrows U161 (the Shelf's top hairline) and U179. Given the Shelf open, then its box's top is `0` and its bottom is the window's bottom, at 1280 by 820 and at 1280 by 800; the Project tab strip (U179) ends at the Shelf's leading edge, so the strip's right edge equals the Shelf's left edge within 1 px, and the strip scrolls sideways within that width as U184 says (its `+` stays in view at the strip's right end, which is now the Shelf's leading edge). **U370 the Shelf closes and opens, by button and by chord.** _Retired in part by U2238 to U2241: `⌘\` is leader `S` (U2239)._ Given an open Project, then a **icon button** (U170; `aria-label` `Hide work items` when open and `Show work items` when closed, `aria-expanded`) in the Shelf's tablist line closes it, and the same button, kept at the window's trailing edge in the header while the Shelf is closed, opens it. `⌘\` toggles it from the Rail, the pane, a Drawer, a text field or a Terminal alike, with U32's chord rules: handled by the webview and never reaching a Terminal, Caps Lock neutral, every other modifier set (`⇧⌘\`, `⌥⌘\`, `⌃⌘\`, `\` alone) does nothing, and a held chord acts once. Help (U54) gains `⌘\  Show or hide the work items` after `⇧⌘J`'s line. Opening moves focus to the open tab, closing returns it to where it was (the Rail, the pane or the Terminal; the body when that is gone). `esc` closes an open Shelf only when focus is inside it and no Drawer or dialog is open, and not while focus is in a text field inside it (the `Add Pad` naming field uses `esc` first). Whether it is open is kept per Project as U100 keeps layout; it is restored in the frame that first shows the tree, with no animation, and a new Project opens with the Shelf **open**. U371 replaces this scenario's earlier no-resize rule: a toggle sends only the Terminal resize required by its mode, never `rail.*` or an unrelated call. Tests (`u370_`): `u370_the_button_closes_and_opens`, `u370_the_chord_toggles_from_every_focus`, `u370_other_modifier_sets_do_nothing`, `u370_a_held_chord_acts_once`, `u370_a_toggle_calls_no_unrelated_rpc`, `u370_focus_returns_where_it_was`, `u370_esc_closes_only_from_inside`, `u370_the_state_survives_a_restart_per_project`, `u370_help_lists_the_chord`.

**U371 the Shelf leaves the centre readable, with an overlay fallback.** Replaces its earlier unchanged-pane and no-Terminal-resize clauses, U370's no-`terminal.resize` clause for reserved-width mode and U370's inside-only Escape rule for overlay mode; U370's other calls, chord, persistence and focus-return acceptance remain. Given an open Project, then:

- **Reserved width.** With available width at least 388 px and no Drawer, a selected Terminal keeps every Check passing. Test: `u371_terminal_remains_visible`.
- **Boundary and resize.** Available widths of 387, 388 and 389 px select overlay, reserved width and reserved width respectively. Resizing either panel or restoring saved widths recomputes the mode; crossing the boundary preserves the selected Rail node. Tests: `u371_minimum_pane_width_selects_the_mode`, `u371_resizing_preserves_the_selection`.
- **Fallback.** Below the minimum, a scrim over the centre closes the Shelf on click. Escape closes it from the centre or Shelf, with no Terminal input sent; an active field inside the Shelf consumes Escape first as U370 says. With a Drawer or dialog open, that surface handles Escape first. There is no scrim in reserved-width mode or after closing. Tests: `u371_overlay_has_a_scrim`, `u371_scrim_and_escape_close_the_shelf`, `u371_drawer_and_dialog_keep_escape_priority`, `u371_escape_closes_the_drawer_before_the_shelf`.
- **Terminal.** In reserved-width mode a visible Terminal refits and receives exactly one `terminal.resize` after opening and one after closing, rather than one per animation frame (U430's existing convention). In overlay mode it receives none. No `rail.*` or unrelated RPC is sent. Tests: `u371_terminal_resizes_once_per_reserved_slide`, `u371_overlay_sends_no_terminal_resize`, `u370_a_toggle_calls_no_unrelated_rpc`.
- **Depth.** Each list's scroll area shows a scroll shadow on an edge only where content is clipped there: a `--shadow-scroll` (`inset 0 8px 8px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.18)` light, `inset 0 8px 8px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.6)` dark, mirrored for the bottom edge) at the top once scrolled down, at the bottom while more is below, none at the top at scroll `0` or at the bottom at the end, and none when nothing is clipped. D8 becomes: no border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline`; only the Drawer and the Shelf have a shadow (`--shadow-drawer`, `--shadow-shelf`), and a list's scroll shadow is `--shadow-scroll` (a bubble has none); no `backdrop-filter` element contains another. D1 reads every one of these as a Token value, and `docs/design-system.md` records the three Tokens. Tests for depth (`u371_`): `u371_scroll_shadow_top_bottom_and_none`, `u371_d8_passes_with_the_new_shadows`.

**U372 what the Shelf shows follows #676's layout.** Narrows U359. A tab switch is instant (seen constantly, like a Glyph change), and neither a restart nor `prefers-reduced-motion` animates anything. U371 retains this Shelf slide and shadow fade. In reserved-width mode the centre releases or reserves width with the same duration and curve; in overlay mode it does not animate. Tests: `u371_overlay_scrim_fades_with_the_interruptible_slide`, `u371_reduced_motion_and_restore_are_instant`.
