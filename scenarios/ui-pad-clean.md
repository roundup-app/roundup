# UI: the Pad shows no Saved caption, focuses on a click, and draws clean (U7414a to U7414c)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U7414a–U7414c.

Work order: [#7414](https://github.com/roundup-app/roundup/issues/7414). Owner, 2026-11-17: "what is that saved word it's useless - by default the app saves! that space is wasted", "an empty pad I should be able to click anywhere in the center to focus on the pad and show the typing cursor", "for drawings there should be a way to hide controls".

Policy: webview only; no App command, Daemon method or contract changes. Reuse: Excalidraw's own `viewModeEnabled`, `zenModeEnabled` and `UIOptions` hide its tools, never CSS (rule 9). No test checks looks; the design critic judges the head's toolbar spacing and the clean drawing at 1512×950, dark, from `just capture U7414a U7414c`.

**U7414a the Pad shows no Saved caption.** Retires U3729's `Saved` caption (`scenarios/ui-shelf-design.md`, heading **U3729**) and the `Saved` exception of U7310d (`scenarios/ui-item-view.md`, heading **U7310d**); the rest of U3729 holds. Given an open Pad whose last write stands, then no `Saved` text and no `role="status"` line show in its head or body, no blank line stays in its place, and the toolbar sits directly under the head. A failed save still shows as a toast (U7310d), with the draft kept. Test: `u7414a_the_pad_shows_no_saved_caption` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/` (after a successful write, no element reads `Saved`; after a failed write, one toast with Dismiss shows). `PadSavedStatus` and `copy.padSaved` are deleted.

**U7414b a click in the Pad's body focuses the editor.** Given an editable Pad with no text, when the user clicks anywhere in the Pad's body area, then the editor takes focus and shows the typing cursor at the start. Given a Pad with text, when the user clicks in the body area below the last block, then the cursor is at the end of the text. A click on a block, link or control keeps its own action. A read-only Pad takes no focus from the click. Test: `u7414b_a_click_in_the_body_focuses_the_editor` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/` (empty Pad: a click on the body area below the editor's first line focuses the editor with the selection at 0; a Pad with text: the selection ends at the text's end; a read-only Pad stays unfocused).

**U7414c a Pad drawing shows clean until Edit.** Narrows U149 (`scenarios/ui-rail.md`, heading **U149**). Given an editable Pad with a drawing block, when it renders, then the drawing shows with no Excalidraw tool bars and no Edit-less editing. The block offers Edit; when the user activates it, the tools show and the drawing edits as U149 says. When the user presses Esc or clicks outside the block, it returns to clean, and every change made stays saved as U149 says. A read-only Pad's drawing is always clean and offers no Edit. Lost: hiding the tools only in the export. Tests: `u7414c_edit_toggles_the_drawings_tools` and `u7414c_escape_leaves_drawing_edit` in `apps/desktop/src/pads/` (Edit shows the tools, Esc and an outside click hide them, and the scene written stays the one edited).
