# UI: buttons and the sidebar (U170 to U173)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U170–U173, U386.

Webview only. No App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #400: buttons are neither designed nor defined, and Solo's sidebar is the bar the user named, "elegant, well designed and tasteful". **U170 five button kinds, each defined once.** Given `docs/design-system.md`, when a test reads its "Click targets" section and every stylesheet under `apps/desktop/src`, then the section defines exactly five kinds: **primary** (`--accent` label, the one action of a state), **quiet** (`--grey` label at rest, `--text` on hover, no surface at rest), **add** (a `+` mark and a label, quiet, trailing a list or group), **row action** (a word or mark shown on a row's hover, focus or selection, and always reachable by keyboard), and **icon button** (a 16 px mark in a 24 px square, with an `aria-label`). Each kind's rest, hover, pressed, focus and disabled style is a pure function of Tokens: one rule per kind in one stylesheet, `buttons.css`, and no other stylesheet sets a `background`, `color`, `border-radius` or `padding` on a `button` element. Every `button`, `[role=button]` and click target in the Rail, Shelf, Drawer, Thread and centre carries one `data-button` attribute naming its kind, and a click target with none fails. A row is not a button: a `treeitem` row of the Rail, a Shelf row and a Todo's title (U139) are exempt, take no `data-button`, and are styled only by U172's Item, Selection and Workstream-row clauses; any button inside a row (its row action, its chevron) is still one of the five kinds. New Tokens (a pressed surface, a disabled label tone) are added to the table in `docs/design-system.md` with a light and a dark value, and U130 still holds. Test: `u170_`.

**U171 every kind shows every state.** Given the five kinds rendered together on a fixture page in light and in dark, when a test drives each by `pointerenter`, `pointerdown`, `focus-visible` and `aria-disabled`, then for each kind: hover differs from rest in background or colour; pressed differs from hover, takes effect at `pointerdown` and has `transition-duration: 0s`; focus is the one `:focus-visible` ring of U133, never clipped by its parent (the ring's box lies inside the nearest clipping ancestor, or that ancestor does not clip it); disabled has the disabled tone at 4.5:1 against its ground, cursor `default` and no hover or pressed change; the hit area is at least 24 px high and no two hit areas in one row overlap (D6, D7). A disabled button stays focusable and keeps `aria-disabled`, never the `disabled` attribute, so its reason can be read as its `title`. The kinds differ from one another: no two kinds have the same rest style. Test: `u171_`.

**U172 the sidebar's hierarchy.** Given the Rail and the Shelf on the seeds `agents-10` and `tree-40` (U26) in both schemes, when a test reads the rendered tree and computed style, then:

- **Workstream row.** A Workstream row has, in order: a square badge of the Workstream's first two letters (uppercase, `--text-caption`, `--radius-row`, on `--selected`), its name in bold `--text-body` with the ellipsis of U29, a disclosure chevron, and a right-aligned value in `--grey` (the count of its Agents that are not `done`). The badge, name and value share one baseline of 28 px or taller; the row is `--space-3` from its neighbours.
- **Groups.** The Rail's Groups (U3) are collapsible; the Shelf's lists have no head (U373's tabs name them). Each group head has a 16 px mark, its label in uppercase `--text-caption` with letter-spacing, a `--hairline` rule filling the rest of the line, and a count `<shown>/<total>` in `--grey`. Collapsing a group hides its rows, keeps the head and its count, and survives a restart as U100 says. The head's label is a `GLOSSARY.md` term, never a synonym from the reference.
- **Item.** An item row has a Kind Glyph (U132, the status dot's role), a title in bold `--text-body`, and below it a description in `--grey` `--text-caption` clamped to two lines with `…`. No key hint is drawn, since no chord addresses a single row; a hint is added only with such a chord. A row with no description is 28 px as U131 says; a row with one grows to hold it and never overlaps the next. A `needs-you` or `error` row is still the only bold-in-Ink row (U132): the title weight above is the item default and Ink is told by tone and Glyph, so from the implementing PR on, U172's title clause replaces U132's "only `needs-you` and `error` rows are bold" clause; that PR rewrites U132 and changes `u132_` in the same change, and names both in its body.
- **Selection.** The selected row has one mark: a `--selected` band with a 2 px `--accent` bar on its leading edge, drawn as a background (a pseudo-element or gradient), never a border (D8). No other row has a band or bar. Hover is `--hover` and never reads as selected.
- **Blocks.** One `--hairline` line sits between each block of the Rail (the Workstream rows, each group); U161's two lines remain, and the implementing PR widens U134's border rule in `docs/design-system.md` and in `scenarios/ui-visual.md` to name them. No border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline` (D8).
- **Density.** Rows have at least `--space-2` of vertical padding. Under U426 the Rail list keeps its leading `--space-4` inset and zero trailing inset (asserted in `u172_the_rail_keeps_its_leading_inset_and_a_row_is_28_tall_at_least`); other rows keep `--space-4` of horizontal padding. The Rail is never under 252 px (its minimum, U389), and `tree-40` at 252 px shows no horizontal scroll, no overlap and no clipped label (D10, U85).

**U471 the App reuses component behaviour.** Given the App's Solid build, when the foundation is installed, then `@ark-ui/solid` is a production dependency of `apps/desktop`, and `apps/desktop/src/ui/` exports Dialog, AlertDialog, Menu, Tooltip and IconButton. Dialog and AlertDialog compose Ark Dialog (AlertDialog sets `role="alertdialog"`); Menu and Tooltip compose their Ark components. The wrappers own Token styling and App defaults; the library owns focus, dismissal, keyboard navigation and ARIA. No wrapper implements a second focus trap or keyboard state machine (policy, inspected in the diff). IconButton requires a string `aria-label` in its TypeScript props: omitting it fails type checking and supplying it succeeds (`u471_icon_button_requires_a_name`, a compile-time fixture exercised by the package typecheck). Its rendered button has that accessible name and `data-button="icon"` (`u471_icon_button_has_its_name`).

Given the five wrappers on an isolated fixture, when keyboard and pointer drive them, then Dialog opens with a linked title and description, traps Tab and returns focus on Escape (`u471_dialog_focus_returns`); AlertDialog exposes `alertdialog` with its linked title and description (`u471_alert_dialog_is_named`); Menu opens from its trigger, arrows move between items, Enter invokes the focused item once and Escape returns focus to the trigger (`u471_menu_keyboard`); Tooltip opens on hover and keyboard focus, links its text to the trigger and disappears on leave or blur (`u471_tooltip_pointer_and_keyboard`). These are behavioural tests under `apps/desktop/src/ui/`, using library behaviour rather than mocked Ark components. Keep Solid, vanilla CSS, `tokens.css` and the five button kinds (policy). Every new public wrapper name is defined in `GLOSSARY.md` in the implementation PR (policy).
