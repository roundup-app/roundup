# UI: buttons and the sidebar (U170 to U173)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U170–U173, U386, U474–U475, U477, U483–U485.

Webview only. No App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #400: buttons are neither designed nor defined, and Solo's sidebar is the bar the user named, "elegant, well designed and tasteful". These scenarios narrow U133 (click-target states), U131 (type and space), U134 and U161 (surfaces and borders) and extend `docs/design-system.md` ("Click targets") with the button kinds below. **U170 five button kinds, each defined once.** Given `docs/design-system.md`, when a test reads its "Click targets" section and every stylesheet under `apps/desktop/src`, then the section defines exactly five kinds: **primary** (`--accent` label, the one action of a state), **quiet** (`--grey` label at rest, `--text` on hover, no surface at rest), **add** (a `+` mark and a label, quiet, trailing a list or group), **row action** (a word or mark shown on a row's hover, focus or selection, and always reachable by keyboard), and **icon button** (a 16 px mark in a 24 px square, with an `aria-label`). Each kind's rest, hover, pressed, focus and disabled style is a pure function of Tokens: one rule per kind in one stylesheet, `buttons.css`, and no other stylesheet sets a `background`, `color`, `border-radius` or `padding` on a `button` element. Every `button`, `[role=button]` and click target in the Rail, Shelf, Drawer, Thread and centre carries one `data-button` attribute naming its kind, and a click target with none fails. A row is not a button: a `treeitem` row of the Rail, a Shelf row and a Todo's title (U139) are exempt, take no `data-button`, and are styled only by U172's Item, Selection and Workstream-row clauses; any button inside a row (its row action, its chevron) is still one of the five kinds. New Tokens (a pressed surface, a disabled label tone) are added to the table in `docs/design-system.md` with a light and a dark value, and U130 still holds. Test: `u170_`.

**U171 every kind shows every state.** Given the five kinds rendered together on a fixture page in light and in dark, when a test drives each by `pointerenter`, `pointerdown`, `focus-visible` and `aria-disabled`, then for each kind: hover differs from rest in background or colour; pressed differs from hover, takes effect at `pointerdown` and has `transition-duration: 0s`; focus is the one `:focus-visible` ring of U133, never clipped by its parent (the ring's box lies inside the nearest clipping ancestor, or that ancestor does not clip it); disabled has the disabled tone at 4.5:1 against its ground, cursor `default` and no hover or pressed change; the hit area is at least 24 px high and no two hit areas in one row overlap (D6, D7). A disabled button stays focusable and keeps `aria-disabled`, never the `disabled` attribute, so its reason can be read as its `title`. The kinds differ from one another: no two kinds have the same rest style. Test: `u171_`.

Each group head has a 16 px mark, its label in uppercase `--text-caption` with letter-spacing, a `--hairline` rule filling the rest of the line, and a count `<shown>/<total>` in `--grey`. - **Selection.** The selected row has one mark: a `--selected` band with a 2 px `--accent` bar on its leading edge, drawn as a background (a pseudo-element or gradient), never a border (D8). No other row has a band or bar. Hover is `--hover` and never reads as selected.
- **Blocks.** One `--hairline` line sits between each block of the Rail (the Workstream rows, each group); U161's two lines remain, and the implementing PR widens U134's border rule in `docs/design-system.md` and in `scenarios/ui-visual.md` to name them. No border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline` (D8).
- **Density.** Rows have at least `--space-2` of vertical padding. Under U426 the Rail list keeps its leading `--space-4` inset and zero trailing inset (asserted in `u172_the_rail_keeps_its_leading_inset_and_a_row_is_28_tall_at_least`); other rows keep `--space-4` of horizontal padding. The Rail is never under 252 px (its minimum, U389), and `tree-40` at 252 px shows no horizontal scroll, no overlap and no clipped label (D10, U85).
