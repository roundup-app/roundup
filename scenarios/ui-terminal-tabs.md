# UI: Terminal tabs at the centre's bottom

Policy amendment — U4313a below keeps the Terminal bar mounted even with zero Terminals and moves the Rail’s New Terminal action to the bar, replacing U4274c’s Rail entry and nonempty-Project mounting condition. U4313b below restores the approved frame geometry. U4274c (`scenarios/ui-navigation-always.md`) restores the Place and stable height bar without a navigation request whenever the open Project has a Terminal, including a minimised or exited Terminal; tab eligibility still follows U469/U524. The shared bar/portal host and Terminal lifetimes stay stable. U2539b still keeps routine Resource values inside requested inspection. All tab actions, selection/ownership, keyboard access and failure recovery remain required. U524 (`scenarios/ui-terminal-open-root.md`) supersedes Open and missing selection's refusal: New Terminal opens at the Project root too. These amendments replace only the contradicted mounting and no-selection assertions (policy).

Module: `apps/desktop/src/bubbles` and `apps/desktop/src/resources`. Ids: U469, U534. Terms: Terminal, Bubble, Run, Workstream, Resource use (`GLOSSARY.md`). Direction: #1499, including the user's choice of bottom tabs and of a message when no Workstream is selected; Resource placement continues #1203.

Policy: this scenario supersedes U164's chip dock and simultaneous visible Bubbles, U165's silent no-selection opening and cycling through chips, U421's restoration alongside other Bubbles, and U444's separate Resource strip line. Their other clauses remain acceptance, including Run ownership, transparency, geometry, snapshots, Terminal lifetime, chord guards, Pull gestures and resource sampling. No RPC method, contract, App seam command or Token changes. The floating emulator remains a Bubble; a tab controls its visibility, not its Terminal's lifetime. No Alt-Tab binding or full-window maximise is required: the requested restore is showing the Bubble at its saved place and size.

**U469 bottom tabs control one Bubble at a time.** Given an open Project, then:

- **Place and stable height.** One bar is the centre stack's last line, below the Terminal pane, with the same side alignment as U444. Its left holds Terminal tabs and a trailing `+`; its right holds U444's Resource strip, showing only the selected running Agent's values, with no Project label or totals (#1963); with any other selection its resource region is empty. No chip dock or second resource line remains. Asserted by `u469_the_bar_is_the_last_line_and_no_dock_remains`.
- **Which tabs.** Every Terminal eligible under U164's selection rule has exactly one tab, whether its Bubble is shown or minimised, in U165's start order: the Workstream's Terminals when that Workstream or a row under it is selected, plus the selected Agent's Runs. An Agent's own pane, an ownerless Terminal and another Agent's Runs are not tabs. Each tab shows the same name and unseen-output dot (U3704) as U164's chip, plus `×`; an exit changes no mark and preserves its tab and readable last screen. Selecting another Workstream replaces the tabs with that Workstream's eligible Terminals and never closes the previous ones. Asserted by `u469_tabs_include_shown_and_minimised_terminals_in_start_order`, `u469_selection_swaps_workstream_tabs_and_only_the_selected_agents_runs`, and `u469_exit_updates_the_tab_and_keeps_the_last_screen`.
- **Show and minimise.** Activating a minimised Terminal's tab shows its Bubble at its saved place and size, focuses its emulator, and minimises any Bubble previously shown. Activating the shown Terminal's tab minimises it and leaves focus on that tab. The shown tab alone has `aria-pressed="true"`; every other tab has `aria-pressed="false"`. The Bubble's own Minimise uses the same state and keeps its tab. Minimising sends no close or kill call. At most one eligible Bubble is visible after every opening, restore, cycle, Pull, selection change or reload. Selection away and back preserves geometry and the remembered shown Terminal when still eligible; invalid saved states with several unminimised Bubbles resolve to the latest-started eligible one. A new Run adds its tab without moving selection or focus; it starts minimised if another Bubble is already shown, otherwise shows unfocused as U164 requires. Asserted by `u469_tabs_show_one_bubble_and_toggle_the_shown_one_off`, `u469_minimise_keeps_the_terminal_running_and_its_saved_geometry`, `u469_selection_and_reload_restore_at_most_one_bubble`, and `u469_a_new_run_keeps_selection_focus_and_the_existing_shown_bubble`.
- **Close.** Activating a tab's `×` uses the Bubble's existing Close path: one `terminal.close {id}` for that Terminal, even if running or minimised. It does not activate the adjacent tab or restore that Bubble, and duplicate activations while the close is pending make no second call. Success removes that Terminal's tab and Bubble; failure retains both states and shows the existing failure message. Closing the shown Bubble leaves the others minimised. When the closed control held focus, focus returns to the next remaining tab in start order, else the previous tab, else `+`; a failure keeps focus on the retained control. Asserted by `u469_tab_close_calls_once_without_toggling_or_restoring`, `u469_failed_close_keeps_the_tab_bubble_and_visible_failure`, and `u469_close_returns_focus_to_a_remaining_control`.
- **Open and missing selection.** The trailing `+`, named `New Terminal`, follows the same opening path as `⌘T`: one `terminal.open` for the resolved Workstream, with the returned Bubble shown and focused and the previous one minimised. U165's in-flight, chord and Daemon-exit guards and failure behavior remain. With an open Project but no selection resolving to a Workstream (including an empty Project), either activation makes no `terminal.open` call and shows exactly `Select a Workstream first` as an accessible status without moving focus. The `+` stays present when there are no tabs. A successful open or selection of a Workstream clears that message. Asserted by `u469_plus_and_cmd_t_share_one_opening_path_and_show_the_new_bubble`, `u469_plus_and_cmd_t_without_a_workstream_show_the_selection_message`, and `u469_open_guards_and_failures_preserve_the_existing_bubble`.
- **Keys and Pull.** `⌘B` and `⇧⌘B` keep U165's forward and reverse start-order cycle with the pane after the last Terminal: reaching a Terminal restores and focuses its Bubble while minimising the previous one; reaching the pane minimises the shown Bubble and focuses the pane. With no tabs they do nothing. `⌘.` toggles the focused Bubble or Terminal tab; `⇧⌘⌫` closes either by the existing one-call path and retains U165's focus-return rule. Other emulator keys and Help remain U165's. U421's Pull selects its existing last-focused/latest-started target, or opens one if needed, restores only that Bubble and minimises the previously shown one, leaving all tabs present. Asserted by `u469_cmd_b_cycles_one_bubble_then_the_pane_in_both_directions`, `u469_bubble_chords_work_from_tabs_without_reaching_the_terminal`, and `u469_pull_restores_only_its_target_and_keeps_all_tabs`.
- **Keyboard access.** Each visibility control, each separate `×`, and `+` is a native button reachable by Tab and activatable by Enter or Space, with no focus ring (U3528, policy). Visibility buttons use the Terminal name as their accessible name; close buttons use `Close <Terminal name>`; duplicate Terminal names still act on their own id. No button contains another button. The mark has an accessible running or exit description, and its meaning does not depend on colour. Asserted by `u469_every_tab_close_and_plus_is_named_and_keyboard_reachable` and `u469_duplicate_names_close_only_the_matching_terminal`.
- **Dense layout and resources.** At 1512×950 in light and dark for the named behavioral tests and dark only for captures and critic Check readings (U3725), sixteen eligible Terminals (eight Workstream shells and eight selected-Agent Runs, X11's per-owner cap), a 60-character name, and the selected Agent's densest resource values (memory `999.9 GB`, CPU `1600%`, disk `12.3 TB`) plus `Not updating`, with no Project values show the failure text and pass the Checks. A shortened visible name retains its full accessible name and hover title. Resource values keep U444's icons, names, spacing, sentence case and quiet status and U408's polling and lifetime behavior. Names are asserted by `u469_every_tab_close_and_plus_is_named_and_keyboard_reachable`; the design critic judges the Checks (AGENTS.md rule 10).

**U534 the tab region stays inside the centre pane and clear of the Resource values.** Found by the break-ui report #2225: an Agent with 8 Runs on the `bubbles` seed at 1280 by 800 drew its tabs to x=1120, so tab 3 was cut by the `413 MB` / `18%` values at x=817 and tabs 4 to 8 sat under them and the Shelf (from x=960), out of the pointer's reach; the strip's own box was 67 px wide with overflow visible. Narrows U469's Dense layout clause, which holds its other words. Given the selected Agent with 8 running Runs and a Project whose Shelf is open, at 1280 by 800 and at 700 by 800, in light and dark for non-capture observers (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), then:

- **Inside.** The tab region's box (`.terminal-tabs-host`) lies inside the centre pane and ends before the left edge of the Resource values and before the Shelf's left edge; no tab's box intersects a Resource value's box, and no tab is drawn outside the region's box. Overflow is the region's scroll, never visible overflow. Asserted by `u534_the_tab_region_ends_before_the_resource_values_and_the_shelf`.
- **Reachable.** Each of the 8 tabs and each `×` can be reached: scrolling the region by pointer wheel or keyboard focus brings it fully inside the region, and the point at its centre then hits that control (`document.elementFromPoint`), not a Resource value, the Shelf or the pane. Asserted by `u534_every_tab_is_reachable_by_pointer_after_scrolling`.
- **Readable.** Every tab shows its name in full up to U469's shortening, whose full accessible name and hover title stay, and the region gives a visible cue that more tabs lie beyond an edge while it can scroll that way. Asserted by `u534_a_scrollable_region_shows_which_edge_has_more_tabs`.
- **Still.** The bar's height and the pane and composer rectangles equal those with one tab. Asserted by `u534_eight_runs_keep_the_bar_height`.

The cue (an edge fade or a scroll button) uses existing Tokens and, if a button, the shared button kinds and a name (D6). Moves: D2, D5, D6, D10; no Check passing on main regresses. **U3661 a failed Terminal close keeps its Retry until that close is replaced.** Narrows U469's Close and P3557c. Given Terminal 2's close failed and its failure line shows Retry, when the user starts a close of Terminal 1 (confirmed, cancelled or failing with its own message), then Terminal 2's failure line stays only while it is the held failure and its Retry stays with it, closing Terminal 2 once through the same Close path; a later failure for Terminal 1 replaces the held failure and its Retry, and a close of Terminal 2 itself, a successful close of Terminal 2 or dismissing the line clears it. Closing or cancelling another Terminal clears neither the line nor its Retry. Asserted by `u3661_closing_another_terminal_keeps_the_failed_close_retry`, `u3661_cancelling_another_close_keeps_the_failed_close_retry`, and `u3661_a_later_failure_replaces_the_held_retry` in `apps/desktop/src/bubbles/`, Vitest with the fake App. Policy: journey stage Intervene; no layout change, so no capture; Moves: none.
