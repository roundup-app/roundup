## Public mappings and boundaries

Shelf presentation/selection itself is webview behavior. `leader.shelf`, `shelf.maximise`, `shelf.close`, `edit.undo` and `edit.redo` are named actions, reachable through the existing `actions`/`do` surface subject to captured focus and availability; they are not new RPCs or MCP tools. [keyboard.md](keyboard.md) enumerates the other row shortcuts. `todo.delete` receives a frozen set of ids plus operation; `todo.deletion`, `todo.restore` and `todo.redo` operate on retained receipts. Pad deletion uses `pad.delete`, `pad.getDeletion`, `pad.restore` and `pad.redoDelete`, preserving incarnation/operation identity. CLI kebab commands and Agent/Door tools are mapped in [endpoints.md](endpoints.md); those actual role offers belong to its older pinned revision, not a fresh tools/list claim here. Failed deletion reads `todo.list` or `pad.list` before presenting surviving failures. Clearing selection, dismissing a diagnostic and folding groups mutate no stored content.

Todo Auto/assignment, triage/nesting, completion, add, row rename/open, last-change age and content metadata belong to the separate Todo/Pad inventory, even where mounted through ScopeAction/OwnerGroups. Pad export E and context Open/Rename/Delete are enumerated in keyboard.md and owning component sources; export/content editing still need their independent decision rows. ScrollShadow and scope-label composition are covered here; decorative styling is not invented as another executable feature.
